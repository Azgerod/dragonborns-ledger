# Source Note: Sideways Trophy Caveats

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000020

## Claim

UESP identifies `Sideways` as a Bronze/20-point achievement for completing 10 side quests, but records unusual counting cases: `Blood on the Ice` and Black Book side quests do not count, `The Affairs of Hagravens` counts despite not being listed on the Side Quests page, `Delayed Burial` counts despite Dark Brotherhood categorization, and `A Scroll For Anska` plus `The Heart of Dibella` have ending-specific exceptions.

## Routing Relevance

The PS4 route must preserve trophies and should avoid assuming that every named side quest increments `Sideways`. This note supports trophy relevance fields for base-game side quest rows and a parent `Sideways` tracking row.

## Evidence Summary

UESP states that completing any 10 qualifying side quests unlocks `Sideways`. It also warns that some quests shown as side quests in the journal do not count and that some quests with unusual categorization do count. For this base-game side quest pass, the important immediate caveats are that `Blood on the Ice` should not be counted for `Sideways`, `Delayed Burial` should be counted despite Dark Brotherhood categorization, and the qualifying endings for `A Scroll For Anska` and `The Heart of Dibella` require individual-page validation before routing.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the broad trophy requirement and the named caveats. Exact PS4 Anniversary Edition trophy timing, ending-specific count behavior, and safe choices for the two exception quests require later trophy and conflict validation.
