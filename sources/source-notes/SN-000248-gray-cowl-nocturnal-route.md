# Gray Cowl of Nocturnal Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000248

## Claim

`The Gray Cowl of Nocturnal` should be completed as a late post-all-perks route before the final homes/services spend.

For this route:

- Leave Silverdrift Lair unentered during the earlier Pale/Nightgate sweep so the Gray Cowl quest chest, key corpse, document, sword, and Disarm word wall can be handled in one clean pass.
- Start the quest in the Riften graveyard by killing the thief and reading `Thief's Note`; if a guard kills him offscreen, locate and loot the body in the graveyard.
- Use the Riften beggar code phrase to get `Stranger's Note`, then open Nivenor's strongbox in Bolli's House after convincing Nivenor to hand over the key.
- Read/take `Nivenor's Journal`, `Stranger's Instructions`, `Forged Deed`, and the Antique Nordic Doll from the strongbox.
- Create a compact hard-save branch before the Gisli/deed handling: first pickpocket Gisli, read/take the pickpocket variant of `Stranger's Final Instructions`, reload, then continue the main route through Erikur's House, `Letter to Gisli`, the real deed, the forged-deed placement, and the alternate variant of `Stranger's Final Instructions`.
- Clear Silverdrift Lair during the same quest pass, take the Silverdrift Chest Key from the corpse with the lantern and two Loot shadowmarks, open the nearby chest, take the Ancestral Sword of Clan Ice-Blade and `The Sword of Clan Ice-Blade`, then continue to the Disarm word wall.
- Return to the Riften graveyard, read `Stranger's Journal`, hand over the deed and sword, receive the Gray Cowl of Nocturnal, and preserve the cowl plus remaining documents and misc/key items in owned storage.
- Do not equip the Gray Cowl around guards or during later brawls.

## Routing Relevance

This closes `OBJ-000526`, `OBJ-000654`, `CHK-QUESTS-0660`, `OBJ-000748`, `OBJ-001440`, `OBJ-001470`, `OBJ-001511`, `OBJ-001512`, `OBJ-001513`, `OBJ-001514`, `OBJ-001515`, `OBJ-001530`, `OBJ-001531`, `CHK-BOOKS-2506`, `CHK-BOOKS-2530`, `OBJ-002149`, `CHK-LOCATIONS-1224`, `CHK-DRAGON-SHOUTS-0905`, and item rows `ITEM-001099` through `ITEM-001105`.

The route is deliberately late because it requires theft/pickpocket/Speech handling, a branch-only document variant, a Nordic ruin with draugr and a word-wall bug note, and a guard-hostile reward. Placing it immediately after the all-perks audit guarantees Speech, Pickpocket, Lockpicking, Sneak, and combat skills are restored before the quest, while still keeping the Gray Cowl reward from becoming early overpowered gear.

The route creates one branch save for the pickpocket-only final-instructions variant. The main route uses the Erikur's House forged-deed path because it routes `Letter to Gisli`, `Forged Deed`, `Land Title and Deed`, and the alternate instructions variant without needing to keep the pickpocket branch in final continuity.

## Evidence Summary

The quest page identifies the Anniversary Edition route start as the Riften graveyard thief rather than the original auto-start behavior. Its walkthrough and stages send the player through the thief note, a Riften beggar, Bolli's House strongbox, the Solitude deed job, Silverdrift Lair, and the Riften graveyard reward handoff. It also records that a guard may kill the thief offscreen, that Nivenor can be convinced for the strongbox key, that the Solitude deed can be handled through Erikur's House, Gisli, purchase/persuade, or pickpocketing, and that pickpocket/lockpicking choices can fail optional Speech objectives without blocking completion.

The Creation page confirms the official start as confronting the thief in the Riften graveyard and lists the quest, documents, keys, and item members in The Gray Cowl Returns. It also records that the Antique Nordic Doll is marked as a quest item until the quest is completed, and that there are two variants of `Stranger's Final Instructions` depending on how the Land Title and Deed is acquired from Gisli. The guide therefore routes a small branch to read/take the pickpocket variant, reloads, and keeps the Erikur's House forged-deed path for main continuity and the alternate variant.

The individual document pages identify the routed pickup sources: `Thief's Note` on the thief, `Stranger's Note` from a beggar, `Nivenor's Journal` and `Stranger's Instructions` in Bolli's House strongbox, `Letter to Gisli` in Erikur's House, both final-instructions variants in the barrel behind The Winking Skeever, `The Sword of Clan Ice-Blade` in the Silverdrift chest, and `Stranger's Journal` in the Riften graveyard.

The items page and Gray Cowl page identify the Gray Cowl as a light helmet with Sneak, carry-weight, Detect Life, warmth, and guard-hostility behavior. The item page confirms `Nivenor's Strongbox Key`, `Silverdrift Chest Key`, `Forged Deed`, `Land Title and Deed`, Antique Nordic Doll, and Ancestral Sword membership. The Ancestral Sword page confirms the sword is unique, found in Silverdrift, deals no damage, and is relevant only to this quest. The guide preserves the cowl and any remaining misc/key/document items after completion, but treats the deed and sword as quest handoff items.

The Silverdrift page confirms Silverdrift Lair is a clearable Nordic ruin in the Pale, has a Disarm word wall, and contains the Ancestral Sword chest when the Creation is installed. It records that the two Loot shadowmarks appear only with The Gray Cowl Returns installed. It also records several bug risks: a stuck spot behind a brazier, a barred-door one-way issue, a word wall that might not teach its word, and a shortcut-exit crevice trap. The guide therefore avoids clearing Silverdrift during the earlier Pale pass, uses the quest source to find the key corpse before opening the chest, confirms the Disarm word at the wall, and leaves the dungeon only after the chest/sword/document/word-wall work is complete.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the quest route, document sources, reward boundary, Silverdrift key/chest handling, branch variant requirement, and Gray Cowl preservation warning.

The source pages do not require a high level. The guide still routes the quest after the all-perks audit because the branch-only Pickpocket document, Speech key option, Silverdrift combat, and Gray Cowl guard-hostility warning are safer and less disruptive when all thief/social/combat skills are restored.

`Disarm` as a full three-word shout remains a separate route-resolution row until all three word locations are closed together. This pass only closes the Silverdrift Lair `Haal` checklist word.
