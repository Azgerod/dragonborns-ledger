# SN-000153 - Solitude Coast, Dragon Bridge, Wild Horse, And Wolfskull Route

Status: route-writing source note for TB-035-MR-025.

## Scope

This note supports the v1 guide section `Solitude Coast, Dragon Bridge, Wild Horse, And Wolfskull`. The section routes the Solitude stable/farm/sawmill work rows, `Horse Whisperer`, Dapple Brown Wild Horse, Solitude Lighthouse and Treasure Map III cache, The Steed Stone, Dragon Bridge town work, `Dragon's Breath Mead`, `Over the Edge`, Steel Soldier armor/documents, Wolfskull Cave clear-state protection, `The Man Who Cried Wolf`, `Necromancer's Ritual`, `Elisif's Tribute`, and `Lorcalin's Orders`.

It deliberately does not route `The Wolf Queen Awakened`, Shield of Solitude, Bone Wolf, Proudspire purchase, Thane of Haafingar, Captain Aldis's `The Mirror` favor, `Lights Out!`, Broken Oar Grotto, Ahtar, Noster, Sybille, Treasure Map VIII, or `The Dainty Sload` yet. Those rows are mapped in the internal coverage file with their route gates.

## Route Decisions

Katla and Hjorunn are routed now because the section starts at Solitude's farm/stable yard and then moves through Solitude Sawmill. `Gather Wheat` lists Katla as the Outside of Solitude crop buyer, and `Chop Wood` lists Hjorunn as the Solitude Sawmill firewood buyer. These are safe colocated checklist rows, so they are included as direct player actions instead of being left for a cleanup pass. The guide warns not to use the apparently free stable horses after Katla's crop sale because Katla's Farm and Solitude Stables source pages describe the ownership side effect as not granting a proper owned mount.

`Horse Whisperer` is routed at Solitude Stables because the hostler map purchase completes the quest, gives both the Map of Wild Horses and `Wild Horse Notes`, and the Dapple Brown Wild Horse is directly south of Solitude Sawmill. The guide buys the map package first, then routes the Dapple Brown horse during the same exterior pass. Saddle and naming services remain for the later mount-service reconciliation because the current route only needs the finite mount row and the player may not keep Dapple Brown as the active travel horse after the section.

Solitude Lighthouse is routed as an exterior marker plus Treasure Map III cache only. The map itself was acquired at Riverside Shack in MR-024, and UESP places its chest northeast of the lighthouse under the cliff edge. The lighthouse interior contains a `The Lunar Lorkhan` skill-book candidate, but the guide does not enter the interior or read skill books before the Scholar's Insight window.

The Steed Stone is routed for discovery only. UESP records that activating the stone replaces the current standing-stone effect, so the guide keeps the player's existing stone active. This follows the route's normal standing-stone policy: discover markers when the path naturally reaches them, but activate only when the route explicitly wants the power.

Dragon Bridge is inserted between The Steed Stone and Wolfskull because the Solitude coast road naturally passes the town. Azzada's crop sale and Horgeir's firewood sale are safe local activity variants, and Olda's `Dragon's Breath Mead` both starts and completes in the immediate Dragon Bridge area. `Over the Edge` is also routed here because all required notes, the dead guard evidence, Peladius, and the Steel Soldier armor reward are clustered in Dragon Bridge and have no route-level gate.

Treasure Map VIII is not routed during this Dragon Bridge visit. The cache is in the Dragon Bridge area, but the map source is far away southeast of Bleakcoast Cave, so the route keeps the cache with the actual map acquisition instead of asking the player to loot it blind. Clinton Lylvieve is recorded only as an option-list child candidate; the adoption/default-family route is not changed by this town pass.

Wolfskull Cave is deliberately pre-cleared before `The Man Who Cried Wolf`. UESP records that the quest changes Wolfskull Cave while active and makes the normal clear state impossible unless the player enters first and kills the bandit boss in the ice-cave section. The guide therefore clears the location in bandit state, returns to Solitude, accepts Falk's quest, and then completes the changed necromancer/ritual version immediately.

The Varnius court save is included because UESP records a Blue Palace court-scene bug if relevant courtiers leave before the audience begins. This section occurs before Bards College, Dark Brotherhood Solitude work, Civil War hostile states, and other named Solitude-disruptive states, so it is the correct stabilizing point.

`Necromancer's Ritual` is learned during the quest visit because Wolfskull Cave lists the Necromantic Grimoire spell tome on a pedestal next to the Ritual Master. `The Refugees` is left closed because the guide reaches that exact altar before the Scholar's Insight reading window.

`The Wolf Queen Awakened` is not routed now. UESP records it begins through `Letter from Falk Firebeard` after leveling up following `The Man Who Cried Wolf`, and it rewards the Shield of Solitude. The project leveled-reward constraint requires the Shield of Solitude handoff at level 40+, so the guide tells the player to keep the courier follow-up for the later shield-gate section. The Bone Wolf letter and `Let Sleeping Wolves Lie` are also held because UESP records that Bone Wolf's courier letter follows completion of `The Wolf Queen Awakened`.

`Elisif's Tribute` is routed immediately after `The Man Who Cried Wolf` because it is unlocked by that quest, gives Proudspire purchase permission, advances Haafingar thane prerequisites, and is safe to complete now. The guide uses a shrine-approach save because UESP records that random encounters can interfere with the Thalmor spawn; the save protects `Lorcalin's Orders` as bug recovery rather than as RNG manipulation.

Proudspire Manor is not purchased now. `Elisif's Tribute` unlocks the steward sale, but the project convention forbids assuming early house money unless the route creates it. Proudspire, the Proudspire Stone of Barenziah, Thane of Haafingar, and Jordis remain with the later property/economy route.

Captain Aldis's `Rare Gifts` variant remains unstarted. The required item is `The Mirror`, a Block skill book, and the Aldis request can spawn or point to copies that interact poorly with the route's Scholar's Insight timing and theft policy. UESP also records the Aldis favor failure risk after Battle for Solitude, so the coverage tracker stages it before hostile Solitude branch state rather than dropping it.

`Lights Out!`, Broken Oar Grotto, Ahtar, Noster, and Sybille are held for a target-aware Solitude coast/radiant bundle. Broken Oar Grotto is a nonrespawning Amulet of the Moon risk in the project's constraint table, UESP warns that killing Captain Hargar before Ahtar's quest can make Ahtar's objective inaccessible, Noster's target is randomized among Broken Oar, Shadowgreen, and Steepfall, and Sybille's vampire target is randomized. The guide therefore does not ask the player to enter Broken Oar or start those radiants during this fixed Solitude prerequisite pass.

`The Dainty Sload` is held for late Thieves Guild restoration. UESP records it as Erikur's Solitude special job and the project constraints require Erikur to remain available before Dark Brotherhood `Bound Until Death`. The Dainty Sload location, Balmora Blue Note, Dainty Sload Stone of Barenziah, and `A Dance in Fire, v6` remain bundled with that Thieves special job.
