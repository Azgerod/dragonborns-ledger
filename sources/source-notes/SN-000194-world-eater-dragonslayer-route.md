# SN-000194 - World-Eater's Eyrie and Dragonslayer Route

## Scope

Supports the v1 guide section `World-Eater's Eyrie and Dragonslayer`.

This pass routes the one-way Skuldafn expedition, Sovngarde, `Dragonslayer`, final main-quest completion, Skuldafn's unique/missable rewards, the quest-locked Storm Call word, and main-quest finale recovery.

## Routing Decisions

The guide makes `HS-MQ-SKULDAFN` before releasing Odahviing because Skuldafn cannot be revisited after entering Sovngarde. This is not a branch save; it protects the one-way main-quest finale, the Nahkriin mask, Diamond Claw, Storm Call: Qo, and the Skuldafn clearable-location row.

The route clears both Skuldafn side towers before entering the main temple because UESP identifies Skuldafn as a four-zone worldspace and the towers are reachable only during this one visit. This is an intentional true-completion insertion, not a same-theme cleanup deferral.

The guide explicitly routes all three Skuldafn puzzles because this is a one-way location and the final guide should be executable without opening a wiki: first-puzzle chest gate Snake/Eagle/Whale, first-puzzle progress gate Snake/Snake/Whale, drawbridge puzzle Whale/Snake/Eagle, and Diamond Claw door Fox/Moth/Dragon.

The guide tells the player to learn Storm Call: Qo before leaving for Sovngarde because UESP identifies it as the only Storm Call word source that is locked to a one-time visit. Storm Call itself remains internally partial until the later Forelhost/Siege on the Dragon Cult route handles Strun.

The portal-side dragons are not forced as a separate objective. The guide routes clearing the exterior and portal approach, and tells the player to update Dragon Hunter only for absorbed souls that actually occur. This follows the existing counter policy: count absorbed souls, not dragon kills, and avoid inventing a fixed soul total where dragon behavior can vary.

The route uses the direct `Dragonslayer` and `Call of Valor` pages for Call of Valor coverage. Tsun teaches all three words after Alduin is defeated, so no dragon souls are reserved for Call of Valor.

The final epilogue preserves the canonical Paarthurnax-alive state. The guide permits speaking to Paarthurnax and Odahviing after the return to the Throat of the World but does not route Sky Haven Temple or Greybeard follow-up dialogue as a new objective because those pages did not identify a checklist-relevant completion row for this pass.

The inherited Vampire Lord fallback remains only as a conditional continuity bridge. If a real playthrough still needs Vampire Lord perk progress after MR-059A, Skuldafn and Sovngarde are the next fresh hostile content; as soon as the eleventh perk is purchasable, the guide returns to the Falion/Serana cure closeout before high-risk AE content.

## Deferred Or Unresolved

No new `NEEDS ROUTE RESOLUTION` rows were introduced by this pass.

Inherited unresolved rows still remain for `Filial Bonds`/Torkild, `Torkild's Letter to Wulf`, `Note from Mogrul`, Potion of Blood, Ironwood Soup Elixir, and Mysterious Potion.

Storm Call remains internally in progress after this pass if Forelhost/Strun has not yet been routed on the current guide draft. The Skuldafn word itself is complete here.
