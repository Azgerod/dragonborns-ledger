# Source Note: Daedric Artifact Outcome Hard Saves

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000098

## Claim

Several Daedric quests require an artifact-awarding outcome for Oblivion Walker progress, while their alternate outcomes preserve different NPCs, followers, or rewards. The route needs hard saves at the final choice for the major alternate outcomes and must default to the artifact-safe choice on the canonical continuity unless the trophy pass proves a safer artifact-maximizing exception.

## Routing Relevance

These rows are route-critical because the specification preserves PS4 trophies and unique items. The table should distinguish the canonical artifact-safe outcome from alternate branch or option-list handling, without drafting the branch route prose yet.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's Daedric quest overview says Oblivion Walker requires artifact-awarding endings, not merely completing Daedric quests. It specifically flags `A Daedra's Best Friend`, `Pieces of the Past`, and `Waking Nightmare` as quests that can complete without a qualifying artifact, and notes that `Ill Met By Moonlight` can award both Savior's Hide and the Ring of Hircine in a way that counts both artifacts.

The individual pages confirm the hard-save forks. `A Daedra's Best Friend` awards the Masque of Clavicus Vile for returning the axe and sparing Barbas; killing Barbas gives the Rueful Axe, which UESP marks as non-qualifying. `Waking Nightmare` awards the Skull of Corruption only if Erandur is killed before destroying it; sparing him gives Erandur as a follower. `Pieces of the Past` awards Mehrunes' Razor only if Silus dies and the Razor is reforged; sparing Silus leaves the Razor out of reach. `The Taste of Death` awards the Ring of Namira only for completing the cannibal outcome with Verulus. `The Black Star` has a final choice between Azura's Star and the Black Star.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the outcome forks. TB-015 must still validate PS4 counter behavior for all artifact outcomes, especially whether the route should intentionally use the UESP-listed dual Hircine outcome or keep it as an alternate branch.
