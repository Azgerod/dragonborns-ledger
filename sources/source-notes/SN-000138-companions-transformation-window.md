# Companions Transformation Window

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000138

## Claim

After `Proving Honor`, the route should complete one more clean Companions radiant, then route `The Silver Hand`, Beast Form/Blood Oath, Gallows Rock, and exactly two Aela revenge radiants before starting `Blood's Honor`. `Blood's Honor` should remain unstarted at the end of this pass so its Falkreath/Glenmoril target can be bundled with the next geographic route block instead of interrupting the short post-Silver Hand radiant window.

## Routing Relevance

The post-`The Silver Hand` radiants are a narrow window. The Companions source and radiant-boundary table agree that two Aela jobs must be completed before the later questline can progress, and that only two of the three Aela revenge-radiant titles can normally be completed on one save. The player-facing guide therefore records the actual two titles and targets, marks the unoffered title as one-save-limited, and does not ask the player to reroll ordinary radiant assignments for a preferred pair.

The same pass also acquires Beast Form and checks Blood Oath. Because lycanthropy is needed for Werewolf Mastered, Totems of Hircine, Frostmoon Crag ring access, and later transformation planning, the guide preserves Beast Blood and explicitly delays cure or Vampire Lord conversion until later transformation work.

## Evidence Summary

The Companions quest order requires one radiant after `Proving Honor`, then `The Silver Hand`, then two more radiants before `Blood's Honor`. The Companions source further notes that the first post-Silver Hand radiant comes from Aela and that only two of the three Silver Hand radiant titles are normally possible on one save. The guide therefore closes the Aela window before allowing `Blood's Honor` to begin.

`The Silver Hand` routes the Underforge ritual, grants Beast Form, moves the player through the forced Whiterun werewolf sequence, then wakes the player with Aela and sends both to Gallows Rock. UESP says Blood Oath unlocks once the player speaks with Aela after surviving the initiation. The guide places Beast Form at the fountain and Blood Oath after waking and speaking with Aela.

Gallows Rock is source-listed as a discoverable and clearable military fort tied to `The Silver Hand`. The place page confirms the two colocated skill books: `The Importance of Where` and `Last Scabbard of Akrash`. The progression-source selection table chooses later/default copies for both titles, so the Gallows copies are left closed during this pass. The place page also notes Treasure Map VII's chest near Gallows Rock, but the map source is Traitor's Post; the route keeps that map/chest pair together later rather than looting a chest without its map context.

The Aela revenge-radiant pages cover the three possible objectives: kill a Silver Hand leader, steal the Silver Hand Stratagem, or retrieve a Fragment of Wuuthrad. All use radiant targets and have bug risks around bad markers, inaccessible quest items, or stuck journal state after turn-in. The guide uses a hard save before taking Aela jobs for bug recovery only, while explicitly avoiding ordinary RNG manipulation for title preference.

Lycanthropy grants Beast Form and Beast Blood, blocks Rested/Well Rested bonuses, and can later be cured only under controlled conditions. The same source explains that the Dawnguard werewolf perk tree requires feeding and that Werewolf Mastered requires all 11 werewolf perks. The route starts the transformation state now but leaves werewolf perk grinding, Totems of Hircine, the player's cure, and Vampire Lord conversion to later transformation sections.

`Blood's Honor` becomes available after the two Aela radiants and sends the player to Glenmoril Coven in Falkreath Hold. Starting it also changes Companion job availability and begins the late main-chain sequence. The guide therefore ends this pass before talking to Kodlak so the next route section can bundle the Falkreath/Glenmoril work coherently.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the quest order, Beast Form/Blood Oath placement, Gallows Rock route, two Aela revenge-radiant boundary, and lycanthropy preservation rule.

The unresolved live-save variable is the exact post-`Proving Honor` Companion job and the exact two Aela radiant targets. The guide resolves that with conditional instructions, target recording, and bug-recovery saves rather than deterministic target claims.
