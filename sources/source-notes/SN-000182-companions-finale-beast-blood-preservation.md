# SN-000182 - Companions Finale And Beast-Blood Preservation

Status: active source note for TB-035-MR-049.

## Scope

This note supports the v1 guide section `Companions Finale, Totems, And Beast-Blood Preservation`. The section finishes the Companions main questline, completes the finite post-quest totem and cure objectives that require werewolf continuity, preserves Beast Blood for the upcoming Solstheim Frostmoon Crag ring purchase, and defers Serana's Vampire Lord conversion and final mortal-state cure until that access is no longer needed.

## Route Decisions

The old scaffold title implied that Werewolf Mastered, Vampire Mastered, and the final mortal-state cure should all happen in one pre-Solstheim transformation section. Source review showed that this would break Frostmoon Crag. Frostmoon access and Majni's four unique werewolf rings depend on retaining lycanthropy, and the Frostmoon page warns that losing lycanthropy after first speaking with Majni can block his ring service. The v1 route therefore keeps Beast Blood through the Solstheim Frostmoon pass and moves Serana's Vampire Lord conversion, Vampire Mastered, Falion's cure, and final mortal-state restoration to a later post-Frostmoon section.

`Purity of Revenge` is routed now because `Blood's Honor` was already complete and Driftshade Refuge had not been cleared earlier. The guide sends the player with Vilkas, retrieves the Fragments of Wuuthrad, clears Driftshade, and avoids Beast Form while Vilkas is present.

The Driftshade book audit found two selected skill-book copies (`2920, Morning Star, v1` and `Warrior`) plus a non-selected `The Rear Guard` copy. Because the route has not reached the Scholar's Insight reading window, the guide names those books only as closed objects to avoid accidental early reads. The selected-source table remains unchanged.

`Glory of the Dead` is routed immediately after `Purity of Revenge`. The guide captures Wuuthrad, Ysgramor's Tomb, Shield of Ysgramor, the Ysgramor's Tomb Animal Allegiance word, and the Harbinger state in one quest chain. The Jorrvaskr Stone of Barenziah is taken only after `Glory of the Dead` completes and the room becomes the Harbinger's room, matching the row's `Glory of the Dead` prerequisite and avoiding a theft/ownership ambiguity.

The Ysgramor's Tomb book audit found `The Legendary Sancre Tor`, but the selected copy remains the Broken Tower Redoubt exterior copy, so this section leaves the tomb copy closed.

All three `Totems of Hircine` assignments are routed now because they require the player to remain a werewolf and are a finite Companions post-quest chain. The player-facing guide follows the random-assignment convention: record the actual assigned target, go there directly, handle only that target site, return to the Underforge, place and pray at the totem, then take the next assignment.

The werewolf perk tree is not falsely marked complete in this section. The guide starts a durable feeding policy using the Ring of Hircine and explicitly preserves Beast Blood until all eleven werewolf perks are purchased. The coverage tracker keeps the individual werewolf perk rows staged until the later post-Frostmoon transformation closeout confirms completion before any cure or Vampire Lord replacement.

Farkas and Vilkas `Purity` are routed after the totems. They consume two Glenmoril Witch Heads and remove the Companions' own beast blood without curing the player. The player keeps Beast Blood for Frostmoon.

## Deferrals

`Dragon Seekers` remains staged for a later isolated dragon-lair radiant block. Its target is random and can include a Solstheim dragon lair, so completing it here would violate the random-assignment convention and could create an awkward pre-Solstheim divergence.

Serana's Vampire Lord service remains preserved but unused. Converting before Frostmoon would replace lycanthropy and risk the Frostmoon ring purchases. Serana also remains uncured because curing her would permanently remove her ability to provide Vampire Lord service.

Falion's `Rising at Dawn` cure is recorded as the later final-state cure path after Vampire Mastered. It is not routed in this section because the route has not yet used Vampire Lord on canonical continuity.

No new unresolved `NEEDS ROUTE RESOLUTION` rows were introduced by this pass.
