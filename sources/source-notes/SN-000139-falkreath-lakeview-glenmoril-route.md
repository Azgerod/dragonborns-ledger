# Falkreath Lakeview And Glenmoril Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000139

## Claim

After the Companions transformation window, the guide should route the funded Falkreath land pass through Siddgeir's bandit-leader assignment when the assigned target is safe to clear now, complete three Falkreath help actions, buy Lakeview Manor when the target path allows it, build the Lakeview foundation, and pre-clear Glenmoril Coven before starting `Blood's Honor` once the character is level 14 or higher. If Siddgeir's target is Cracked Tusk Keep, the bandit job and dependent Lakeview/thane work should remain parked until the later Daedric-route visit that can handle that location safely; TB-044 now adds that delayed closeout after the Mehrunes/Cracked Tusk route. TB-044 later superseded the original Knifepoint hold: `SN-000219` treats a Knifepoint assignment as a bug-conflict result that must reload `HS-FALKREATH-LAND-JOB`, because the same source cluster shows no safe path that both preserves `Boethiah's Calling` and completes Siddgeir's Knifepoint bounty.

## Routing Relevance

Siddgeir's Falkreath land path depends on his `Rare Gifts` favor and `Kill the Bandit Leader` quest. The earlier post-dragon Falkreath stop gives Siddgeir the Black-Briar Mead before the Hearthfire level-9 courier letter can fail or replace the mead favor, then leaves the bandit job until the guide has the 5,000 gold needed for Lakeview Manor. `Kill the Bandit Leader` has a fixed target pool for Siddgeir, but the target may have been chosen the first time a Falkreath location was entered, so the guide records the actual target and follows a conditional route rather than pretending one deterministic camp exists.

Bannermist Tower and Bilegulch Mine can be handled as ordinary Siddgeir target clears in this pass. If Embershard Mine is the target, the guide first starts `Smith 'N Slash` from Whiterun Guard Barracks so the Orcish Plate quest, Yakhtu's Journal, Orcish Plate armor, and Embershard clear happen together instead of pre-clearing the cave. Cracked Tusk Keep is held because it intersects both Mehrunes' Razor routing and Kharjo's Moon Amulet nonrespawn risk. The original Knifepoint hold is no longer accepted after TB-044; `SN-000219` reclassifies it as reload-only bug recovery.

Falkreath thaneship uses three help actions that are compact, source-backed, and already in the Falkreath/Lake Ilinalta corridor: Thadgeir's Berit's Ashes delivery to Runil, a crop sale to Mathies, and a firewood sale to Hert at Half-Moon Mill. The generic thane-help source says citizens befriended before the thane objective starts count, so those three actions can be done even on a save where Siddgeir's target is parked and the formal thane objective is not yet available.

Lakeview Manor is routed only when Siddgeir's bandit job can be completed in this pass. The guide requires normal route money rather than grinding or exploits, buys the plot for 5,000 gold, checks Landowner, visits the property, selects Small House Layout, builds the foundation with one sawn log and ten quarried stone, and records Land Baron progress at one of three plots. Rayya is unlocked with Falkreath thaneship but not assigned as Lakeview steward until the later Main Hall/service pass.

Glenmoril Coven is bundled with the Companions handoff because the cave is in Falkreath Hold and `Blood's Honor` sends the player there. The place page lists Glenmoril Coven as minimum level 14, no respawn, clearable, and bug-prone if it is not cleared before `Blood's Honor`; the guide therefore pre-clears the normal hag version before speaking to Kodlak, leaves the colocated skill books closed, then returns during `Blood's Honor` to kill all five Glenmoril Witches and take all five heads.

## Evidence Summary

`Rare Gifts` and `Kill the Bandit Leader (Jarl)` support completing Siddgeir's Black-Briar Mead favor before Ysolda's Mammoth Tusk favor and before level-9 Hearthfire letter behavior can interfere. `Kill the Bandit Leader (Jarl)` lists Siddgeir's five possible targets and notes that the target location and boss are often chosen before Siddgeir gives the quest. The guide therefore records the target and branches by the actual assigned location without asking the player to manipulate the save.

The safe target branches are handled immediately. Bannermist Tower and Bilegulch Mine are clearable Falkreath locations with colocated skill-book copies; the player-facing guide clears the location but leaves those skill books closed for the Scholar's Insight plan. If the target is Embershard Mine, the `Smith 'N Slash` source requires the Whiterun Guard Barracks dossier and sends the player to Yakhtu in Embershard, so the guide starts that AE quest before clearing the mine.

Cracked Tusk Keep is not a safe early clear. It overlaps Mehrunes' Razor piece routing and is called out on the Kharjo Amulet page as a nonrespawning location that can break `Amulet of the Moon` if visited before that quest sends the player there. Knifepoint Ridge is also unsafe as an early clear, but TB-044 later found that it is not safe to park for Boethiah either: both the Knifepoint place page and `Boethiah's Calling` page identify bugs around clearing or entering Knifepoint before the Boethiah state, and the Knifepoint page also flags the Jarl bounty conflict once Boethiah's champion objective is active. The current guide parks only Cracked Tusk and reloads the pre-assignment save if Siddgeir assigns Knifepoint.

`Thane of Falkreath` requires helping three Falkreath citizens and rewards the title, Blade of Falkreath, and Rayya with Hearthfire installed. `Making Friends and Influencing People` says citizens helped before the thane objective starts still count. The guide uses Thadgeir/Runil, Mathies, and Hert because they are compact and avoid Dengeir/Sinding/Lod quest-state entanglements. Delivery confirms Thadgeir gives Berit's Ashes for Runil and that the delivery counts toward the hold's thane-help requirement. Berit's Ashes shares bone meal's alchemy properties and has respawning samples elsewhere, so delivering Thadgeir's sample is not a unique-item loss.

`Build Your Own Home` supports buying land for 5,000 gold after permission, then selecting Small House Layout and building a foundation with one sawn log and ten quarried stone. The same page records Landowner and Land Baron achievement handling and warns that Lakeview should be bought before Dark Brotherhood Helvard consequences. The guide buys and starts Lakeview only when Siddgeir's bandit job was completed in this pass; otherwise the property chain waits for the later Daedric-location return. Rayya is unlocked with thaneship, but her steward assignment waits because later Lakeview services require Main Hall and service-building validation.

Half-Moon Mill provides Hert as a firewood buyer and lumber contact, so it is a good third Falkreath-help stop and a useful Lake Ilinalta support marker. The same place contains Fishing Supplies and a `Physicalities of Werewolves` copy, but the guide does not route fishing or that regular/skill-book handling here because the current pass is land/thane/Glenmoril focused and the book policy still holds until the planned reading window. Sweet Roll is now routed here because the pet's clearing is immediately south-southwest of Half-Moon Mill and the thief's body provides both `Thief's Journal` and a deterministic sweet roll for taming.

Glenmoril Coven is level 14 minimum, no respawn, and source-listed as clearable only if handled before `Blood's Honor` replaces the normal hags with Glenmoril Witches. The guide therefore pre-clears it once in normal state at level 14+, leaves `A Tragedy in Black` and `Horror of Castle Xyr` closed, then starts `Blood's Honor`, returns, kills all five witches, and takes all five heads for later cure options.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the Siddgeir prerequisite sequencing, immediate target handling for Bannermist/Bilegulch/Embershard, the Cracked Tusk hold and later closeout, the Knifepoint reload correction from `SN-000219`, Falkreath help trio, Sweet Roll insertion, Lakeview purchase/foundation placement, Rayya staging, and Glenmoril pre-clear policy.

The remaining live-save variable is Siddgeir's actual target. If the save assigns Cracked Tusk Keep, the guide knowingly delays Falkreath thaneship and Lakeview Manor until the later Daedric-route section can complete that location safely, then reports Siddgeir's job, buys Lakeview, discovers the property, builds the foundation, and picks up The Woodsman's Friend. If the save assigns Knifepoint Ridge, `SN-000219` now requires reloading `HS-FALKREATH-LAND-JOB` and taking a clean assignment instead of carrying that conflict forward.
