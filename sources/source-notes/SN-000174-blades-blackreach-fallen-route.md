# SN-000174 - Blades, Blackreach, And The Fallen Route

Status: route-writing source note for TB-035-MR-043.

## Scope

This note supports the v1 guide section `Blades Research, Blackreach, And The Fallen`. The section finishes the Blades recruitment and Esbern research window before the Elder Scroll reading, climbs to Paarthurnax, completes the Elder Scroll / Blackreach / Oghma Infinium path, collects and turns in Crimson Nirnroot, then completes `Alduin's Bane` and `The Fallen` while bypassing `Season Unending` under completed Imperial Civil War continuity.

## Route Decisions

`Rebuilding the Blades`, one Blades `Dragon Hunting` radiant, and `Dragon Research` were moved out of the later Paarthurnax branch scaffold and into this main-continuity section. UESP states that Esbern will not finish `Rebuilding the Blades` or start `Dragon Hunting` while the optional Paarthurnax quest is active, and the existing project source note for Karthspire explicitly warned that Esbern's Potion should be resolved before the Elder Scroll/Paarthurnax support window closes. The guide therefore completes Blades recruitment and Esbern's Potion before the Time-Wound Elder Scroll reading, while the canonical route still preserves Paarthurnax.

The selected Blades recruits are Lydia, Uthgerd the Unbroken, and Calder. All three are already unlocked before this section and are not the planned default Goldenhills or Hearthfire stewards. This keeps the irreversible Blades commitment away from Faendal and the Hearthfire housecarl steward defaults while still using durable, route-supported followers.

The Blades dragon hunt is written as an isolated random assignment. The guide lists the source-listed dragon lair candidates and instructs the player to handle only the assigned lair, its dragon, and its local word wall before returning to Sky Haven. It does not route surrounding regional objectives around a possible target because the assignment is random and should not reshape the main itinerary.

The guide uses Paarthurnax, not Esbern, for the `The Fallen` main-route handoff after `Alduin's Bane`. This avoids starting the Paarthurnax-kill branch during main continuity. The Paarthurnax branch scaffold was updated so it no longer duplicates `Rebuilding the Blades`, `Dragon Hunting`, or `Dragon Research`; that branch will now only record the branch-exclusive kill/support outcome.

`Season Unending` is bypassed rather than routed. UESP states that the peace council is skipped after the final Civil War city battle or when one side controls at least eight holds, and the immediately preceding Imperial Civil War section completed `Battle for Windhelm`. This also preserves the route's War Hero handling by avoiding treaty handoffs.

The Blackreach pass routes same-location objectives aggressively because the player is already entering through Alftand for `Elder Knowledge`: `A Return To Your Roots`, more than thirty Crimson Nirnroot samples, Sinderion's Field Journal, Sinderion's Serendipity, Great Lift at Alftand, Great Lift at Mzinchaleft, Great Lift at Raldbthar, Tower of Mzark, Elder Scroll (Dragon), Runed Lexicon, Targe of the Blooded, Alftand, Drelas' Cottage, and the `Discerning the Transmundane` blood/Oghma path.

The Crimson Nirnroot route deliberately collects more than thirty samples. Avrusa takes thirty for `A Return To Your Roots`, and extra samples should remain available for the later alchemy-effect pass. The guide does not ask the player to consume Crimson Nirnroot here.

The Alftand blood route returns immediately after Septimus gives the Essence Extractor. UESP identifies Alftand as a four-race blood source after the main Alftand path, but notes that the player must return after receiving the extractor. Drelas' Cottage supplies the missing Dark Elf blood while also clearing a source-listed location.

The Oghma Infinium is acquired but not read. The project progression policy reserves Oghma for late skill/perk math, and the source constraints require a hard save before opening the cube/Infinium state and resolving the Septimus path before a long delay. The guide places `HARD SAVE: HS-SEPTIMUS-OGHMA` before the blood turn-in and then preserves the book unused.

Skill-book sources in this route are audited but not read because Scholar's Insight is not active. The guide gives local warnings only where the player is being routed into the exact object room: `The Locked Room` in Alftand Animonculory and `The Armorer's Challenge` in Tower of Mzark. `2920, MidYear, v6`, `A Dance in Fire, v6`, `Warrior`, and `De Rerum Dirennis` remain later skill-book-window candidates or alternates, not current reads.

The selected source for `Chimarvamidium` was changed from Alftand Glacial Ruins to Septimus Signus's Outpost. The Alftand source page identifies the Alftand copy as the non-skill-book version, so it cannot satisfy the Heavy Armor skill-book objective. The selected source for `The Armorer's Challenge` was changed from Bilegulch Mine to Tower of Mzark because the tower is a guaranteed main-route location and Bilegulch can be consumed by random target routing.

Alftand Ruined Tower is not routed here. It is a separate building from the main Alftand/Blackreach path; its `Spell Tome: Lesser Ward` candidate is already satisfied by the College spell-learning route, and the Dwarven Fishing Rod schematic work belongs to the Fishing quest state rather than the Elder Scroll pass.

`Letter from Septimus Signus` is not routed. The route starts and advances `Discerning the Transmundane` well above level 15, so the under-level-15 courier trigger is not part of this main route. This is an internal exclusion, not a player-facing action.

## Coverage Summary

This pass places or completes `Rebuilding the Blades`, one representative Blades `Dragon Hunting` radiant, `Dragon Research` / Dragon Infusion, `The Throat of the World`, `Clear Skies`, Fire Breath Shul from Paarthurnax, Paarthurnax meditation default, Alftand, Targe of the Blooded, Great Lift at Alftand, Great Lift at Mzinchaleft, Great Lift at Raldbthar, Tower of Mzark, `Elder Knowledge`, Elder Scroll (Dragon), Runed Lexicon, the remaining `Discerning the Transmundane` stages, Essence Extractor, Oghma Infinium acquisition, Blackreach Crimson Nirnroot collection, Sinderion's Field Journal, `A Return To Your Roots`, The Nirnroot Missive, Sinderion's Serendipity, Drelas' Cottage, `Alduin's Bane`, Dragonrend, `The Fallen`, Call Dragon, and the no-treaty `Season Unending` bypass.

Rows staged for later: Oghma Infinium use, Crimson Nirnroot alchemy effects, skill-book reads from Blackreach/Alftand/Tower of Mzark, and any random Blades dragon-lair word wall not assigned during this playthrough. Rows intentionally excluded from player-facing route: `Letter from Septimus Signus` under the route's level state and Alftand Ruined Tower Fishing-schematic work before the Fishing quest.

No new unresolved `NEEDS ROUTE RESOLUTION` rows were introduced by this pass.
