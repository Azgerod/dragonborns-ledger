# Source Note: Lunar Weapon Spawn Thresholds

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000093

## Claim

The six source-listed Lunar weapons are random leveled one-handed weapons at Silent Moons Camp. The iron Lunar weapons have their strongest individual tier at level 6+, the steel Lunar weapons have their strongest individual tier at level 8+, and UESP's Silent Moons Camp page says the best possible Lunar weapons spawn if the player is at least level 8.

## Routing Relevance

Silent Moons Camp is a location-level timing constraint, not just a pickup checklist. The route should not clear or repeatedly pass near the camp too early if the guide wants the best Lunar weapon candidates without relying on respawn cleanup.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's individual Lunar weapon pages list the top iron tiers at level 6+ and the top steel tiers at level 8+. The Silent Moons Camp page lists four random leveled Lunar weapon placements and notes that the best possible Lunar weapons spawn if the player is at least level 8.

The same location page also warns that the enchanted weapons may be missing if the player's level is too low, including if the player was in the area too early and keeps passing nearby, preventing respawn. It gives leaving the area and waiting 30 days as the reset workaround.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the level 8 route-safe collection rule. TB-013 should decide whether to express this as a strict first-entry warning for Silent Moons Camp or as a softer "avoid until 8, otherwise wait for respawn" warning. TB-030/checklist mapping must later decide whether the route needs one Lunar weapon, all six named Lunar weapon forms, or only the enchantment source under the final checklist.
