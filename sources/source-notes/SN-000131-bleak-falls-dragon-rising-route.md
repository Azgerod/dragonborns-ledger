# Bleak Falls Barrow And First Dragon Route Detail

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000131

## Claim

The v1 guide should start `The Golden Claw` during the first Riverwood visit, then route Bleak Falls Barrow with the claw quest already active. The Bleak Falls pass clears the dungeon, obtains the Dragonstone, returns the claw to Lucan before the Whiterun handoff, then completes `Bleak Falls Barrow` and `Dragon Rising` as a single early main-quest gate. This pass also starts Sideways tracking at one qualifying side quest and unlocks the first Unrelenting Force word, the first dragon soul trophy, dragon-soul tracking, Western Watchtower discovery, Whiterun thaneship, the Axe of Whiterun, and Lydia's housecarl availability. After `Dragon Rising`, `In My Time Of Need` becomes available from the Alik'r gate scene, but it should be staged for the next Whiterun block so the quest start, branch/default handling, and Swindler's Den trip stay together.

## Routing Relevance

This section is the first full dungeon-and-return pattern in `main-guide-v1.md`. It needs route-level support for Arvel/Golden Claw handling after the MR-002 Riverwood start, Sideways counter start, puzzle solutions, first word-wall state, Dragonstone handoff, first dragon fight, shout unlock, dragon-soul tracking, and the Whiterun relationship/reward rows that unlock when `Dragon Rising` is reported.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's `Bleak Falls Barrow` quest page places `The Golden Claw` as a concurrent quest and records the Dragonstone handoff to Farengar as the completion boundary for `Bleak Falls Barrow`. The same page and the Bleak Falls Barrow place page identify the first puzzle as Snake, Snake, Whale; the claw door as Bear, Moth, Owl; the word wall as the first Unrelenting Force word; and the guardian's Dragonstone drop. The place page also lists Bleak Falls Barrow as a clearable Nordic ruin and identifies the `Thief` skill-book copy just beyond the first puzzle.

UESP's `The Golden Claw` page records Lucan as the normal quest giver, notes that the quest can start from overheard bandits, but also warns of a completion bug if the player skips speaking to Lucan before obtaining the claw through the related quest. It also records Arvel as the Golden Claw carrier, the Golden Claw return to Lucan or Camilla as the completion boundary, and the Sideways qualification.

UESP's Bleak Falls Barrow place page states that Thomas's body and the associated note are not present at level 17 or below. The route therefore should not force the low-level opening pass to recover `Note to Thomas`; it can only collect it now if the level-18+ version of the cavern is active.

UESP's `Dragon Rising` page records the Western Watchtower investigation, Mirmulnir fight, first soul absorption, automatic unlock of the first Unrelenting Force word, Dragon Soul achievement, Whiterun thaneship, Axe of Whiterun, and Lydia as a follower/housecarl reward. It also notes that dragons begin appearing in Skyrim after `Dragon Rising`, and the existing Dragon source supports dragon souls as the tracked resource for shout unlocks and Dragon Hunter.

UESP's `In My Time Of Need` page lists `Dragon Rising` as the previous quest and says the Alik'r gate scene can occur after Irileth reports the Western Watchtower dragon. The guide therefore treats `In My Time Of Need` as unavailable during the first Whiterun entry and available after the first-dragon gate, while still holding the actual start for the next Whiterun block where branch/default handling and Swindler's Den travel can be routed coherently.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for MR-004 quest order, dungeon route details, first dragon-soul handling, reward placement, and `In My Time Of Need` availability staging. The final guide still needs later validation for long-term Golden Claw display/final-possession policy, full dragon-claw set reconciliation, exact Dragon Hunter counter-check cadence, and whether `Note to Thomas` should be collected on a later deliberate return if the opening pass is below level 18.
