## Route Decisions

`The Forgemaster's Fingers` is routed at Mor Khazgur before Dawnguard-side `Prophet` completes. The reason is not geographic theming: Dawnguard-side `Prophet` can grant Blood-Kin through Durak, and `The Forgemaster's Fingers` is a pre-Blood-Kin Orc stronghold quest. Routing it during Sorine's Mor Khazgur recruitment leg preserves the quest and also permits the Mor Khazgur ore-sale and Larak brawl rows to be completed immediately after Blood-Kin access.

Gunmar, Ancient Technology, Lost Relic, and Dawnguard side radiants are treated under the current random-assignment convention. The player follows the actual marker, does only the assigned job and directly colocated assignment item/document, returns to Fort Dawnguard, and does not build unrelated regional routing around the random target.

All six `Ancient Technology` assignments are routed before the Dawnguard worldspace/finale work to avoid late marker/availability risk and to unlock the finite Dawnguard technology set while Sorine is already installed at the Fort.

`Bolstering the Ranks` is started after Dexion is rescued but before the Elder Scroll reading. This installs Florentius early enough for the Fort Dawnguard service/radiant/Lost Relic loop without advancing into the Soul Cairn route first.

`Cleansing Light` excludes Movarth's Lair as the representative target because the source page identifies it as a problematic target for the boss-kill objective. This is a narrow source-backed exception to the normal no-reroll random-assignment rule.

## Deferrals

The Akaviri Sword is not acquired during the Dexion rescue. Dexion's source page says the sword requires Misdirection pickpocketing or Disarm, and the rescue sequence is not the clean supported theft window. The row is staged for a later pickpocket/disarm pass while Dexion remains accessible.

The Expanded Crossbow Pack and Elite Crossbows rows remain outside this section. The Fort Dawnguard technology unlocked by `Ancient Technology` is routed here, but the AE crossbow packs use separate Solitude/Ironback/crafting sources and should be handled in their own crossbow or crafting route window.

Florentius's merchant investment is not routed here because investment requires the Investor perk and belongs to the later merchant-investment validation pass.

Soul Cairn, Castle Volkihar revisit, Forgotten Vale, Auriel's Bow, and the remaining shared Dawnguard main quests are intentionally left to the next Dawnguard worldspace section. `Chasing Echoes`, `Seeking Disclosure`, and `Scroll Scouting` are started here only because they naturally begin when Dexion reads Elder Scroll (Sun).
