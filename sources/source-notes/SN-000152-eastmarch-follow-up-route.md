# SN-000152 - Eastmarch Follow-Up Route

Status: route-writing source note for TB-035-MR-024.

## Scope

This note supports the v1 guide section `Windhelm Follow-Up And Eastmarch Roads`. The section routes Malborn's post-Embassy Windhelm objective, then bundles safe Eastmarch work around Candlehearth Hall, Yorgrim Overlook, Cronvangr Cave, Riverside Shack, Ansilvund, Kynesgrove, Darkwater Crossing, Mara's Eye Pond, and Gallows Hall. It deliberately leaves randomized Eastmarch retrievals, Stony Creek/Bards work, Hjerim ownership, Eastmarch thaneship, Windhelm investments, Sam Guevenne/Morvunskar, and the Solstheim ferry for later coherent route sections.

## Route Decisions

Malborn is placed first. UESP records that arriving near Windhelm after a surviving Embassy escape starts a hidden one-day timer, and that Malborn can fail if he dies before disappearing at Refugees' Rest. The guide therefore sends the player to New Gnisis Cornerclub immediately, kills J'datharr, takes the note, clears Refugees' Rest before reporting back, waits through the source-backed 24-hour buffer, then escorts Malborn until the game removes him safely.

Candlehearth Hall starts both `Caught in a Web` and `Tilted Scales` in one controlled inn stop. The guide saves before reading `Adonato Leotelli's Journal` because UESP records a start bug. `The Crimson Dirks, v4` is read before Yorgrim Overlook because UESP records that visiting the overlook first can clear the site without spawning the reward state. Both AE quests then resolve immediately in their target spaces.

Cronvangr is routed as a full bundle: the clearable cave, `Caught in a Web`, Fists of Randagulf, Ice Blade of the Monarch, Grenwulf/Holrik notes, `The Locked Room` skill-book hold, Arachnia, the Vampire Naturalist's Journal, and Teleport Pet: Arachnia. This is a direct application of the nearby-objective rule because the vampire lair and Web Mother route are inside the same cave. The guide explicitly forbids ghost follower commands because UESP records a crash risk.

Riverside Shack is routed during this Eastmarch road pass because it is a small clearable Eastmarch stop with a finite treasure map and a nearby Kynesgrove route line. The Treasure Map III chest itself is not looted here because its chest is near Solitude Lighthouse; the guide records the map now and hands the chest to the later Solitude coast section. The Fishing Map - Eastmarch is not checklist-mapped in current project data and is not treated as a completion cue.

Ansilvund is routed now because the section already moves through eastern Eastmarch, the dungeon has a full coherent bundle, and its deterministic Frost Salts source lets Dravynea's Kynesgrove favor finally start and finish in one loop. The guide leaves `2920, Sun's Dawn, v2` closed under the global Scholar's Insight policy, takes Ghostblade and the Stone of Barenziah at the end, takes `Imperial Condolences`, and uses the exit-room Frost Salts for Dravynea. The duplicate Transmute tome is only a fallback if the earlier Halted Stream source was missed.

Kynesgrove and Darkwater Crossing are used for paid ore-sale and delivery/favor work after the associated roads are already active. Kjeld and Verner are routed because the guide can mine and sell the ore in the same settlement. Sondas is now routed as a direct delivery to the actual active recipient rather than forcing Quintus Navale; this matches the random-assignment convention and avoids player-facing technical contingency text for non-PC journal mismatch cases. Darkwater Pass/Derkeethus is conditional because MR-016 may already have completed it; if not, this Eastmarch pass is the next coherent chance to finish the cave and follower row.

Mara's Eye Pond, Mara's Eye Den, and Gallows Hall are bundled. The pond only receives its cleared state once the den is cleared, so the guide enters the island trapdoor and finishes both together. Gallows Hall is then routed because it is next to the pond, grants property ownership, provides safe storage, and contains a compact AE quest, artifact, book, and crafting-system bundle. The guide tells the player not to freely loot during `Dreams of the Dead` because UESP records soul-gem/puzzle bugs. The Bone Forge is unlocked but not used because UESP records that its undead followers are permanent and cannot be dismissed. `Hooded Skeleton` and `Letter to Naara` remain internal coverage exclusions for now because their UESP book pages are linked to Gallows Hall/Dreams of the Dead but list no in-world location to route; unlike `Naara's Plea`, the Dreams walkthrough does not name them as pickup documents.

Randomized Eastmarch retrievals are not started in this block. Roggi's Ancestral Shield, Queen Freydis's Sword, Shahvee's Amulet, Annekke's bounty, Brunwulf's bounty, Harsh Master, and Torbjorn's Rare Gifts variant all either use randomized targets, depend on later Civil War or relationship state, or benefit from a later target-aware shared-location route. The guide does not ask the player to reroll targets.

Stony Creek Cave and Finn's Lute are held for the Bards College section because the instrument must be assigned before collection. Hjerim ownership, Eastmarch thaneship, Calder, Windhelm investments, and House of Clan Shatter-Shield work remain after later Civil War/property/investor state. The Solstheim ferry remains later with Dragonborn/Solstheim setup, and `A Night to Remember`/Morvunskar remains tied to the Sam Guevenne level/state route rather than this cold Eastmarch pass.
