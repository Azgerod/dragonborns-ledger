## Routing Decisions

The cultist encounter is inserted retrospectively after High Hrothgar because UESP places it after `The Way of the Voice` at the first city or town entered. The guide kills the cultists and reads `Cultists' Orders` then, but delays sailing until the Solstheim block so the main-route/Karthspire/Dawnguard/Companions dependencies stay intact.

The first Solstheim pass merges the old "entry" and "Raven Rock core" scaffolds because Raven Rock immediately offers several safe, local objectives. The route establishes Fethis, Glover, Milore, Geldis, the Temple, and the Bulwark while already in town, instead of deferring them to a later Raven Rock bucket.

`It's All In The Taste` is completed before `Served Cold` because Mirri Severin and Tilisu Severin are available recipients before the conspiracy resolves, and UESP explicitly notes this timing advantage. The guide uses nine town recipients, then Aphia or Crescius in Raven Rock Mine, so the favor finishes before `Served Cold`.

`Fetch the Netch` is started with Milore because the player is already speaking to her and Raven Rock Mine soon supplies two guaranteed Netch Jelly samples. The quest remains in progress because five samples are required; later netch-hunting/alchemy routing should finish it without forcing an isolated early netch detour.

`Paid in Full` is started with Glover because the player is a Thieves Guild member and is already speaking to him for `An Axe to Find`. Completion is held for the later Castle Karstaag/north-island route, where the Bonemold Formula and Glover's Letter/Exquisite Sapphire chain belong.

`Recipe for Distraction` is routed immediately after `March of the Dead` because it is a local Raven Rock objective unlocked by that quest and does not need a later island circuit.

Raven Rock and directly entered strongboxes are routed now: Northern Maiden, Alor House, Retching Netch, Temple Ancestral Tomb, Fort Frostmoth, Raven Rock Mine x2, Bulwark, Caerellius House, Ashfallow x3, Morvayn Manor Chambers, and the Shrine of Zenithar near Frostmoon. Glover Mallory's House strongbox is held for `Paid in Full` because that quest later grants house access and basement content. The remaining island strongboxes stay with their natural future loops: Deathbrand/north coast, Thirsk/Hrothmund, Stalhrim/Northshore, Tel Mithryn/east-island, Wreck of the Strident Squall, Vahlok's Tomb, Karstaag, and broader Solstheim sweep.

`The Final Descent` is routed start-to-finish on first Raven Rock Mine entry. This preserves the Bloodskal Blade route state, reopens the mine, acquires Bloodskal Blade/Zahkriisos/Dragon Aspect, and unlocks `Black Book: The Winds of Change`. The guide routes all four Potions of Waterwalking because UESP identifies them as the only four in the game, and takes the two guaranteed Netch Jelly bowls because they support `Fetch the Netch` and later alchemy planning.

Scholar's Insight is chosen immediately from `The Winds of Change` because the route has intentionally deferred skill-book reading. `Words and Philosophy` is then read in the same Apocrypha visit after Scholar's Insight is active, making it the first explicit skill-book read in the Scholar window.

`Deathbrand` is started by reading the Bloodskal Barrow copy after `The Final Descent`; the route is already far above the level 36 start threshold. The actual treasure circuit is held for the later north-island Deathbrand loop because that work spans multiple distant coastal sites and Gyldenhul Barrow.

`Served Cold` is routed after `March of the Dead` and `The Final Descent`, with a hard save before the conspiracy handoff. Severin Manor storage/display use is delayed until Councilor Morvayn awards the house.

Frostmoon Crag is routed immediately after Raven Rock stabilization because Beast Blood is still active and Majni's ring sales depend on lycanthropy. The guide buys all four rings before clearing Frostmoon Crag. The nearby Shrine of Zenithar pendant is routed because it is directly adjacent to the Frostmoon trip; Moesring Pass proper remains for a later northern/Thirsk pass rather than turning this into a broad pass sweep.

Held for later with concrete reasons:

* `Dragonborn` completion through the Temple of Miraak, `The Temple of Miraak`, Skaal work, and `Cleansing the Stones`: next Solstheim main-quest section, because this pass focuses on Raven Rock stabilization and Frostmoon Beast Blood access.
* `First Edition`: Cindiri's quest dialogue requires Raven Rock citizens to be freed from Miraak's influence.
* `Feeding the Addiction`: held until after `Cleansing the Stones`; the later Temple/Skaal/Stalhrim section now completes it on the Raven Rock return after the Earth Stone.
* `A New Debt`: held until the Tel Mithryn `Reluctant Steward` chain creates the Drovas/Mogrul context.
* `Put A Fork In It`: exact trigger warned in-guide because the note/fork are visible in The Retching Netch; completion needs the later Staff of Sheogorath route and staff-enchanter context.
* `Ghosts of the Tribunal`: exact Temple trigger warned in-guide because the dossier is in a routed Temple visit; branch handling remains later.
* `My Pet Nix-Hound`: held for Revus Sarvani/Tel Mithryn rather than using Geldis as a fallback.
* `Chaos Damage`: Champion's Cudgel is preserved as a unique item; learn the enchantment later from a replaceable source.
* Glover Mallory investment row: source/checklist data says the store is not investable, so this remains a data-reconciliation/merchant-reference row, not a player action.
