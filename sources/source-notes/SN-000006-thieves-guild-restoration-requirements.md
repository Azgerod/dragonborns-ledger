Status: needs review.

## Claim

Under New Management requires Thieves Guild restoration work: five Delvin or Vex jobs in each of Markarth, Solitude, Whiterun, and Windhelm, followed by a special reputation quest for each city.

## Routing Relevance

The guide specification requires Thieves Guild restoration requirements and bounded radiant jobs. The objective database therefore needs a restoration job placeholder and rows for the four finite reputation quests before route skeleton work begins.

## Evidence Summary

UESP states that Delvin and Vex jobs can be repeated and count toward restoring guild status. It identifies four required target cities for restoration jobs: Markarth, Solitude, Whiterun, and Windhelm. After five jobs in a city, Delvin gives a city-specific reputation quest: Silver Lining, The Dainty Sload, Imitation Amnesty, or Summerset Shadows.
