## Route Decisions

The Soul Cairn is split into a main-quest stabilization pass and an immediate side sweep. The first pass goes straight through Valerica, the three Keepers, Durnehviir, Elder Scroll (Blood), Soul Essence Gem, and the exit to Tamriel. This completes `Beyond Death` before the long Jiub/Arvak/Reaper sweep, matching the source-backed advice to avoid leaving the main Soul Cairn script in a fragile state while collecting side content.

The Durnehviir/Soul Tear sequence is routed immediately after the first Soul Cairn exit. The route already has late-game dragon-soul capacity, and it reserves six dragon souls here: three to unlock Summon Durnehviir and three to unlock the Soul Tear words taught after the three Tamriel summons. Learning all three Soul Tear words now prevents a later orphaned shout/trophy pass.

The Soul Cairn side sweep is still done before moving on to Dexion because it is all in the same separate worldspace and includes finite items that would otherwise become a late cleanup problem: Jiub pages, Saint Jiub rewards, Arvak, all three Soul Cairn conjuration tomes, all three Reaper Gem Fragments, and the Reaper encounter.

Forgotten Vale routing keeps the pilgrimage intact rather than splitting the dungeon chain. The section pulls forward all safe same-worldspace content: Unknown Books, all five paragons, paragon-platform destinations, Auriel's Shield, the Drain Vitality word, Dawnguard-only ingredients, Ancient Falmer armor, Sunhallowed/Bloodcursed arrows, and the Auriel's Bow sun-shot action.

The Forgotten Vale Books Quest is turned in before `Kindred Judgment` because all four Unknown Books are in hand and Urag's Arcanaeum handoff is the natural completion boundary. This is not a theme bucket; it prevents the four tomes and their translated-copy rows from lingering into later Solstheim and transformation sections.

The main Dawnguard finale is routed in this section because Auriel's Bow, Sunhallowed/Bloodcursed arrows, Dexion handoffs, and Serana's post-finale Vampire Lord service are all immediate downstream consequences of the same Dawnguard worldspace block.

## Deferrals

Serana cure dialogue is deliberately not routed after `Kindred Judgment`. The next section needs Serana's Vampire Lord service for transformation completion, and the source page says curing Serana removes her ability to provide Bloodcursed arrows or turn the player into a Vampire Lord.

Elder Scroll sale/removal is not routed in this section. The guide has now satisfied the Dawnguard use cases for the Dragon, Sun, and Blood scrolls, but the project has not yet made a final document-preservation versus post-use sale decision. The scrolls remain carried until that explicit policy is placed.

The Ancient Falmer Crown remains excluded from the route body. Vyrthur is the only normal source for Ancient Falmer armor, but the source page identifies the crown as unavailable without console commands or an unofficial patch; PS4 AE main continuity therefore routes only boots, cuirass, and gauntlets.

Darkfall Passage notes, broad Soul Cairn background books, and non-checklist Forgotten Vale loot are not routed as completion objectives. They are ordinary readable/lootable world detail outside the current required book/document scope unless a later checklist reconciliation promotes a specific title.
