# Source Note: Survival Storage, Food, and Base Infrastructure

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000117

## Claim

Survival Mode makes early safe storage, repeatable food/rest access, carry relief, and regional base infrastructure route-shaping requirements. Inns, houses, Goldenhills Plantation, Hearthfire homesteads, Camping, Adventurer's Backpacks, followers/pets, and horses each solve part of the Survival logistics problem but do not replace one another.

## Routing Relevance

The final route will collect thousands of objectives, books, ingredients, crafted materials, unique items, and location clears under a 150-base carry limit. It needs deliberate storage approval, rest points, cooking/food sources, emergency camps, and pack/carry tools before broad collectible sweeps or construction/crafting blocks.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's Survival Mode page recommends housing development, carriage access, pack pets, efficient cooked food, hot soups, and Camping Supplies as Survival supports. It also notes that completing favor quests can raise disposition for free beds and food, which makes representative favor choices potentially useful as logistics overlap rather than isolated checklist actions.

Inns provide food and lodging; renting a room costs 10 gold and gives bed access for a day, and inn sleep can grant Well Rested. The Inns page also identifies the Retching Netch as the only inn on Solstheim, making Raven Rock a key restock and sleep anchor for Dragonborn routing.

Houses are source-listed safe-storage and sleep bases. Existing property support rows identify early central candidates such as Breezehome, Tundra Homestead, and Goldenhills Plantation, later regional bases such as Honeyside, Shadowfoot Sanctum, Myrwatch, and Severin Manor, and major display/storage homes that require later route validation. Goldenhills adds crop, pantry, livestock, income, horse, steward, and farmhand systems after setup.

Camping adds a movable campsite with shelter, a warmth-providing campfire, limited cooking, and sleep. Camping Supplies require three firewood and one leather at a forge or anvil. However, camp sleep does not grant Well Rested and still counts as outdoor sleep under Survival Mode, so camping is an emergency and cold-travel tool rather than a full replacement for inns or homes. Adventurer's Backpack items increase carrying capacity by 75 points, and horses provide travel/carry relief while mounted.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high that base/storage/food/carry infrastructure must be planned before large sweeps. TB-031D selects Breezehome as first-storage bridge, Tundra Homestead as main base, Goldenhills as food/income support, Severin Manor as Solstheim base, and Whiterun Stable Horse as first bought horse. Farm crop mix, pet/follower cargo policy, camping-supply stock levels, property safe-storage final approval, and bug-safe acquisition sequencing remain constrained by later property, progression, and warning tasks.
