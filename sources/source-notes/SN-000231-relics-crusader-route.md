# Relics of the Crusader and Pilgrim's Path Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000231

## Claim

`Relics of the Crusader` should be routed during the existing Reach road pass that already clears Four Skull Lookout. The guide should make `HARD SAVE: HS-DIVINE-CRUSADER-FOUR-SKULL`, clear Four Skull Lookout, kill Remy, Viparth, Eigorn, and Oren, read/take `Viparth's Journal`, loot and preserve all twelve Divine Crusader relics from Oren and Eigorn, and then avoid equipping any Crusader relic until the final clean-continuity pilgrimage step.

`The Pilgrim's Path` should be completed once near final reconciliation, after the Master Criminal branch has been reloaded and before the accepted clean end-state save. The guide should make `HARD SAVE: HS-DIVINE-CRUSADER-PILGRIMAGE`, equip one stored Crusader relic to start the quest, unequip it, pray at the nine named shrines without committing new infamy-counting crimes, then store the relic set and avoid re-equipping it after the final check.

## Routing Relevance

The current guide already visits Four Skull Lookout during the Markarth/Old Hroldan Reach road bundle and later revisits the same location for the Destruction master ritual. Relic acquisition belongs in the first Four Skull clear so the guide does not create a shallow clear-now/reward-later split and does not miss `Viparth's Journal`.

The pilgrimage does not belong immediately after the Four Skull clear. UESP records that `The Pilgrim's Path` is tied to the player's crime statistics and can be restarted or reset by later infamy changes. The route still has required thief, Dark Brotherhood, Vampire Lord, and Master Criminal branch content after the Reach pass. Completing the pilgrimage after the Master Criminal branch is reloaded gives the guide one controlled no-crime shrine circuit and one accepted final storage state.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's Divine Crusader page says the Creation adds the Crusader's Relics and the Reforged Crusader's Relics, and its official summary points players to Four Skull Lookout east-northeast of Markarth. The same page says Oren carries the Mace of the Crusader and heavy relic armor set, while Eigorn carries the Sword of the Crusader and the light Reforged relic set. It lists `Viparth's Journal`, both weapons, the five heavy pieces, the five light pieces, and both quests as Divine Crusader content.

UESP's `Relics of the Crusader` page says the quest does not appear in the journal. Its quick and detailed walkthroughs send the player to Four Skull Lookout, have the player kill the named bandits, and complete the quest by looting all twelve relic items from the bodies. The reward list names Mace of the Crusader, Shield of the Crusader, Boots of the Crusader, Cuirass of the Crusader, Gauntlets of the Crusader, Helm of the Crusader, Sword of the Crusader, Fastened Shield of the Crusader, Reforged Boots of the Crusader, Reforged Cuirass of the Crusader, Reforged Gauntlets of the Crusader, and Reforged Helm of the Crusader. The page also says `Viparth's Journal` can be looted for the relic backstory and records that Anniversary Edition added the `The Pilgrim's Path` morality handling.

UESP's Four Skull Lookout page confirms the location is clearable, has a minimum level of 8, is in the Reach south of Karthwasten and east of Blind Cliff Cave, is one of the three `Destruction Ritual Spell` locations, and contains the named Divine Crusader bandits when the Creation is installed. The existing route is already safely beyond the location's minimum level, and the same Four Skull clear now handles location coverage, relic acquisition, and `Viparth's Journal` together.

UESP's `The Pilgrim's Path` page says the quest starts by equipping any Crusader armor piece from Four Skull Lookout when Infamy is above zero, and it requires prayer at nine shrines: Akatosh north of Rorikstead, Arkay at Wayward Pass southwest of Ironbind Barrow, Dibella south of Old Hroldan, Julianos southeast of Tumble Arch Pass, Kynareth northeast of Brood Cavern, Mara northwest of Volunruud, Stendarr at Stendarr's Beacon, Talos east of Lakeview Manor, and Zenithar west of Four Skull Lookout. Its quest stage 100 is the completion boundary.

The route does not use the Crusader relics as active combat gear. UESP records that wearing relics while unfit imposes major Health/Stamina regeneration and Carry Weight penalties, and that the quest can restart or lose shrine progress when infamy changes or certain shrine activations fail. The guide therefore claims the items at Four Skull, stores them as preserved uniques, and waits until the final accepted continuity has reloaded the Master Criminal branch before starting and completing the pilgrimage.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the Four Skull acquisition route, the full twelve-item reward list, `Viparth's Journal` pickup, Four Skull clearability, pilgrimage start condition, shrine list, and final storage policy.

The main operational risk is `The Pilgrim's Path` bug surface. On PS4 there is no console fallback for the source-listed shrine/progress bugs, so the guide uses a named save before triggering the pilgrimage and keeps the shrine circuit crime-free. Live PS4 playtesting should verify that the final shrine circuit completes from the route's late clean-continuity state.
