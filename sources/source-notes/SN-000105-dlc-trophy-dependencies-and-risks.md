# Source Note: DLC Trophy Dependencies and Risks

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000105

## Claim

Dawnguard, Hearthfire, and Dragonborn trophies add quest, property, adoption, transformation-perk, shout, artifact-use, crafting, dragon-riding, location, and Solstheim house requirements. Several require route protection: use Auriel's Bow before it can be lost, complete Werewolf/Vampire Lord perk trees before permanently losing access, delay Legendary Dragon hunting until high level, protect `A New Source of Stalhrim`, and avoid vampire state when using Galathil for `A New You`.

## Routing Relevance

These trophies must be integrated into the main continuity despite the canonical Dawnguard side and final-state decisions. The route should complete both transformation perk trees in controlled phases, build all Hearthfire houses for trophy credit, and route Dragonborn systems so Solstheim trophy counters are completed without relying on random cleanup.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's achievements page lists ten Dawnguard trophies, five Hearthfire trophies, and ten Dragonborn trophies. It also marks `Auriel's Bow`, `Werewolf Mastered`, and `Stalhrim Crafter` as missable or access-sensitive. The Auriel's Bow page confirms that shooting the sun with Sunhallowed or Bloodcursed Elven Arrows is the bow's special power behavior; the achievements page warns that the bow can be missed for trophy purposes if lost after `Kindred Judgement`.

The Lycanthropy and Vampire Lord pages each state that the relevant perk tree has 11 perks. Lycanthropy can be restored by Aela only once after cure, and becoming a Vampire Lord can cure lycanthropy, so the route should finish werewolf perks before permanent cure choices. Vampire Lord can be obtained from Harkon on the Volkihar route or from Serana later; the same page says Galathil will not change the player's face while they are a vampire or Vampire Lord.

For Hearthfire, UESP lists adoption and construction trophies, and the Construction page clarifies that `Master Architect` credit requires all three wings on each of the three Hearthfire houses. For Dragonborn, UESP lists the trophy requirements for five Black Books, one Stalhrim item, five ridden dragons, Raven Rock home ownership, and 30 Solstheim locations; the Dragon page says Legendary Dragons begin appearing at level 78.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for DLC trophy requirements and major trophy risks. TB-016 should decide NPC and transformation-state protections; TB-017 should handle bug-specific no-console mitigations; TB-020 should integrate all-perks grinding with Werewolf/Vampire Lord perk phases.
