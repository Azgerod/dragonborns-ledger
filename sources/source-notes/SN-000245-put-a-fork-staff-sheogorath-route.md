# Put A Fork In It Staff of Sheogorath Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000245

## Claim

`Put A Fork In It` should be routed after `Reluctant Steward` unlocks Neloth's Staff Enchanter during the existing Tel Mithryn section.

For this route:

- During the first Raven Rock core stop, leave the green-glowing `Mysterious Note` and Fork of Horripilation untouched so the trial is not started before Staff Enchanter access.
- In the Tel Mithryn section, complete `Reluctant Steward`, receive the Staff Enchanter key, and use the Staff Enchanter room for the existing `Black Book: The Hidden Twilight` and Staff of Flames craft.
- Return to The Retching Netch, read/take `Mysterious Note`, take the Fork of Horripilation, and start `Put A Fork In It`.
- Kill the marked bull netch on the Isle of Basalt west of the Earth Stone with the Fork of Horripilation and loot `Ciirta's Eye`.
- Kill the marked bull netch due west of Skaal Village near the icy waterfall with the Fork of Horripilation and loot the `Branch of the Tree of Shades`.
- Return to the Tel Mithryn Staff Enchanter with two Heart Stones available after the Staff of Flames craft and Neloth's Heart Stone sale. If short, mine Heart Stone deposits on Solstheim before crafting.
- Craft the Staff of Sheogorath with `Ciirta's Eye`, the `Branch of the Tree of Shades`, and two Heart Stones, then preserve the staff and the Fork of Horripilation at Severin Manor.

## Routing Relevance

This closes `OBJ-000648` `Put A Fork In It`, `CHK-QUESTS-0578`, `OBJ-000745` `Staff of Sheogorath Unique Staff`, `OBJ-001463` `Mysterious Note (Staff of Sheogorath)`, `ITEM-001094` through `ITEM-001097`, and the Staff Enchanter cross-reference row `ITEM-000761`.

The route belongs in `Tel Mithryn, Nchardak, and Kagrumez` because that section already unlocks the Tel Mithryn Staff Enchanter through `Reluctant Steward`, already uses the Staff Enchanter, already maintains Heart Stone logistics, and already returns through Raven Rock and northern Solstheim. Starting the quest earlier at first Raven Rock arrival would create unnecessary backtracking or a long open quest state before the staff can be crafted.

No branch route is needed. The quest has a single completion path in current AE routing: take the note/fork, kill both specified netches with the fork, craft the staff, and preserve the unique weapon/staff rewards.

## Evidence Summary

The quest page records that the trial begins from the `Mysterious Note` and Fork of Horripilation in The Retching Netch. It also records that the two special bull netches must be killed with the fork, with one near the Isle of Basalt west of the Earth Stone and the other due west of Skaal Village by an icy waterfall.

The same quest page records the crafting endpoint: after looting `Ciirta's Eye` and the `Branch of the Tree of Shades`, the player must use a Staff Enchanter and two Heart Stones to craft the Staff of Sheogorath. It notes that Tel Mithryn Staff Enchanter access requires `Reluctant Steward`, while any Staff Enchanter can satisfy the recipe.

The Creation and item pages confirm that the Creation contains the `Mysterious Note`, the two netch quest items, the Fork of Horripilation, the Staff of Sheogorath, and the quest `Put A Fork In It`. The item page records the fork as the quest weapon and the staff as craftable at a Staff Enchanter, making both the fork and final staff preservation-relevant unique items.

The AE note on the quest page records that the original install-time quest trigger changed in Anniversary Edition. The current route therefore starts the quest by reading/taking the Retching Netch note during normal Solstheim play rather than assuming an automatic installation quest entry.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the quest start, required fork handling, two netch locations, quest-item drops, Staff Enchanter and Heart Stone requirements, and Tel Mithryn placement after `Reluctant Steward`.

There is no remaining open route question for `OBJ-000648`, `OBJ-000745`, or `OBJ-001463`. The guide uses the Tel Mithryn Staff Enchanter rather than Myrwatch because Tel Mithryn is already unlocked in the same Solstheim section.
