# Source Note: Companions Primary Quests

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000003

## Claim

The base-game Companions primary questline consists of six named primary quests, with required radiant quest gates between several primary quests. Its faction trophies are tied to Take Up Arms, joining the Circle during The Silver Hand, and Glory of the Dead.

## Routing Relevance

The objective database needs one row per primary quest and one placeholder row for the required interstitial radiant gates so the eventual route does not skip questline progress requirements. Trophy-linked rows need trophy cues later in route prose.

## Evidence Summary

UESP lists the Companions primary questline as Take Up Arms, Proving Honor, The Silver Hand, Blood's Honor, Purity of Revenge, and Glory of the Dead. The page records required radiant quest gates after Take Up Arms, after Proving Honor, and after The Silver Hand. The trophy entries are Take Up Arms, Blood Oath, and Glory of the Dead.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the quest list and trophy names. Individual radiant quest type selection, availability windows, bug risks, and exact route placement still require later radiant and constraint-table research.
