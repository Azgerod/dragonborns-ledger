# Source Note: Permanent Quest Ability and Power Sets

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000049

## Claim

Several base-game, DLC, and AE quests grant permanent abilities or reusable powers that are completion-relevant and need source-list objective coverage before route and checklist synchronization passes.

## Routing Relevance

The specification requires permanent spells, powers, and abilities obtainable on the main route. This pass records the reward layer without deciding exact timing, default choice recommendations, final state, or whether a later checklist requires additional temporary, racial, shrine, disease, or item-granted effects.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's Powers page provides the broad reward inventory, while quest-specific pages support important rows whose route timing depends on quest access, NPCs, or faction state. This pass intentionally records parent or reward-system rows where the later route needs to know that a power or ability exists, but does not yet decide exact route order or preferred choices.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is moderate to high for the recorded source-list rows. Later validation should check whether the checklist expects any additional nonpermanent effects, item-equipped powers, racial powers, shrine blessings, disease states, or Creation Club spell powers as separate rows.
