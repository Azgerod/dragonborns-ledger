# Source Note: Dragonborn Trophy and System Objectives

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000033

## Claim

Dragonborn adds trophy-relevant objectives for arriving on Solstheim, completing three main-quest milestones, learning all three Dragon Aspect words, learning secrets from five Black Books, crafting a Stalhrim item, taming and riding five dragons, owning a house in Raven Rock, and discovering 30 Solstheim locations.

## Routing Relevance

These trophies span quest, shout, Black Book, crafting, property, dragon-riding, and location systems. Parent trophy rows make later QA easier because the guide must prove each trophy's underlying requirements are routed and trophy-safe without prematurely writing final route prose.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's achievements page lists the Dragonborn trophies and their descriptions, including `Hidden Knowledge` for five Black Books, `Stalhrim Crafter` for one crafted Stalhrim item, `Dragonrider` for five dragons, `Raven Rock Owner`, and `Solstheim Explorer` for 30 locations. The Dragon Riding page states that riding requires all three words of Bend Will and notes that dragon riding is a Survival Mode fast-travel exception alongside carriages and boats. UESP's Stalhrim and `A New Source of Stalhrim` pages identify the quest and Smithing/Ebony Smithing requirements behind Stalhrim crafting. The Black Book compilation lists seven Black Books, so the maximalist scope should expect all seven even though the trophy requires five.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for trophy names and broad requirements. Later validation must still check PS4-specific behavior, Dragon Aspect word locations, exact rideable dragon candidates, Stalhrim crafting bug risks, Severin Manor ownership bugs, and the Solstheim location list.
