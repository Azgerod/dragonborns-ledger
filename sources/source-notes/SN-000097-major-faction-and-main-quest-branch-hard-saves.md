# Source Note: Major Faction and Main-Quest Branch Hard Saves

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000097

## Claim

The route needs hard saves for the Civil War side choice, Dawnguard versus Volkihar, Dark Brotherhood join versus destroy, and the Paarthurnax/Blades conflict. The project's canonical continuity is Imperial, Dawnguard, Dark Brotherhood join, and Paarthurnax preserved; alternate substantial content belongs on branches.

## Routing Relevance

These are full branch-route decisions, not option-list choices. The main route should not cross any commitment point without a named hard save, and the alternate branch should be played from that save before reloading to the canonical continuity.

## Evidence Summary

UESP lists distinct Imperial and Stormcloak Civil War questlines. It also records that Season Unending can change the required Civil War subset and can put Civil War progress on hold until the main quest is complete, with a War Hero missability risk if the relevant fort battle is skipped.

For Dawnguard, `Bloodline` is the explicit faction fork: accepting Lord Harkon's gift starts the Volkihar path, while refusing sends the player back to Isran and the Dawnguard path. UESP lists faction-specific rewards and finite/radiant work on each side.

For the Dark Brotherhood, `With Friends Like These...` forks at the Abandoned Shack: killing a captive opens the join route, while killing Astrid fails that route and starts `Destroy the Dark Brotherhood!`. UESP's destroy page lists major join-route rewards and services that become unavailable. `Delayed Burial` must be resolved before entering the Sanctuary or starting the destroy route if the route wants that side quest completed.

For Paarthurnax, UESP records reciprocal faction consequences: killing Paarthurnax restores Blades help but loses Greybeards support, while sparing him preserves Greybeards support and leaves Blades help blocked. UESP also records timing-sensitive Blades content around Esbern, Delphine, and dragon-hunting support.
