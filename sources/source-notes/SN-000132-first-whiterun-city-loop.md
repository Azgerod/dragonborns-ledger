# First Whiterun City Loop Route Detail

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000132

## Claim

The v1 first-Whiterun section should be geographic, not theme-bucketed. Once the player enters Whiterun for `Before the Storm`, the guide should route the safe same-city objectives that naturally attach to the first city loop: Dragonsreach discovery, `Greatsword for a Great Man`, `Salt for Arcadia`, Carlotta's `A Few Words with You`, `Bullying Braith`, `Andurs' Arkay Amulet`, the Whiterun Hall of the Dead Stone of Barenziah, Hulda chop-wood support, crime-safe `Argonian Ale Extraction`, the Uthgerd brawl, and the relevant counter updates. Objectives that depend on later state, level gates, branch saves, property permission, or unrelated building/content bundles should remain out of the player-facing route until their coherent route window.

## Routing Relevance

Earlier guide drafts treated the first Whiterun visit as a narrow main-quest checkpoint with many "do not start" warnings. The current drafting standard instead requires a nearby-objective audit across all objective types. Whiterun contains several short finite miscellaneous objectives that start and finish in the same city or naturally pair with the Dragonsreach/Bannered Mare/Hall of the Dead path. Routing them now avoids artificial returns and gives the player a concrete early Hero of the People counter baseline.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's `Greatsword for a Great Man` page starts the objective with Adrianne Avenicci at Warmaiden's and sends the player to Proventus Avenicci in Dragonsreach. Since the route is already entering Whiterun and going to Dragonsreach, this delivery belongs in the first city loop. The guide does not store or display Balgruuf's Greatsword, avoiding the page's rack/plaque bug.

UESP's `Salt for Arcadia` page starts from Farengar in Dragonsreach by asking whether he is the only wizard in town, then sends the player to Arcadia's Cauldron during business hours. Because the route is already speaking to Farengar for the Dragonstone objective and Arcadia is a short same-city turn-in, the delivery belongs in the first Whiterun loop.

UESP's `A Few Words with You` page includes the Whiterun Carlotta/Mikael variant: Carlotta gives the problem in the market, Mikael is in the Bannered Mare, and the target can be persuaded, bribed, intimidated, or brawled. The guide tries Persuade first and records Snake Tongue progress only if it succeeds; otherwise the brawl fallback completes the miscellaneous objective without relying on an early Speech check.

UESP's `Bullying Braith` page starts with Lars Battle-Born, who can only be spoken to between 8am and 8pm, then sends the player to Braith and back. The guide therefore places this in the daytime city loop and does not split it across later visits.

UESP's `Andurs' Arkay Amulet` page sends the player from Andurs in Whiterun's Hall of the Dead into Whiterun Catacombs. The page identifies Andurs' Amulet on a table, a Whiterun Catacombs Unusual Gem/Stone of Barenziah, and a `Death Blow of Abernanit` skill-book copy in the same catacombs. It also warns that starting while carrying an ordinary Amulet of Arkay can block completion unless ordinary amulets are dropped first. The guide therefore bundles the amulet, Stone of Barenziah, and skill-book hold in one explicit Hall of the Dead pass, with the amulet bug mitigation before speaking to Andurs.

UESP's `Argonian Ale Extraction` page unlocks Brenuin's ale request after the player gives him a gold coin and asks whether he is always drunk. It places the Argonian Ale in the Bannered Mare kitchen and notes that raising Hulda's disposition by selling firewood makes the low-value inn items free to take. UESP's `Chop Wood` page lists Hulda as a Whiterun firewood buyer, requires a woodcutter's axe, and notes that activating a chopping block is sufficient for the Hard Worker wood action. The Bannered Mare page confirms the rear wood chopping block and the unique Argonian Ale in the kitchen. The guide therefore chops and sells firewood to Hulda before taking the ale, and uses the item prompt as the player-facing theft check.

The counter plan records Hero of the People as a finite miscellaneous-objective counter and warns not to rely on drunk favors or repeated no-journal actions. The guide therefore counts `A Lovely Letter` plus the six finite Whiterun miscellaneous objectives completed in this loop for a 7/50 Hero of the People checkpoint. It records Hulda chop wood for the representative activity and Hard Worker action progress, but does not count it for Hero of the People.

UESP's `In My Time Of Need` page lists `Dragon Rising` as the previous quest and describes the Alik'r gate scene after Irileth reports the Western Watchtower dragon. The first Whiterun visit is therefore too early for this objective. The guide notes the post-Dragon Rising availability in MR-004 and keeps the quest start, branch/default handling, and Swindler's Den travel together for the next Whiterun block.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the first Whiterun city-loop placement, the finite miscellaneous objective completions, the Hall of the Dead bundle, the crime-safe Argonian Ale route, and the counter updates. The remaining open questions belong to later sections: exact Whiterun property/storage purchase sequencing, final Snake Tongue guaranteed slots if the Mikael Persuade fails, later skill-book copy/read timing, and later AE/Creation Whiterun document bundles that are not touched by this route.
