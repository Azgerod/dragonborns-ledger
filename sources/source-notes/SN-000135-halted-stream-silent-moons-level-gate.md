# Halted Stream And Silent Moons Level Gate

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000135

## Claim

After the early Whiterun farm-road loop, the guide should correct the remaining nearby farm discoveries, then route a north-of-Whiterun loop through Whitewatch Tower, Halted Stream Camp, and Silent Moons Camp. Halted Stream is routed first because it cleanly supplies the pickaxe-supported mining action for Hard Worker, a Mammoth Tusk for Ysolda, `Poacher's Axe`, `Spell Tome: Transmute Mineral Ore`, and a clearable-location row. Silent Moons Camp remains gated until level 8+ for the Lunar weapon placements.

## Routing Relevance

This pass applies the geographic-routing standard without asking the player to manipulate randomized radiant target assignment. Amren's Family Sword was accepted early because UESP records a Whiterun first-visit risk for Amren and Ysolda. The guide now respects the actual active marker: if Amren's radiant target is Halted Stream Camp, the sword is collected during the Halted Stream clear; otherwise the favor remains active for its assigned hideout.

## Evidence Summary

The farm-loop correction adds Pelagia Farm and Chillfurrow Farm because both are source-listed discoverable Whiterun farm markers in the same early farm-road geography as Battle-Born Farm. Pelagia's page also notes the optional Companions giant scene. The guide lets that scene resolve if it appears but keeps the actual Companions join sequence in its later faction block.

UESP's Halted Stream Camp page lists a pickaxe near the first interior ore vein, sixteen iron veins, one corundum vein, Mammoth Tusks in the first hallway and main chamber, `Poacher's Axe` on a bandit in the mine, and `Spell Tome: Transmute Mineral Ore` in the boss chamber. Killing the bandit leader clears the camp. This makes Halted Stream a coherent early bundle for Hard Worker mining, Ysolda's tusk, the unique axe, the Transmute spell, and Delver/location progress.

UESP's `Rare Gifts` page lists Ysolda's Mammoth Tusk objective and names Halted Stream Camp as a non-stealing tusk source. The same page records that only one rare-gift quest of the type can be assigned at once, so the guide clears Ysolda's favor promptly after the tusk source.

UESP's `Dungeon Delving (Bandits)` page lists Amren's possible target dungeons as Halted Stream Camp, Redoran's Retreat, Swindler's Den, Valtheim Keep, and White River Watch. It places the quest item in the target boss chest. The guide therefore does not try to force a target; it gives a conditional Halted Stream pickup only when the player's active marker naturally points there.

UESP's Whitewatch Tower page lists it as a non-clearable discoverable tower north-northeast of Whiterun and notes guarded chest ownership risks. The nearby unmarked camp has AE Staff of Hasedoki and skill-book context, but that camp is not part of the tower discovery. The guide only routes the tower marker here.

UESP's Silent Moons Camp page says the best possible Lunar weapons spawn at level 8+, gives four random Lunar weapon placements, and warns that the weapons can be absent if the player was in the area too early and kept preventing respawn. The guide therefore only enters and loots the camp at level 8+, names all six possible Lunar weapon forms as exact conditional checklist cues, and preserves every acquired Lunar weapon. The `Light Armor Forging` copy is left closed under the global Scholar's Insight skill-book policy. `Notes On The Lunar Forge` remains a regular-book scope exclusion rather than a required checklist pickup.

The Light Damage page identifies Silent Moons Enchant as learned by disenchanting one of the Lunar Iron or Lunar Steel mace, sword, or war axe forms. Current progression-source selections defer the actual disenchant action until storage and source-item selection are routed, so MR-007 sources the enchantment but does not mark it learned.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the farm-marker correction, Halted Stream bundle, Ysolda turn-in, Amren conditional handling, Whitewatch discovery, and level-8 Silent Moons entry gate. Lunar weapon exact-form coverage remains random-spawn dependent: this pass records every form that actually appears and preserves all acquired Lunar weapons without asking the player to reload or manipulate the outcome.
