# Heart Of Crimson And Ebony Plate Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000222

## Claim

`Heart of Crimson` should be routed as a level-32 Windhelm support action after Malborn's `Find the Thalmor Assassin` danger window is safe and before the East Empire Company island trip if the courier letter has arrived. The route reads and preserves `Letter from Tyra Blood-Fire`, gives the Windhelm Docks beggar one gold, defeats Tyra Blood-Fire at the Watcher of Windhelm Shrine of Talos, reads and preserves `The Crimson Dirks, v1` and `To (Player)`, preserves Tyra's four enchanted Ebony Plate pieces, and records that normal Ebony Plate pieces are craftable/random loot after quest completion.

## Routing Relevance

The quest page identifies the level-32 courier start, the Windhelm Docks beggar step, the shrine west/southwest of Windhelm as Tyra's combat location, and Tyra's enchanted Ebony Plate reward set. The Creation page confirms that `Heart of Crimson`, `Letter from Tyra Blood-Fire`, `The Crimson Dirks, v1`, `To (Player)`, Tyra, the beggar, and the Ebony Plate armor pieces belong to `Alternative Armors - Ebony Plate`.

The route deliberately waits until Malborn is safe because the current Windhelm follow-up says Malborn's post-embassy danger window must be handled before other Windhelm errands. It then places `Heart of Crimson` before `Rise in the East` so the player is still at Windhelm Docks and has not entered the no-follower/no-pet Japhet's Folly sequence.

The item page separates Tyra's enchanted four-piece set from the normal four Ebony Plate pieces. Tyra's set is the deterministic quest reward and is preserved immediately. The normal Ebony Plate armor, boots, gauntlets, and helmet can appear as random loot and can be crafted after `Heart of Crimson`; the guide defers those normal copies to the later Ebony Smithing crafting window in the All-Perks Loop rather than treating random loot as required timing. The same item page marks the Blindfold unobtainable and records that Blades Ebony Shield is random heavy-armor loot with a quest-script bug if looted. The guide therefore excludes the Blindfold, warns not to pick up Blades Ebony Shield before `Heart of Crimson` completes, and treats any later shield appearance as opportunistic preservation rather than a deterministic quest reward.

## Evidence Summary

`Heart of Crimson` starts when the courier delivers `Letter from Tyra Blood-Fire` after level 32. The quest's first action is not combat; it sends the player to give alms to the Windhelm Docks beggar. The guide chooses the one-gold option because the source says the choice does not change whether Tyra can be faced, and one gold is the lowest-resource normal-play choice.

Tyra's shrine fight is deterministic once the beggar objective is complete. She is a hostile nightblade with a high player-level multiplier and a full enchanted Ebony Plate set, so the guide makes a rotating save and keeps the fight away from civilian splash damage. The completion boundary is Tyra's defeat plus taking the armor from her body.

The book/document handling is deterministic. `Letter from Tyra Blood-Fire` is the courier start document, while `The Crimson Dirks, v1` and `To (Player)` are carried by Tyra. The route reads and takes all three because the checklist maps `The Crimson Dirks, v1` to `Heart of Crimson`, and the other two are AE quest documents for the same Creation.

The parent-set handling needs three policies. First, Tyra's enchanted armor, boots, gauntlets, and helmet are required and preserved now. Second, the normal unenchanted armor, boots, gauntlets, and helmet are unlocked after quest completion and are deferred to the later Ebony Smithing crafting window, where the route can create one normal copy of each without relying on random loot. Third, the Blindfold is excluded as unobtainable, and Blades Ebony Shield is not a deterministic route pickup because the item page says it is random heavy-armor loot and has a quest-script bug when looted.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the quest start, level gate, beggar step, Tyra fight location, Tyra reward set, and document pickups.

The only retained nonblocking item-policy caveat is Blades Ebony Shield. Current sources show it as obtainable from leveled heavy-armor loot, not a fixed quest reward. The guide therefore prevents the prequest bug and preserves it only if it appears later, while the final random-loot acquisition policy remains a broader unique-item route question if the project later decides every random-only item needs deterministic proof.
