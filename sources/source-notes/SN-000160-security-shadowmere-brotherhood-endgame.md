## Route Decisions

`Breaching Security` uses the city-kill bonus because Olava's Token is required for `Locate the Assassin of Old`. The guide defaults to Whiterun because Gaius has a clean city stop there and UESP documents the provocation/self-defense method. `Letter from Father`, `Gaius Maro's Schedule`, and the Dark Brotherhood `Incriminating Letter` are routed as quest-document rows.

`Locate the Assassin of Old` is routed immediately after receiving Olava's Token. Hag's End is not entered before Olava's reading because the hidden-room switch is quest-state dependent and the assassin corpse has a source-listed absence bug. If Deepwood Redoubt was already cleared by the earlier randomized Reach-target branch, the guide passes through it rather than duplicating the clear. Otherwise it clears Deepwood Redoubt now. Predator's Grace, the Ancient Shrouded set, Slow Time: Tiid, Bloodthorn, and Hag's End's clear row all belong to this visit. Loose skill books and Red Eagle-starting books remain closed.

`The Cure for Madness` preserves Cicero on the main route. UESP records that a spared Cicero returns after `Hail Sithis!` and becomes a follower; killing him would close that option. Cicero's clothing set is therefore staged for a later controlled pickpocket or clothing-source pass rather than acquired by killing him now. Shadowmere is acquired immediately when the quest presents him, with a hard save because the horse can fail to appear.

Nightgate Inn is expanded beyond the Brotherhood target because the same stop contains `Nightgate Inn Patron's Note`, the barrel behind the inn, and `Undercover Guard's Journal`. `Brothers in Irons` has no level gate in the current constraint index, and at this point the route is past the early Legendary/Survival stabilization phase, so the guide completes the whole Nightgate-to-Clam-Digger's-Camp chain instead of leaving an AE quest half-started.

Balbus's Fork remains a random-encounter problem. The guide names the Writ window and tells the player what to do if Balbus appears naturally, but it does not ask the player to manipulate random encounters. The coverage tracker keeps this as `NEEDS ROUTE RESOLUTION` until a deterministic or explicitly approved random-encounter policy exists.

`Recipe for Disaster` hides Balagog's body for Nightweaver's Band. `To Kill an Empire` preserves Jarrin Root because UESP says the decoy Emperor can be killed without poisoning the potage, and the project alchemy plan needs the one-copy ingredient for the later effect-discovery pass. The guide leaves `Catalogue of Weapon Enchantments` closed because this loose skill book should wait for Scholar's Insight; UESP notes the Emperor's Tower remains accessible after the quest, while the dining hall itself locks.

The Katariah visit routes every safe same-ship objective with current support: Windshear, The Katariah clear, Emperor's Robes, Gilded Wristguards, `Brothers of Darkness`, and `Glories and Laments`. The Katariah `Lost Legends` copy is now a duplicate because the retrospective Folgunthur audit moved the Blue Palace copy and Mikrul fragment into the earlier Bone Wolf route.

`Hail Sithis!` includes Commander Maro and Amaund because both are source-listed optional finale beats and no longer protect later route dependencies. Volunruud is already cleared, so the finale uses the existing chamber for the payment dead drop rather than adding a new Volunruud clear.

The Dawnstar Sanctuary restoration is routed immediately after the 20,000 gold reward because the full upgrade bill is 19,000 gold. The guide buys all five upgrades, returns for Tumblerbane Gloves, records both Dark Brotherhood Initiates as follower options, interrogates all four torture victims at once, and then collects all four hidden caches. `The Dark Brotherhood Forever` is handled as one representative post-finale contract; because client and target are random, the guide lists all possible clients/targets and instructs the player to follow the actual marker rather than rerolling.

## Coverage Notes

This pass directly places the Dark Brotherhood endgame questline, the Nightgate `Brothers in Irons` AE chain, the Olava/Ancient Shrouded/Deepwood/Hag's End bundle, Shadowmere, Dawnstar Sanctuary, Nightweaver's Band, preserved Jarrin Root, Blade of Woe, the Katariah unique gear and books, Dawnstar Sanctuary restoration, Tumblerbane Gloves, Dark Brotherhood Initiates, all four hidden treasure caches, and one representative post-finale contract.

Held or staged rows are mapped in coverage rather than exposed as broad player-facing lists: loose skill books in Deepwood Redoubt, Hag's End, Dawnstar Sanctuary, Nightgate Inn, The Katariah, and Castle Dour; Cicero's clothing set; Jarrin Root effect discovery; Absorb Health enchantment learning from nonunique sources; the remaining Gauldur stages after the earlier Folgunthur route; and Balbus's Fork if the random encounter does not occur during the writ window.

This pass has one unresolved underlying objective, represented by two coverage rows: Balbus's Fork needs a deterministic random-encounter solution or an approved random-encounter policy.
