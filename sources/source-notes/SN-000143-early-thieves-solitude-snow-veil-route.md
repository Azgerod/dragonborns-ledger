# Source Note: Early Thieves, Solitude, And Snow Veil Route

Status: reviewed for TB-035-MR-015 draft placement.

Source note ID: SN-000143

## Claim

The v1 guide can route `Loud and Clear`, `Dampened Spirits`, `Scoundrel's Folly`, and `Speaking With Silence` as one early Thieves Guild chain, while using the forced `Scoundrel's Folly` Solitude visit to complete safe Solitude city favors, Blue Palace objectives, `The Mind of Madness`, and colocated collectibles. The route must stop after `Speaking With Silence` because `Hard Answers` is next and awards the Nightingale Blade, which the project holds to the level-46 reward block.

## Routing Relevance

This section replaces the old scaffold that treated the Thieves chain as a narrow faction bucket. `Scoundrel's Folly` sends the player to Solitude and the East Empire Company Warehouse, so the route now pulls forward same-city objectives whose start and next progress are in the same visit: Sorex's delivery, Octieve's favor, `Return to Grace`, `Fit for a Jarl`, `The Spiced Wine`, `No News is Good News`, `The Mind of Madness`, the Blue Palace Stone of Barenziah, Purple Butterfly in a Jar, and Firebrand Wine Case. It also records which nearby Solitude content is intentionally held for a later coherent bundle.

## Evidence Summary

`Loud and Clear` routes Goldenglow after Vex identifies the sewer approach. The Goldenglow Estate page places the Queen Bee Statue and Bee in a Jar in Aringoth's quarters and the bill of sale in the safe. `Loud and Clear` requires burning exactly three beehives; burning more forfeits the gold reward. The guide therefore uses the sewer, takes the two room collectibles, reads the bill of sale, burns exactly three hives, and turns in the Queen Bee Statue immediately.

`Dampened Spirits` sends the player from Maven to Mallus at the Bannered Mare, then to Honningbrew. UESP places Hamelyn's Journal on Hamelyn, the Promissory Note and `Have Need of Cynric` in Sabjorn's quarters, and the Honningbrew Decanter upstairs. It also records that Mallus becomes the Honningbrew fence after the quest. The guide therefore clears the basement/boilery route, reads the documents, takes the decanter, keeps one Honningbrew Mead for the later Charmed Necklace random encounter, completes the quest, and turns the decanter in to Delvin.

`Scoundrel's Folly` sends the player to Solitude and the Blue Palace for Firebrand Wine before Gulum-Ei can be followed. The Solitude pass routes safe colocated objectives whose start and next progress are in the same city visit: Sorex's delivery, Octieve's debt favor, `Return to Grace`, `Fit for a Jarl`, `The Spiced Wine`, `No News is Good News`, and `The Mind of Madness`. Sorex's delivery is placed before `No News is Good News` because UESP records a bug where accepting `No News is Good News` first can prevent Sorex from offering his delivery.

The Blue Palace page supports the Firebrand Wine Case placement, the Solitude Stone of Barenziah in Elisif's bedroom, and the Pelagius Wing access route. `The Mind of Madness` supports starting with Dervenin, receiving Pelagius' Hip Bone, getting Pelagius Wing access from palace staff, completing the Wabbajack trials, and receiving Wabbajack. The Miscellaneous Items and Saints & Seducers item pages place Purple Butterfly in a Jar in the Pelagius Wing.

`Scoundrel's Folly`, Gulum-Ei, the East Empire Company Warehouse, and Brinewater Grotto pages support the warehouse follow, East Empire Shipping Map pickup, Brinewater books, Gulum-Ei confrontation, and sparing Gulum-Ei to keep him as a fence. The route uses a named branch save for Gulum-Ei's Confession because the confession requires killing him, while the main route keeps him alive. It then returns to Mercer, upgrades the Thieves Guild Armor chest piece through Tonilia, and turns in the East Empire Shipping Map.

`Speaking With Silence` and Snow Veil Sanctum support the locked Mercer entry, no-follower door caution, Model Ship, Disarm word wall, Gallus's Encoded Journal handoff, and quest order into `Hard Answers`. Snow Veil has a source-listed clear-state caveat, so the guide marks discovery/entry but does not promise a normal cleared tag. `Hard Answers` is the next quest and awards the Nightingale Blade, so the guide stops with `Hard Answers` active and does not proceed to Enthir or Calcelmo until the level-46 Thieves reward block.

`The Litany of Larceny` records the larceny item locations and a completion-state caveat if items are held too long. The guide therefore turns in the Queen Bee Statue, Honningbrew Decanter, East Empire Shipping Map, and Model Ship promptly rather than stockpiling them.

## Nearby Objective Audit And Deferrals

The Solitude first visit intentionally does not start Bards College, `Tending the Flames`, Bards instrument quests, `The Man Who Cried Wolf`, `Lights Out!`, Dead Man's Dread, Lord's Mail, Proudspire Manor, Solitude merchant investments, or the Solitude horse. Bards College has its own bug/order constraints and remote Dead Men's Respite/instrument dungeons; `The Man Who Cried Wolf` belongs with the Potema and Shield of Solitude route; Dead Man's Dread and Lord's Mail are AE quest-start documents that deserve coherent Creation routes; Proudspire and investments require later money/perk/state checks; and the Solitude horse is redundant while the route already owns the Whiterun horse.

Katla's Farm is routed because it is the carriage landing/stable farm marker for this Solitude arrival. Solitude Sawmill, Solitude Lighthouse, Treasure Map III, the Dapple Brown Wild Horse, and the surrounding Haafingar coast are held for a later exterior/coast bundle so that related map, horse, work, lighthouse, and cold-travel objectives stay together.

The skill-book copies in Goldenglow, Honningbrew, the Blue Palace, the East Empire Company Warehouse, Brinewater, and Snow Veil remain closed because the guide has not reached the Scholar's Insight reading window. The regular book `Wabbajack` is not in the Pelagius Wing; its source-listed locations are the Arcanaeum and Lost Knife Hideout, so that book-row handling remains for a later book route.

Delvin/Vex side jobs are not accepted in this section. The job ledger is created here because the player has completed enough guild setup to understand the system, but city-targeted assignment blocks wait until the route is deliberately working Markarth, Solitude, Whiterun, and Windhelm restoration counts. The guide records the job types and city counts without forcing ordinary radiant rerolls in this chapter.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the early Thieves Guild quest order, larceny turn-ins, Solitude city-favor inclusions, Gulum-Ei branch handling, Snow Veil gate, and level-46 `Hard Answers` stop. No MR-015 `NEEDS ROUTE RESOLUTION` notes remain. Later route passes still need to place Bards College, Potema/Shield of Solitude, the Solitude exterior/coast bundle, Lord's Mail, Dead Man's Dread, Proudspire Manor, Solitude merchant investments, Solitude Thieves restoration jobs, and the `Wabbajack` book copy.
