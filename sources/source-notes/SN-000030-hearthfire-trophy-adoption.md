# Source Note: Hearthfire Trophy and Adoption Requirements

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000030

## Claim

Hearthfire adds five trophy-relevant achievements: `Proud Parent`, `Landowner`, `Architect`, `Land Baron`, and `Master Architect`. Adoption is finite for route purposes because Hearthfire allows up to two adopted children, and child choice should be treated as an option/default recommendation rather than a branch.

## Routing Relevance

The final route must preserve PS4 trophy eligibility while completing Hearthfire's property and adoption scope. Trophy parent rows are useful here because the requirements span multiple property rows and later route checks need to verify that land purchases, house wings, and adoption occur safely.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's achievements page lists the five Hearthfire achievements as adopting a child, buying one plot of land, building three wings on a house, buying three plots of land, and building three houses. The Construction page cross-lists the construction achievements and notes that `Master Architect` credit requires constructing all three wings on each of the three houses.

UESP's Adoption page states that Hearthfire allows up to two adopted children, marriage is not required, and adoption requires either a child bedroom in a house or extra beds and containers in self-built houses. It also flags a bug where adoption may fail after first lacking a room for the child.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for broad trophy and adoption requirements. Later trophy validation must check PS4 timing and bug behavior. Later writer-recommendation work must choose default adopted children and family home handling without turning isolated preference choices into branches.
