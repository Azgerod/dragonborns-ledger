# Source Note: Dark Brotherhood Primary and Branch Quests

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000007

## Claim

The base-game Dark Brotherhood join-route primary questline consists of fourteen entries, with Delayed Burial marked optional. The alternate destroy quest is separate from the join route and makes the Dark Brotherhood trophy set unavailable on that branch.

## Routing Relevance

The user-resolved main route joins the Dark Brotherhood, while the destroy path is branch content.

## Evidence Summary

UESP lists the primary join-route entries and also lists Destroy the Dark Brotherhood and Where You Hang Your Enemy's Head as other Dark Brotherhood quests. UESP's achievements page states that the three Dark Brotherhood achievements become unavailable if the destroy route is chosen.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the primary list and branch separation. Contract quests, bonuses, missable rewards, NPC dependency risks, and branch-route details require later research.
