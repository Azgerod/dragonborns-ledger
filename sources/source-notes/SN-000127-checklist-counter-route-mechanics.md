## Evidence Summary

Counter trophies require explicit tracking because several are not simple parent-quest completions. `Sideways` should count only source-qualified side quests; `Hero of the People` should count distinct miscellaneous objectives; `Delver` and `Explorer` need observed clear/discovery counters; `Reader` needs different skill-book titles rather than duplicate copies; `Thief` has separate lock and pocket halves; and `Snake Tongue` needs one successful persuade, bribe, and intimidate action.

The Thieves Guild faction page gives two separate side-job boundaries: five jobs in each of Markarth, Solitude, Whiterun, and Windhelm plus their special jobs for restoration, and display/safe thresholds through 125 Delvin/Vex jobs. It also warns that Riften does not advance restoration, Raven Rock has no reputation quest, Raven Rock Bedlam is bugged, and some assignment/reroll methods can leave failed journal entries or persistent generated items. TB-031F therefore uses a clean assignment-save/reload policy for city filtering and a separate total-job counter.

The Dawnguard sources make `Lost Relic` a three-completion finite chain, but the filler radiants between relics are semi-random. TB-031F therefore requires recording every filler and taking `Lost Relic` whenever offered until all three rune artifacts are obtained, rather than inventing a fixed filler count.

The Fishing activity page makes fishing species route-sensitive: catches depend on fishing spots, rods, biome, and weather, and some rare fish require special rods. TB-031F therefore treats Fishing as a structured species/quest sweep rather than incidental casts during travel.

`Rebuilding the Blades` can recruit three followers and then unlock `Dragon Hunting`, but the same source notes that Esbern will not finish the quest or start Dragon Hunting once the Paarthurnax conflict blocks Blades support until Paarthurnax is slain. Because the canonical route preserves Paarthurnax, these checklist rows belong to the compact Paarthurnax/Blades branch, not the main continuity.

Angi's page supports the checklist's `Archery Practice` row as a real source-backed activity: Angi provides six Archery increases through practice rounds and gives Angi's Bow after completing the regimen. `Hired Muscle` supports the checklist's `Scare My Enemy` row as a Companions brawl/intimidation radiant and carries bug/target cautions, so it maps to the existing representative Hired Muscle objective rather than a new objective.

The Quest Items table lists Firebrand Wine Case at the Blue Palace for `Scoundrel's Folly` and Map of Dragon Burials at the Sleeping Giant Inn for `A Blade in the Dark`. TB-031F promotes those checklist rows as parent-quest quest-item checklist cues; final route QA still needs to verify whether the item is retained, turned in, or only checked at acquisition/use.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the counter boundaries, Blades branch classification, Angi/Hired Muscle mapping, Firebrand Wine Case, and Map of Dragon Burials source-readiness resolutions.
