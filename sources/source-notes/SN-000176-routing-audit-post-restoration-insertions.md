## Route Decisions

The Thieves Guild restoration block supplies two route enablers that earlier sections did not have: a large ordinary-money runway from the 125-job counter and the strongest Amulet of Articulation. The audit therefore moves several staged objectives forward before the Civil War branch instead of leaving them for a vague later cleanup.

Breezehome and Tundra Homestead are purchased after `Under New Management`, the 125 Delvin/Vex side-job total, and Shadowfoot ownership. Earlier sections correctly avoided assuming 5,000 or 7,500 gold; this point no longer has that problem because the guide has just routed a long money-generating Thieves Guild block and restored fences. Breezehome handles the base-game city-house/Citizen surface, while Tundra becomes the selected central home. Family moves and display placement still wait for the later homes/display section.

`Smith 'N Slash` is no longer allowed to remain only conditional on Siddgeir assigning Embershard Mine. That conditional preserved the early cave state correctly, but all saves still need the Orcish Plate quest and Embershard clear eventually. The audit places it in the Whiterun return unless the player already completed it through the Falkreath target branch.

`The Staff of Hasedoki` is now route-ready as a full Whiterun-western-road and Brittleshin bundle. The guide reads `Smuggler's Trade Notes`, handles Modig's ledger, clears Brittleshin through the Staff stage, preserves the staff, and reveals both Brittleshin entrances. The nearby Saints & Seducers Elytra/Saints camp is still not pulled into this step because that is a separate bug-marked Creation chain and pet route, not a directly colocated Staff objective.

`Missing In Action` is routed after the Amulet/Thieves block but before the Civil War hard-save branch. The guide uses the Jon Battle-Born `Letter from Olfina` path so the document row is covered without trespassing in House Battle-Born, then sends the player to Northwatch Keep as a direct rescue. This avoids the page's incomplete peaceful-release route and keeps Northwatch's clear/location work with the Thorald rescue.

`Onmund's Request` is routed in the same follow-up return because the source specifically allows a sufficiently high Speech or Amulet of Articulation persuasion route. This avoids adding a random Grand Staff of Charming dungeon assignment after the College and Thieves target pools have already been managed.

`The Blessings of Nature` is not routed before the Civil War because the source records a tree-state bug when Battle for Whiterun is completed after the quest. The audit therefore places the full Danica, Nettlebane, Maurice, and Eldergleam route after the canonical Imperial Civil War. The sapling path is selected because it preserves Maurice and the sanctuary NPCs and still completes the quest; no full branch is needed for this isolated outcome choice under the current branch policy. Nearby skill-book candidates around Orphan Rock and Eldergleam are not pulled into the player-facing escort leg because they remain under the Scholar's Insight read-timing policy and are not required to complete the Danica/Maurice route.

## Coverage Summary

This audit directly places Breezehome ownership/furnishings, Tundra Homestead discovery/ownership, `Citizen`, conditional-or-final `Smith 'N Slash`, Embershard Mine clearing, Guard's Dossier: Yakhtu gra-Orkulg, Yakhtu's Journal, Orcish Plate armor, `The Staff of Hasedoki`, Smuggler's Trade Notes, Staff of Hasedoki, Brittleshin Pass clearing, both Brittleshin entrance markers, the linked AE Staff books, `Missing In Action`, Letter from Olfina, Imperial Missive, Northwatch Keep, `Onmund's Request`, Onmund follower/marriage option, `The Blessings of Nature`, Orphan Rock, Nettlebane, Eldergleam Sanctuary, and the sapling outcome.

Rows still intentionally held: Tundra/Breezehome display placement and household moves remain with the later homes/display section; Tundra, dungeon, Northwatch, Orphan Rock, and Eldergleam-area skill books remain closed or unrouted for the Scholar's Insight window; Saints & Seducers/My Pet Elytra remains with its own Creation route. The former `Gift of Kynareth` Survival conflict is now resolved in the Imperial Civil War source note through the user-approved temporary Survival Mode exception.
