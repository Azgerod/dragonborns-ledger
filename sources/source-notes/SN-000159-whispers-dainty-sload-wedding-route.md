# SN-000159 - Whispers, Dainty Sload, And Wedding Route

Status: route-writing source note for TB-035-MR-030B.

## Scope

This note supports the v1 guide section `Whispers, Solitude Jobs, And The Wedding Assassination`. The pass routes `Whispers in the Dark`, the required five Solitude Thieves Guild jobs, `The Dainty Sload`, `Kill Lurbuk`, `Kill Hern`, `The Silence Has Been Broken`, Volunruud/`Silenced Tongues`, Eduj, Okin, Dragon Priest Dagger, Aura Whisper: Nir, `Bound Until Death`, Firiniel's End, Vittoria's wedding gear, and Summon Spectral Assassin.

The old broad Brotherhood continuation scaffold is split here. Shadowmere, Olava's Token, `Locate the Assassin of Old`, Cicero's later state, Dawnstar Sanctuary, `Hail Sithis!`, sanctuary restoration, torture-room prisoners, and the post-finale representative contract remain in the next Dark Brotherhood section.

## Route Decisions

`Whispers in the Dark` is completed at the start of the section because UESP records that the quest completes when the player takes Nazir's next side contract after the Night Mother scene. The route accepts both Hern and Lurbuk immediately, then pauses the Brotherhood line for the required Solitude Thieves Guild work before the wedding.

`The Dainty Sload` is moved forward despite the general pre-Nightingale Delvin/Vex job hold. UESP records that the Solitude special job requires five Solitude side jobs, and project constraint tables require Erikur to remain alive and reachable before `Bound Until Death`. The route therefore creates a narrow controlled exception: five Solitude jobs are completed now, exactly enough to unlock `The Dainty Sload`, while Markarth, Whiterun, Windhelm, Fishing, Bedlam, all remaining city restoration, `Under New Management`, and the 125-job counter remain in the late Thieves block.

The Solitude job mix uses Numbers, Burglary, Sweep, Shill, and Heist. This avoids the Fishing pickpocket job for now, preserving the separate controlled pickpocket/Thief counter plan, and avoids Bedlam's broad stolen-value objective during this dependency patch. The guide uses save/reload assignment filtering only because this is a known repeatable counter system with city-targeting caveats; it is not a general instruction to manipulate ordinary radiant targets.

`The Dainty Sload` is routed as a single start-to-finish job after the five Solitude jobs. The guide uses hard saves before accepting the special job and before acquiring Balmora Blue because UESP records Erikur, Sabine, Balmora Blue, and quest-start bugs that are not recoverable with console commands on PS4. The route takes `Balmora Blue Note` from the Red Wave Safe, retrieves Balmora Blue from Sabine's under-dock footlocker, plants it aboard the Dainty Sload, takes the ship's Stone of Barenziah, leaves `A Dance in Fire, v6` closed for the Scholar's Insight window, and completes the special job with both Erikur and Delvin.

The second Nazir contract pair is routed after Dainty Sload. Lurbuk waits until `Laid to Rest` is complete so he is not standing outside Movarth's Lair, and Hern is killed through a hostile-dialogue setup so the player is not pushed toward murdering Hert or accepting a bounty. Both contracts are marked complete after reward turn-in to Nazir.

Volunruud is expanded because `The Silence Has Been Broken` sends the player there, and the local side quest, unique weapons, shout word, document, unique dagger, and location clear all belong with that visit. The guide reads `Heddic's Volunruud Notes`, speaks to Amaund, collects the ceremonial weapons, opens Elder's Cairn, lets Kvenel leave his throne before killing him, loots Eduj and Okin, takes the container copy of `Night Falls on Sentinel` without reading it, learns Aura Whisper: Nir, collects the Dragon Priest Dagger, and marks Volunruud clear. `Mace Etiquette` remains closed because it is a loose skill book and the route has not reached Scholar's Insight.

`The Silence Has Been Broken` is completed after Delvin appraises the Jeweled Amulet and gives the Letter of Credit. `Letter of Credit` and `Amaund Motierre's Sealed Letter` are routed as quest documents in guide prose even though the current checklist matrix does not expose separate checklist rows for both titles.

`Bound Until Death` is routed only after `The Spiced Wine`, the Bards College Solitude festival, and `The Dainty Sload` have all been handled. The route chooses Firiniel's End from Gabriella's sniper ledge rather than the loose gargoyle because UESP records body-clipping and bonus-award bugs with the gargoyle. The guide kills Vittoria during her balcony speech, loots her wedding outfit and Vittoria's Wedding Band immediately or from Solitude Catacombs if the body disappears, and does not kill Asgeir for his wedding band. UESP records that Asgeir's band remains available from him after the wedding if he survives, so it is safer to place that row in the later controlled pickpocket pass.

Olava's Token is not a `Bound Until Death` reward. UESP's quest-item table associates it with the later `Breaching Security` bonus path, so `Locate the Assassin of Old`, the Ancient Shrouded set, and the token are deferred to the next Dark Brotherhood section.
