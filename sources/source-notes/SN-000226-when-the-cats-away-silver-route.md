# When the Cat's Away And Silver Armor Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000226

## Claim

`When the Cat's Away` should be routed in the existing post-Silent Moons Whiterun to Rorikstead/Goldenhills pass. The route reads and takes `M'Sharra's Diary` in the Bannered Mare backroom, reads and takes `M'Sharra's Confession` and `M'Sharra's Key` near the Dragonsreach Dungeon evidence chest, then opens the Lund's Hut chest during the already-routed western Whiterun road loop to acquire `The Crimson Dirks, v2` and the full Silver armor set. The player then exits the hut and defeats the post-chest Imperial Captain and East Empire Wardens.

## Routing Relevance

The first Whiterun city loop deliberately skipped the Bannered Mare trigger diary because the route was still stabilizing Survival support, gold, and early Whiterun services. The later Goldenhills/Rorikstead section starts in Whiterun, reads another Bannered Mare Creation note, travels to Rorikstead, and already discovers Lund's Hut during western-road cleanup. Starting and closing `When the Cat's Away` there avoids a standalone Whiterun/Rorikstead trip and closes the Silver Armor parent-set and linked document gaps.

The route uses a rotating manual save before the Dragonsreach Dungeon pickup because UESP records a confirmed bug where `M'Sharra's Confession` may spawn under the floor. The player-facing guide does not add console or wall-clip recovery instructions; the save is a narrow route-control precaution for the normal intended pickup.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's quest page starts `When the Cat's Away` from `M'Sharra's Diary` in the Bannered Mare, sends the player to `M'Sharra's Confession` near the Dragonsreach Dungeon evidence chest, and then sends the player to Lund's Hut. The quest reward is a full Silver armor set plus `The Crimson Dirks, v2`; after the chest pickup, an Imperial Captain and two East Empire Wardens attack outside.

The individual UESP book pages place `M'Sharra's Diary` on a backroom table behind the Bannered Mare kitchen, `M'Sharra's Confession` hidden behind the Dragonsreach Dungeon evidence chest, and `The Crimson Dirks, v2` inside a chest at Lund's Hut. The Lund's Hut page records that the Anniversary Edition chest requires a key and cannot be picked. Because UESP does not identify the key's exact pickup location, the route uses the community wiki as secondary support for taking the key beside the Confession note.

The Silver page confirms Silver Armor is a heavy armor set added by the Creation. A full set comes from `When the Cat's Away`; the set has four named pieces: `Silver Armor`, `Silver Boots`, `Silver Gauntlets`, and `Silver Helmet`; it has no shield. The route preserves all four quest pieces and does not depend on the level-10 random-loot appearance or the Advanced Armors crafting route.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the quest start, Confession location, Lund's Hut armor chest, `The Crimson Dirks, v2`, Silver armor-piece membership, Lund's Hut route placement, and the hostile post-chest encounter.

Confidence is medium for the exact `M'Sharra's Key` pickup location because the executable route needs the key, UESP confirms the Anniversary Edition chest key requirement, and lower-priority secondary support identifies the key as next to the Confession note. The route therefore names the key pickup but records the source-priority caveat here.

The main unresolved risk is the confirmed `M'Sharra's Confession` spawn-under-floor bug. This is a PS4-relevant risk because the documented console fallback is unavailable; the guide uses a rotating manual save before entering the dungeon as the route-control mitigation and avoids routing the quest earlier than necessary.
