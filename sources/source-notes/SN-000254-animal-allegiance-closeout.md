## Route Decisions

Animal Allegiance has three source-listed word walls: Raan at Ancient's Ascent, Mir at Angarvunde, and Tah at Ysgramor's Tomb. The current guide already routes Angarvunde during the Rift road pass and Ysgramor's Tomb during `Glory of the Dead`; the unresolved gap was that the Falkreath pine-forest loop cleared Ancient's Ascent without explicitly telling the player to use its word wall.

The guide now treats Bonechill Passage and Ancient's Ascent as one local climb in the Falkreath loop. At Ancient's Ascent, the player defeats the dragon, lets any soul absorption settle, learns Animal Allegiance: Raan from the word wall, and clears the lair. This is the earliest natural route point for Raan because Ancient's Ascent is already in the warm Falkreath support loop and does not require pulling a new high-risk detour into the opening Survival route.

Ysgramor's Tomb stays on the normal Companions finale route. UESP notes that its word wall can be reached by mountain climbing, but the guide does not use that bypass because `Glory of the Dead` is already required and gives a cleaner, source-backed quest entry to the tomb.

Learning the words closes the shout objective and the three checklist word rows. Unlocking or using the shout still depends on the route's dragon-soul pool and global shout mechanics; this closeout does not create a separate dragon-soul grind.

## Coverage Summary

This pass closes `OBJ-000761` by making Ancient's Ascent explicitly teach Animal Allegiance: Raan in the Falkreath loop and by recognizing the already-routed Angarvunde Mir and Ysgramor's Tomb Tah words. It also updates Ancient's Ascent support data with the individual source page and the word-wall role.
