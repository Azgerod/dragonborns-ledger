# Legends Lost Sunder Wraithguard Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000247

## Claim

`Legends Lost` should be completed as a late high-level mainland route immediately after Black Book default maintenance sets `Scholar's Insight` active.

For this route:

- Start the Creation from `Lost Caravan Guard's Note` in Windhelm's New Gnisis Cornerclub.
- Follow the source-listed caravan-note chain in order: north of Bronze Water Cave, east of Loreius Farm, Whiterun Hall of the Dead, and northwest of Peak's Shade Tower.
- Take `Carriage Driver's Note` before reading it from inventory, and take `Velyne's Letter` before reading it, because the quest page records advancement bugs for both notes.
- Take the Attunement Crystal from Velyne's skeleton, then complete Sightless Pit and the Sightless Vault in one continuous pass.
- Claim both Sunder and Wraithguard after defeating the Messenger, then preserve both artifacts in owned storage.
- Read the selected `The Doors of Oblivion` skill-book copy on the skeleton southwest of Sightless Pit while `Scholar's Insight` is active.

## Routing Relevance

This closes `OBJ-000506` `Sunder & Wraithguard`, `OBJ-000650` `Legends Lost`, `CHK-QUESTS-0618`, `OBJ-000747` `Sunder and Wraithguard Artifact Parent Set`, `OBJ-001375` `Caravan Captain's Note`, `OBJ-001376` `Carriage Driver's Note`, `OBJ-001451` `Lost Caravan Guard's Note`, `OBJ-001500` `Seedy Guard's Note`, `OBJ-001542` `Velyne's Letter`, `ITEM-000807`, `ITEM-000808`, and `ITEM-000809`.

The route is deliberately late because Sightless Pit is a one-way Falmer dungeon ending in a Dwemer-vault centurion fight, the first caravan is adjacent to the Saints and Seducers Svarig camp, and Wraithguard has extra route value after Keening is already acquired. Placing it after Black Book default maintenance also lets the nearby `The Doors of Oblivion` selected skill-book copy be read under `Scholar's Insight`.

No branch route is needed. Sunder and Wraithguard are both required final artifacts from the single main quest outcome.

## Evidence Summary

The quest page identifies the start as reading `Lost Caravan Guard's Note` in New Gnisis Cornerclub and lists the reward as Sunder and Wraithguard. Its walkthrough sends the player from Windhelm to the caravan north of Bronze Water Cave, the caravan east of Loreius Farm, Whiterun's Hall of the Dead, the caravan northwest of Peak's Shade Tower, and finally Sightless Pit/Sightless Vault.

The Creation page confirms Sunder & Wraithguard includes the five route documents, the Attunement Crystal, Sunder, Wraithguard, Sightless Vault, and `Legends Lost`. It also records that the Bronze Water caravan can be encountered before the quest because it overlaps a Saints and Seducers location, but the Carriage Driver's Note chest does not appear until `Legends Lost` has begun. The route therefore avoids early accidental handling and places the full route after the Saints/Seducers Svarig camp is already cleared.

The quest and Sightless Pit pages both describe using the Attunement Crystal near the Sightless Pit exit/Abandoned Cave to enter Sightless Vault. Both pages describe the vault valve puzzles, Dwemer spider/sphere fights, the Messenger centurion, and the final Sunder/Wraithguard gates opening after the Messenger is defeated.

The quest page records several route-relevant bugs. `Carriage Driver's Note` can fail to advance the quest if it is read before it is taken, so the guide takes it first and reads it from inventory. `Velyne's Letter` can leave the route stuck on a read objective if it is read before pickup, so the guide takes it first. The Attunement Crystal can be lost if the player leaves after placing it without finishing the vault puzzle, so the guide makes a hard save before Sightless Pit and completes the dungeon/vault in one pass. The Messenger can fail to respawn if only one artifact is obtained and the quest is received again, so the guide takes both Sunder and Wraithguard before leaving.

The Sunder page identifies Sunder as a one-handed mace acquired from the Sightless Vault during `Legends Lost`, weight 18, with elemental and stamina/magicka damage, and with extra benefit while Wraithguard is equipped. The Wraithguard page identifies Wraithguard as heavy gauntlets acquired by completing `Legends Lost`, weight 8, warmth 13, with multiple resistances and extra benefits for Keening and Sunder. This supports preserving both artifacts and placing the route after Keening has already been acquired through Arniel.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the quest start, note chain, item acquisition boundary, note-order bug mitigations, one-pass Sightless Vault requirement, and artifact preservation policy.

The source pages do not identify a hard level gate. The guide still routes the quest late because the one-way dungeon, Falmer/Dwemer combat, Messenger fight, Saints/Seducers overlap, and Keening/Scholar's Insight synergies make late placement safer and more efficient for Survival/Legendary play.
