Status: needs review.

## Claim

UESP's Miscellaneous Quests page lists Open World and Eastmarch miscellaneous objectives and favors, including several radiant favor types and several named one-off objectives. This pass covers only base-game Open World and Eastmarch entries that are not already represented by prior faction, Bards College, side-quest, Hearthfire, property, or Thane rows.

## Routing Relevance

The guide specification requires all finite miscellaneous quests and favors that are reasonably trackable. This note supports objective database inventory rows for the first miscellaneous/favor slice; exact targets, rewards, NPC-dependency risks, and route placement remain deferred.

## Evidence Summary

UESP describes miscellaneous quests as minor quests listed together in the journal's Miscellaneous section, with only the current objective visible. The page also states that many miscellaneous quests use the Radiant quest system and that the page does not include all possible miscellaneous quests.

For this slice, the Open World base-game entries are `Amulet of the Moon` and `The Forgemaster's Fingers`; the Hearthfire spouse kidnapping entry is deferred to Hearthfire. The Eastmarch entries recorded here are the outside-town objectives from Darkwater Crossing and Kynesgrove plus Windhelm objectives such as delivery favors, item retrieval favors, `Extracting an Argonian`, `Find the Thalmor Assassin`, `That Was Always There`, `Harsh Master`, and other Windhelm favor rows. Thane and property rows are deferred to relationship/property passes, and Bards College instrument rows were already entered.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the inventory and broad row eligibility. The radiant target pool, first-visit availability risks, NPC survival issues, reward handling, and whether any row should become representative-only require later radiant-boundary, NPC-dependency, and route validation.
