# Source Note: Miscellaneous Reach, Rift, Whiterun, and Winterhold Inventory

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000024

## Claim

UESP's Miscellaneous Quests page lists the remaining base-game hold and city miscellaneous objectives for the Reach, the Rift, Whiterun Hold, and Winterhold. This pass records objective-bearing base-game rows from those sections and defers activities with no displayed objective, tutorials, Thane/property rows, Hearthfire/Dawnguard rows, and later DLC sections.

## Routing Relevance

The guide specification requires all finite miscellaneous quests and favors that are reasonably trackable. This note supports objective database inventory rows for the final base-game hold/city slice before separate bounty, dungeon-miscellaneous, tutorial, and leftover-reconciliation passes.

## Evidence Summary

UESP lists Reach entries for Old Hroldan, Orc stronghold delivery, Kolskeggr Mine, Soljund's Sinkhole, Rogatus's delivery, Karthwasten's Sanuarach Mine dispute, and Markarth objectives from Banning, Lisbet, Kerah, Degaine, Bothela, Omluag, Calcelmo, Ghorza, Igmund, Moth, and Ondolemar. Duplicate table appearances for Kolskeggr Mine and Rogatus's Letter are represented once.

UESP lists Rift entries for Sarethi Farm, Merryfair Farm, Heartwood Mill, Ivarstead, Riften, and Shor's Stone. This includes named objectives such as `Smooth Jazbay`, `Bow to the Master`, `Flight or Fight`, `The Straw that Broke`, `Climb the Steps`, `Grin and Bear It`, `Lifting the Shroud`, `Grimsever's Return`, `Few and Far Between`, the Riften skooma chain, and Shor's Stone delivery and mine objectives. Thane/property, marriage, adoption, Dawnguard surgery, and activity rows are deferred to their appropriate later passes.

UESP lists Whiterun Hold entries including Arngeir's `The Words of Power`, Riverwood's `A Lovely Letter`, Rorikstead's `Erik the Slayer`, and Whiterun objectives for Amren, Carlotta, Farengar, Andurs, Lars, Brenuin, Adrianne, and Ysolda. Tutorial rows in Riverwood and Whiterun are deferred to the tutorial inventory pass.

UESP lists Winterhold entries for `Drowned Sorrows`, Haran's persuasion favor, Korir's Helm of Winterhold retrieval, and Malur's theft favor. Thane rows are deferred to relationship and property passes.

Activity/favor rows with no listed objective in these sections, such as chopping wood, mining ore, gathering wheat, beggar gifts, drunk gifts, and generic brawls, were reviewed in SN-000026. Their relationship, marriage, training, investment, property, or checklist implications are deferred to later passes rather than entered here as standalone objective rows.
