# Source Note: Bounty, Dungeon Miscellaneous, and Tutorial Inventory

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000025

## Claim

UESP lists four base-game bounty quest types, twelve base-game dungeon miscellaneous quests, and three crafting tutorial quests from the pages linked by the Miscellaneous Quests page. This pass records those inventory rows without selecting final route timing, exact bounty targets, dungeon order, or tutorial givers.

## Routing Relevance

The guide specification requires all finite miscellaneous quests and favors that are reasonably trackable, plus bounded representative completions of meaningful radiant types. This note supports a bounded bounty-type inventory, the remaining dungeon miscellaneous inventory, and the three crafting tutorial objective rows.

## Evidence Summary

UESP lists four bounty quest types: `Bounty: Bandit Boss`, `Bounty: Dragon`, `Bounty: Forsworn`, and `Bounty: Giant`. It describes bounty quests as repeatable with limits, notes that only one of each type can be active at once from separate holds, and gives routing-relevant gates: giant bounties require level 20, dragon bounties require `Dragon Rising`, and Forsworn bounties are Reach-exclusive. It also records bugs and edge cases around bounty availability, Jarl exile, dragon souls, and non-respawning dungeon targets.

UESP's Dungeon Quests page separates dungeon side quests from dungeon miscellaneous quests. The side-quest entries were already represented in the side-quest inventory where applicable, including `The Lost Expedition`; this pass records the dungeon miscellaneous section: `Angarvunde`, `Ansilvund`, `The Affairs of Hagravens`, `Frostflow Abyss`, `Mistwatch`, `Moss Mother Cavern`, `Telrav's Request`, `Ragnvald`, `Rebel's Cairn`, `Sleeping Tree Cave`, `The Savior of Selveni Nethri`, and `Yngol's Barrow`.

UESP's Tutorials page lists three crafting tutorials: `Alchemy Tutorial`, `Blacksmithing Tutorial`, and `Enchanting Tutorial`. It describes them as available near the start from NPCs in Riverwood, Whiterun, and Falkreath, and notes that the tutorials do not provide an advantage in using the crafting skills.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for inventory coverage and broad classification. Later passes still need to select exact bounty representatives, validate whether any bounty type should be required beyond representative completion, validate individual dungeon objectives and rewards, decide whether `The Affairs of Hagravens` contributes to Sideways on the target platform, and choose whether tutorials have route value beyond completion accounting.
