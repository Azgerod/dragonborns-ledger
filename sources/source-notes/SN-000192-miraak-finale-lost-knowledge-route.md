# SN-000192 - Miraak Finale, Lost Knowledge, And Last Black Books Route

## Scope

This note supports the TB-035 expansion of `Miraak Finale, Lost Knowledge, And Last Black Books` in `drafts/final-guide/main-guide-v1.md`.

The pass completes the parked Storn handoff, the level-60 Miraak finale, the Waking Dreams chapter route, maximum-tier Miraak equipment, Root of Power, Neloth's remaining `Lost Knowledge` Black Book work, White Ridge Barrow, The Sallow Regent, Benkongerike, Untold Legends, the final East Empire Company Pendant, and `Pain in the Necklace`.

## Routing Decisions

- The section starts only at level 60+ because UESP records Miraak's Sword, Miraak's Staff, and the Miraak mask as leveled items with their final tier at level 60. The hard save before reading Waking Dreams protects that reward gate and the Waking Dreams pre-finale chapter pickups.
- The route now closes `Filial Bonds` before Storn. If `Torkild's Letter to Wulf` was acquired naturally during earlier Solstheim travel, it is turned in immediately; otherwise the guide runs an isolated search through the UESP-listed Torkild sites and then returns the letter to Wulf before `At the Summit of Apocrypha` starts.
- Waking Dreams is routed as a complete book route rather than a boss-only finale. UESP records required On Apocrypha books, Waking Dreams skill-book placements, the Chapter V pedestal solution, Chapter VI's Dragon Aspect word, and the fact that the first five chapters cannot be revisited after defeating Miraak.
- Sahrotaar is recorded as Dragonrider 1 of 5 because the quest requires using full Bend Will to tame and ride him. Later Dragonrider rides should still be routed as controlled ride attempts instead of using random convenience travel.
- Waking Dreams reset portals are unlocked after Miraak, but the guide does not spend dragon souls on resets yet. Those resets remain a controlled late-progression tool.
- Root of Power is routed immediately after the finale because `At the Summit of Apocrypha` frees the Tree Stone. This closes the held Tree Stone power without creating a separate later All-Maker Stone fragment.
- Morwen's Bera necklace favor is closed on the Skaal Village visit before Storn. The quest had already been started and delivered during the Falkreath Bittercup trip, so this is the earliest natural Solstheim return point for the reward and Morwen option unlock.
- `Lost Knowledge` is pulled immediately after `The Gardener of Men` and the Miraak finale because Neloth's lead becomes available at that state and White Ridge/Benkongerike were deliberately held for this quest. The guide uses the actual lead Neloth gives and routes only the target site before returning to him.
- Raven Rock Mine is already cleared from `The Final Descent` and `Black Book: The Winds of Change`, so any `Lost Knowledge` lead to Raven Rock Mine is handled as a direct return to the existing Black Book chamber rather than a new regional sweep.
- White Ridge Barrow is routed as one complete block: dungeon clear, Merilar key, Servos cage, one representative Mind Control Spider output at the Imbuing Chamber, Dukaan, Cyclone: Nos, The Sallow Regent, 2920 Second Seed, Detect Undead, Flame Thrall, and Seeker of Sorcery.
- Benkongerike is routed as one complete block: cave clear, Telekinesis, final East Empire Company Pendant, puzzle gate, Cyclone: Ven, Untold Legends, the four Chapter IV skill books, and Secret Servant.
- `Pain in the Necklace` closes immediately after the Benkongerike pendant because that is the final held pendant member.

## Source Corrections

The direct UESP shout pages contradict the earlier checklist-mapping word-wall notes used by the route scaffold:

- Dragon Aspect: Mul is in Waking Dreams, Qah is in Raven Rock Mine, and Diiv is in the Temple of Miraak.
- Cyclone: Ven is in Benkongerike, Gaar is in Kolbjorn Barrow, and Nos is in White Ridge Sanctum.

The guide and internal coverage tracker have been corrected to follow the direct UESP shout pages. The raw checklist mapping still contains the older word-wall descriptions and should be treated as a checklist-row label source rather than gameplay-routing authority for these six word locations.

## Deferred Or Unresolved

- `Note from Mogrul` remains unresolved from the earlier Tel Mithryn pass because the current source page has no fixed executable pickup path.
- The inherited Bittercup item rows for Potion of Blood, Ironwood Soup Elixir, and Mysterious Potion remain unresolved until a source identifies deterministic normal-play pickups or confirms exclusion.
- Highpoint Tower and `Old Friends` remain held for the later `Briarheart Necropsy`/Neloth follow-up because this section is about the post-Gardener Black Book lead pool, not all remaining Tel Mithryn radiants.
