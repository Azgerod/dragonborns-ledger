# The Arms of Chaos Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000227

## Claim

`The Arms of Chaos` should be routed across three already-open route surfaces: read/take `Loss of the Sea Stallion` and preserve the nearby waterbreathing support during the Windhelm East Empire Company visit; during the Winterhold northern coast sweep, dive at Pilgrim's Trench for `Master Ellane's Last Words` and the `Sovereign Band`, clear Skytemple Ruins, read/take `Hyenril's Journal`, take the unenchanted `Arm of the Moon`, unenchanted `Arm of the Sun`, and mundane `Warlock's Mark`, then use the eastern-shore altar to defeat the three Dremora and loot the Blue, Green, and Orange Sigil Stones; after the cold coast loop, use already-owned Myrwatch to craft the restored `Warlock's Mark`, `Arm of the Moon`, and `Arm of the Sun`, complete the quest, and preserve the final rewards.

## Routing Relevance

The route already enters the Windhelm East Empire Company office for `Rise in the East`, already discovers Pilgrim's Trench and clears Skytemple Ruins in the Winterhold coast sweep, and already owns Myrwatch with its Staff Enchanter and All Forge before this Winterhold section. Routing `The Arms of Chaos` there closes the high-severity quest row, the Arms of Chaos equipment parent set, `Hyenril's Journal`, `Loss of the Sea Stallion`, `Master Ellane's Last Words`, and the matching checklist rows without adding a standalone final-cleanup detour.

The route uses a rotating manual save before the Skytemple table because UESP records a confirmed bug where `Warlock's Mark` can glitch into the table. The PS4 route cannot use console recovery, so the guide makes the table pickup a source-backed route-control point. The route also completes the crafting at Myrwatch soon after the Dremora fight to avoid carrying weight-bearing quest-item versions of the staves and amulet deeper into the run.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's quest page starts `The Arms of Chaos` by reading `Hyenril's Journal` at Skytemple Ruins. The same Skytemple table has the unenchanted `Arm of the Moon` and `Arm of the Sun`; `Warlock's Mark` is hidden beneath the table and can be hard to see. The page then sends the player to the East Empire Company office in Windhelm for `Loss of the Sea Stallion`, then to Pilgrim's Trench for Ellane's coffin, `Master Ellane's Last Words`, and the `Sovereign Band`.

The quest page notes that the Windhelm visit can be skipped if the Pilgrim's Trench coffin was already looted. The route still reads and takes `Loss of the Sea Stallion` during the earlier Windhelm East Empire Company visit because it is a required AE quest document with a deterministic table source, but it uses the Pilgrim's Trench coffin route to make the Winterhold coast sequence executable without a fresh Windhelm backtrack.

After the ring is recovered, the quest sends the player to the eastern shore of the Skytemple Ruins island. Activating the altar summons three Dremora in sequence: an ice mage carrying the Blue Sigil Stone, a shock mage carrying the Green Sigil Stone, and a fire warrior carrying the Orange Sigil Stone. The quest page records that each Dremora also carries a Daedra Heart, and the Dremora Sovereign has a Daedric Sword of the Inferno at all levels.

The quest completes after the three restored items are crafted. The `Warlock's Mark` can be reforged at any forge or anvil, while the two staves require a Staff Enchanter. The Staff Enchanter page confirms the `Arm of the Moon` recipe uses the unenchanted Arm of the Moon and the Green Sigil Stone, and the `Arm of the Sun` recipe uses the unenchanted Arm of the Sun and the Orange Sigil Stone. The Arms of Chaos Creation page notes that Myrwatch has both the required Staff Enchanter and the All Forge, and the current route already owns Myrwatch before this pass.

The item page confirms the final reward set includes `Sovereign Band`, restored `Warlock's Mark`, restored staff `Arm of the Moon`, restored staff `Arm of the Sun`, and the intermediate/quest-component forms of both staves, `Warlock's Mark`, and the Sigil Stones. The route treats internal nonplayable duplicates as audit-only rows, routes the intermediate pieces as acquired quest components consumed or transformed by the crafting step, and preserves the final two staves, final amulet, and ring.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the quest start, document locations, Pilgrim's Trench coffin route, Skytemple artifact pickup, Dremora order, Sigil Stone acquisition, Staff Enchanter recipes, Myrwatch crafting surface, and final reward preservation.

The main PS4-relevant risk is the confirmed `Warlock's Mark` table-glitch bug. The guide mitigates it with a rotating save before the table pickup and a normal reload if the amulet cannot be targeted cleanly. The UESP-listed message-swap bug for crafting the two staves is only a display issue and does not change route instructions. The older Myrwatch Staff Enchanter experience bug is not used as an exploit route reason; the route uses Myrwatch because it is already owned and has both required crafting surfaces.
