# The Lost Paladin and Chrysamere Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000229

## Claim

`The Lost Paladin` should be completed immediately before the existing Forelhost dragon-priest route. The guide should make `HARD SAVE: HS-CHRYSAMERE-FORELHOST`, avoid speaking to Captain Valmir first, climb to the Forelhost Battlements by the exterior shortcut, kill the Lost Paladin at the word wall, take and preserve Chrysamere, learn Storm Call: Strun, and only then return to the courtyard to complete `Siege on the Dragon Cult` and the full Forelhost clear.

## Routing Relevance

The existing guide already clears Forelhost from the Rift side for `Siege on the Dragon Cult`, Rahgot, Dragon Priest Staff (Rahgot), the Glass Claw, local quest documents, and Storm Call: Strun. Chrysamere belongs in that same bundle, but not after the Valmir start.

UESP records that Anniversary Edition no longer exposes `The Lost Paladin` as a normal journal quest, but the reward remains obtainable by finding the artifact. UESP also records a confirmed Lost Paladin spawn bug tied to `Siege on the Dragon Cult`; if Siege has started, the ghost may fail to appear even after Siege is complete. The guide therefore claims Chrysamere first from the battlements shortcut and keeps a named save before the approach.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's `The Lost Paladin` quest page states that the quest does not appear in the journal in Anniversary Edition, but the player can still obtain the reward by finding the artifact. Its quick walkthrough sends the player to Forelhost, then has the player defeat the Lost Paladin and take Chrysamere.

The quest page places the Lost Paladin at the Forelhost Battlements in front of the word wall and says he wields Chrysamere. It also records that the player can usually reach the objective by navigating the dungeon, but may instead jump up the exterior outcropping and use Whirlwind Sprint to land on the battlements. The Forelhost page separately confirms that the word wall can be reached by mountain-climbing or Whirlwind Sprint, bypassing the dungeon.

The routing reason for the shortcut is the source-listed bug. UESP warns that starting `Siege on the Dragon Cult`, usually by speaking with Captain Valmir, can make the Lost Paladin fail to appear on the battlements, sometimes even after Siege is complete. The same page recommends avoiding Valmir and climbing to the battlements first. The Lost Paladin NPC page also records that the Paladin is not protected and may be killed early by a dragon, so the guide uses a named hard save before the exterior approach.

The Chrysamere Creation page says the Creation adds `The Lost Paladin`, sends the player to Forelhost to kill the Lost Paladin, and rewards the Chrysamere two-handed greatsword. The item page confirms Chrysamere is a greatsword artifact acquired through `The Lost Paladin` by claiming it from the Lost Paladin at Forelhost. It grants fire resistance, spell absorption, and slow combat health regeneration when equipped; tempering requires an Ebony Ingot, a Ruby, and Arcane Blacksmith. The item has no usage cost, so charges do not drain.

After Chrysamere is claimed, the existing Forelhost route remains valid: the Forelhost page lists `Siege on the Dragon Cult` and `The Lost Paladin` as related quests; lists Chrysamere, Dragon Priest Staff (Rahgot), Glass Claw, Rahgot, Flames, and `The Exodus` among the treasures; places the Lost Paladin in front of the word wall when the Creation is installed; and confirms the Glass Claw door code as Fox-Owl-Snake. The guide therefore treats the first battlements pass as the Chrysamere/Storm Call pickup and the second dungeon pass as the Siege/Rahgot/clearable-location route.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the non-journal AE quest state, Forelhost location, Lost Paladin target, Chrysamere reward, exterior shortcut, and preservation route.

The open operational risk is the source-listed Lost Paladin spawn bug and unprotected NPC state. The guide mitigates it by saving before Forelhost, avoiding Valmir until after Chrysamere is claimed, and reloading if the ghost is absent. Live PS4 testing should still verify that the battlements shortcut works reliably from the route's late-Rift state.
