# SN-000173 - Imperial Civil War Route

Status: route-writing source note for TB-035-MR-042.

## Scope

This note supports the v1 guide section `Imperial Civil War`. The section resumes the canonical save after the Stormcloak branch reload, resolves the Imperial-aligned `Battle of the Champions`, joins the Imperial Legion, completes the no-Season-Unending Imperial Civil War through `Battle for Windhelm`, then finishes Hjerim, Thane of Eastmarch, and Calder under the new Windhelm political state.

## Route Decisions

`Battle of the Champions` is routed before the Imperial oath because UESP gives a deterministic pre-faction start through `Battle of Champions` in The Drunken Huntsman. The guide chooses the Legion champion path using the Amulet of Articulation for the persuasion check, avoiding courier timing while still matching the project default of Imperial-aligned Civil War Champions handling. UESP and the item table support taking every item from the Imperial chest and looting the defeated Stormcloak champion, which covers both obtainable equipment sets. The unobtainable Imperial Dragon Cloak and Storm-Bear Cloak remain data-level exclusions, not player-facing objectives.

`Gift of Kynareth` is routed in this pass after the user approved the UESP Survival Mode workaround. The guide reads and takes `Letter to General Tullius` while already in Castle Dour, clears the Alik'r camp southeast of Fort Greymoor, loots Majid's key, the cursed Lord's Mail, `Majid - Please Read`, and the unique Redguard clothing set, then returns to Solitude. The only Survival Mode exception is outside the Temple of the Divines: disable Survival Mode, activate the Shrine of Kynareth to cleanse the armor, then re-enable Survival Mode immediately.

The Imperial Civil War is run before `Season Unending`. UESP's `Reunification of Skyrim` table shows that without treaty changes the required Imperial holds are The Pale, The Rift, Winterhold, and Eastmarch. Hjaalmarch, Falkreath, and the Reach are initially Imperial-controlled, so the Imperial versions of `The Battle for Fort Snowhawk`, `The Battle for Fort Neugrad`, and `The Battle for Fort Sungard` are not expected on this main route. Fort Greenwall is therefore the main-route War Hero fort.

The guide follows the source-backed reporting cadence: report to Tullius when the quest sends the player to Castle Dour before loading the next hold's Imperial camp, then report to Rikke at the assigned camp. This keeps the sequence legible and avoids routing nearby objective sweeps around temporary military targets.

Korvanjund is routed on main continuity during `The Jagged Crown`, so the guide now claims the Ebony Claw, Korvanjund discovery, and the Korvanjund Slow Time word here. The Fort Hraggstad copy of `The Gold Ribbon of Merit`, the Korvanjund copy of `Jornibret's Last Dance`, the Fort Kastav copy of `Warrior`, and the Fort Greenwall Captain's Quarters copy of `Mace Etiquette` are not read in this pass. Current progression selections prefer Angi's Camp for `The Gold Ribbon of Merit`, Autumnwatch Tower for `Jornibret's Last Dance`, and Fort Greenwall Captain's Quarters for the later post-Scholar's Insight `Mace Etiquette` read; the Civil War fort battle does not require entering Fort Greenwall Captain's Quarters.

Hjerim, Thane of Eastmarch, and Calder are routed immediately after `Battle for Windhelm` because their major prerequisites are now satisfied: `Blood on the Ice` was completed earlier, the Imperial route has installed Brunwulf, and the route has already completed more than five Eastmarch citizen helps. The guide buys Hjerim and the cleanup upgrade first because the Hjerim source warns that buying the living-room upgrade before cleanup can remove the cleanup option. The 22,250-gold preflight covers the maximum Hjerim purchase drain plus all listed upgrades, including the Hearthfire child's bedroom.

## Coverage Summary

This pass places the Imperial-aligned `Battle of the Champions`, `Battle of Champions`, Civil War Champions package and equipment parent, all obtainable Imperial Dragon and Storm-Bear set members, `Gift of Kynareth`, Lord's Mail package and armor state, `Letter to General Tullius`, `Majid - Please Read`, the Lord's Mail mercenary clothing/key rows, Imperial Introductions, `Joining the Legion`, `Get Outfitted`, the Imperial `Jagged Crown`, `Message to Whiterun`, `Battle for Whiterun`, `Report to the Jarl of Whiterun`, `Reunification of Skyrim`, `A False Front`, `The Battle for Fort Dunstad`, `Compelling Tribute`, `The Battle for Fort Greenwall`, `Rescue from Fort Kastav`, `The Battle for Fort Amol`, `Battle for Windhelm`, main-route War Hero and Hero of Skyrim state, Hjerim ownership/furnishings, Thane of Eastmarch, and Calder.

Rows intentionally not executed on the clean Imperial route: `The Battle for Fort Snowhawk (Imperial)`, `The Battle for Fort Neugrad`, and `The Battle for Fort Sungard (Imperial)` because the route avoids `Season Unending` before Civil War completion and those Imperial variants require treaty-state changes.

No TB-035-MR-042 `NEEDS ROUTE RESOLUTION` rows remain after the approved Survival Mode exception for `Gift of Kynareth`.
