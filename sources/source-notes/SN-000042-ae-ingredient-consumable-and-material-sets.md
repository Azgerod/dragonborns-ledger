Status: needs review.

## Claim

The AE Creation Club bundle adds source-listed ingredient, consumable, food, curio, and material sets that need parent objective coverage before item-by-item alchemy, cooking, fishing, and Survival Mode validation.

## Routing Relevance

These rows are parent coverage only: they preserve the existence of finite AE item sets without deciding exact merchant visits, fishing catches, cooking recipes, alchemy discovery order, or Survival Mode food policy.
