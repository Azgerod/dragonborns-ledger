# If I had a Hammer Stendarr Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000246

## Claim

`If I had a Hammer` should be completed during the late Markarth property and homes pass, immediately after Vlindrel Hall is owned and furnished.

For this route:

- Earlier Dwemer Museum visits should continue to leave Stendarr's Hammer in place because the hammer weighs 100 and would burden the Markarth road, Aetherium, and `Hard Answers` route segments.
- Calcelmo's existing museum access is enough for the route's planned museum entry; the source pages record that entering the museum without Calcelmo's permission and a key is trespassing.
- After Vlindrel Hall is bought and furnished, enter the Dwemer Museum, take Stendarr's Hammer from the southwest corner/stone display pedestal, and immediately store it in Vlindrel Hall or another owned home.
- The pickup itself is not theft, even though the quest description uses "steal"; the route still avoids carrying the 100-weight hammer until owned Markarth storage is available.
- Under Anniversary Edition, the quest may not appear in the journal; the completion boundary for guide/checklist purposes is the successful acquisition and preservation of Stendarr's Hammer.

## Routing Relevance

This closes `OBJ-000649` `If I had a Hammer`, `CHK-QUESTS-0581`, `OBJ-000746` `Stendarr's Hammer Unique Weapon`, and `ITEM-001098`.

The route belongs in `Homes, Household, Services, Pets, and Mounts` rather than the first Markarth or Aetherium passes because the guide owns and furnishes Vlindrel Hall there. That solves the Survival/carry problem without adding a special storage shuttle from the early Reach route or burdening the level-46 Thieves Guild stealth route through Calcelmo's Laboratory.

No branch route is needed. The Creation has a single unique weapon pickup and no alternate reward state.

## Evidence Summary

The quest page records that the quest does not appear in the journal under Anniversary Edition, but Stendarr's Hammer can still be acquired from the Dwemer Museum. It lists the core route as gaining museum entry and taking the hammer, with the quest reward as Stendarr's Hammer.

The quest and Creation pages both record that museum entry is illegal without Calcelmo's permission and a key. The Understone Keep page adds that the museum door is guarded, needs Calcelmo's permission and a key for legal entry, and has patrolling guards inside. The guide already gains Calcelmo access during the earlier Nchuand-Zel route and uses that same access for later museum passes.

The quest page and Creation page both record that taking Stendarr's Hammer itself is not theft. The quest page still describes the action as stealing the hammer and records a successful theft stage, so the guide treats the pickup as the completion boundary while avoiding a crime-state assumption.

The quest, item, and item-list pages all support the carry/logistics reason for late placement: Stendarr's Hammer weighs 100. The item page also notes that equipping it can slow the player and affect stamina while sprinting, so the guide preserves it as a unique weapon rather than making it a route combat tool.

The Creation and Understone Keep pages locate the hammer in the Dwemer Museum's southwest corner/stone display pedestal. The guide therefore places the pickup during the late Markarth property pass, immediately after Vlindrel Hall gives the player a legal nearby owned storage point.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the acquisition location, access condition, AE journal behavior, non-theft pickup behavior, weight, unique-weapon identity, and late storage placement.

The quest page records a bug where the hidden quest can remain incomplete even after the hammer is acquired. Because PS4 has no console recovery, the guide treats the visible execution boundary as acquiring and preserving the hammer and does not add player-facing console-style recovery prose.
