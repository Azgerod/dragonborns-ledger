## Route Decisions

The section begins only after level 46 because the project currently uses the conservative source-tier maximum for Nightingale Blade, Nightingale Bow, and Chillrend. The earlier route stopped after `Speaking With Silence`; `Hard Answers` must happen next, so the level-32 Nightingale Armor threshold was only a readiness checkpoint, not a real route point.

Calcelmo's Laboratory is routed as part of `Hard Answers` because the quest sends the player through the Dwemer Museum, laboratory, Wizard's Balcony, and tower. The guide therefore routes `Aicantar's Lab Journal`, the Spider Control Rod, and the Dwemer Puzzle Cube while the player is already there. It leaves `Twin Secrets` and `Chimarvamidium` closed because this pass is still before the Scholar's Insight skill-book reading window; the selected-copy table moves those two titles away from Calcelmo's lab/tower.

The guide does not manufacture `Buy Dwarven artifact` here. The quest depends on a purchased Dwarven artifact and courier timing, while this section already has a tight Thieves quest state. It stays staged for a later deliberate Calcelmo/vendor or Dwarven-artifact policy.

`Vald's Debt` is routed before Riftweald Manor because the source path gives peaceful access to Mercer's House Key and keeps the Riftweald back-yard approach aligned with `The Pursuit`. The guide enters through the top-ramp door, then routes `To The Owner`, `Many Thanks`, Mercer's Plans, the Bust of the Gray Fox, and level-46 Chillrend in the same pass. The Bust and the earlier Dwemer Puzzle Cube are turned in to Delvin before Nightingale Hall so larceny items are not allowed to pile up unnecessarily before the final Left Eye turn-in.

`Trinity Restored` is now safe to route because the section is well past the level-32 armor threshold and the prerequisite `Hard Answers`/`The Pursuit` order has been satisfied. The guide takes the full Nightingale Armor set, but leaves Nightingale Hall skill books closed because this is still not the skill-book reading pass and their selected copies are not needed here.

`Blindsighted` is routed immediately after `Trinity Restored` because it is the direct next quest, contains Irkngthand, the Skeleton Key, the Left and Right Eyes of the Falmer, `Thief's Last Words`, and the Nightingale Bow reward. Bronze Water Cave is treated as the exit only. The clearable cave row and `Beneath Bronze Waters` stay staged because the Fishing quest's journal/mechanism/spider state belongs to the later fishing route and has a source-backed dependency risk if the exterior ambush is touched early.

`Darkness Returns` is routed immediately after returning the Left Eye to Delvin. Twilight Sepulcher contains the Skeleton Key resolution, `Nystrom's Journal`, `Anders's Message`, and the Nightingale Agent power choice set. Agent of Shadow is the route default because it is useful, reversible by returning later, and does not require a branch save. Agent of Subterfuge and Agent of Strife remain available options if the route later changes the default.

The Skeleton Key is not preserved. It is returned permanently during `Darkness Returns` and does not count as a kept Daedric artifact for Daedric Influence or Oblivion Walker.

Delvin/Vex city jobs remain staged for the next section rather than being threaded into this chain. At this point the Nightingale/Skeleton Key arc is complete and the city-job restoration block can start with the right prerequisites, explicit job-type accounting, city totals, and the Amulet of Articulation endpoint.

## Coverage Summary

This pass places `Hard Answers`, Nightingale Blade, `Vald's Debt`, `The Pursuit`, Chillrend, `Trinity Restored`, the full Nightingale Armor set, Nightingale Hall, `Blindsighted`, Irkngthand, Skeleton Key custody, Left Eye of the Falmer, Right Eye of the Falmer, Nightingale Bow, Twilight Sepulcher, `Darkness Returns`, Agent of Shadow default selection, and Nocturnal artifact handling.

Colocated items placed in the same section: `Aicantar's Lab Journal`, Spider Control Rod, Dwemer Puzzle Cube, `To The Owner`, `Many Thanks`, Bust of the Gray Fox, `Thief's Last Words`, `Nystrom's Journal`, and `Anders's Message`.

Rows intentionally staged with concrete reasons: `Twin Secrets`, `Chimarvamidium`, `The Red Kitchen Reader`, `Purloined Shadows`, Bronze Water Cave clearing, `Beneath Bronze Waters`, Fisherman's Journal, Calcelmo's `Buy Dwarven artifact`, Calcelmo investment, Calcelmo vendor spell sources, and Delvin/Vex city jobs.

No TB-035-MR-039 unresolved route rows are introduced by this pass.
