## Evidence Summary

UESP's Bards College page lists `Tending the Flames` as the Bards College side quest and lists `Investigate the Bards College`, `Finn's Lute`, `Pantea's Flute`, and `Rjorn's Drum` as miscellaneous quests. The page notes that the college has no true questline, no separate game-stat category, and no achievements for entering or completing the college.

The quest pages identify `Tending the Flames` as starting from Viarmo in Solitude with Dead Men's Respite and the Blue Palace as major locations and Bards College membership, the Solitude Fire Festival, a Whirlwind Sprint word, and leveled gold as rewards. The instrument quests become available after `Tending the Flames` and reward one-point increases to all Stealth skills for Finn's Lute, all Magic skills for Pantea's Flute, and all Combat skills for Rjorn's Drum.
