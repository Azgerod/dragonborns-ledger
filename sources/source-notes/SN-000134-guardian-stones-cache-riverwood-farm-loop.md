# Guardian Stones Cache, Riverwood Spell, And Whiterun Farm Loop

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000134

## Claim

After `In My Time Of Need` and the first Whiterun horse, the route should return to the Guardian Stones/Riverwood corridor for the Treasure Map I camp and cache, use the Riverwood Trader's guaranteed `Spell Tome: Clairvoyance` instead of clearing Embershard Mine for the placed copy, then sweep the safe Whiterun farm-road markers and complete the representative crop-sale activity at Battle-Born Farm. Breezehome remains a fund target rather than a purchase in this section, and Embershard Mine should remain untouched until the later `Smith 'N Slash` / Orcish Plate / clearable-location bundle.

## Routing Relevance

Earlier guide drafts either skipped nearby objectives too defensively or risked pulling Embershard forward as an early generic mine. The current standard is a conscious nearby-objective audit: route safe colocated work, but keep related bundles together when partial routing would create later duplication, reward loss, power-curve pressure, or state uncertainty.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's `Treasure Map I` page places the map at an unmarked bandit camp just south of The Guardian Stones and describes the associated cache as inside the trunk of a fallen tree near Riverwood. That camp was intentionally held out of the immediate post-Helgen road because it is optional bandit combat before the first settlement support stop. After Riverwood/Whiterun support, the first horse, and a stable save loop, the camp and cache become a reasonable early objective bundle. Because the same camp contains a `Night Falls on Sentinel` skill-book copy, the player-facing guide gives a local warning to leave that book closed for the Scholar's Insight window.

UESP's Riverwood Trader page lists `Spell Tome: Clairvoyance` as a guaranteed standard-merchandise item. Since the route is already returning to Riverwood to resolve Treasure Map I, buying and reading this tome here is cleaner than using Embershard Mine's placed copy as the first Clairvoyance source.

UESP's Pelagia Farm, Chillfurrow Farm, and Battle-Born Farm pages identify three source-listed discoverable farm markers around Whiterun's southern/eastern farm roads. The route therefore discovers Pelagia and Chillfurrow as part of the same farm-road loop instead of leaving them as incidental road markers. UESP's Battle-Born Farm page identifies Alfhild as a buyer for leeks, gourds, and wheat, and UESP's `Gather Wheat` page explains that crop sales are direct dialogue rather than merchant barter. The route therefore uses gourds from Battle-Born's fields for the representative crop-sale activity.

UESP's Breezehome page confirms that Breezehome costs 5,000 gold after the purchase prerequisite is met. The route has not generated a deterministic 5,000-gold fund by this section without extra grind or exploit assumptions, so Breezehome remains a target rather than a player-facing purchase.

UESP's Embershard Mine page confirms the mine is clearable and contains a pickaxe, iron ore veins, a forge, `Spell Tome: Clairvoyance`, and `Light Armor Forging`. However, UESP's `Smith 'N Slash` page uses the same cave for the Orcish Plate Creation quest, notes Yakhtu as a tougher min-level-15 enemy, and warns that if the cave has already been cleared, the quest chief still appears but a full unenchanted Orcish Plate set is not guaranteed from the few spawned bandits. The route therefore does not mine or clear Embershard now merely to finish Hard Worker; the cave should be introduced later as a coherent quest, armor, skill-book, and clearable-location bundle.

The western-road nearby-objective audit checked Gjukar's Monument, Fort Greymoor, and Sleeping Tree Camp after the Saadia route raised the question. Gjukar's Monument is better kept with `The Book of Love` and its nearby skill-book/ore context. Fort Greymoor has Civil War garrison behavior, a non-clearable source state, and skill/spell-book support rows that should not be split into an incidental discovery. Sleeping Tree Camp is a giant/mammoth camp with a Ysolda-message quest and nearby skill-book puzzle/altar content; it is not appropriate as a first-horse western-road side stop.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for Treasure Map I timing, Riverwood Trader as the first Clairvoyance source, the Whiterun farm-road marker correction, Alfhild as the crop-sale target, and holding Embershard Mine out of this section. No MR-006 route-resolution notes remain open. Breezehome timing remains an economy target for later route work rather than a gameplay uncertainty.
