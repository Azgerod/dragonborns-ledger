## Route Decisions

This pass is deliberately not a full College cleanup. It routes the College work that is executable now and whose random or material boundaries can be controlled without weakening the route.

`Enchanting Pick-Up` is routed before the late Thieves Guild `Hard Answers` block. UESP records an Aicantar caveat after `Hard Answers`, so doing one representative Sergius pickup now is safer than waiting for Guild restoration. The guide treats the assigned city client as an isolated delivery errand rather than building a city sweep around the random target.

`Restocking Soul Gems` is routed now because it is a College-service request with no travel target. The randomized type/amount is handled by telling the player to satisfy Sergius's actual empty-gem request.

`Aftershock` is routed now because the player is Arch-Mage after `The Eye of Magnus`, and Tolfdir's rupture target is an outdoor isolated assignment. The guide sends the player directly to the actual rupture and back to Tolfdir.

`Arniel's Endeavor` is routed now rather than later because the remaining stages are already unlocked after the College main quest and additional broad dungeon clearing only increases target-pool risk for Staff of Tandil and the courier stage. The guide uses the project's random-assignment convention: accept the actual target, complete only that assignment, and return to the College route. The convector route uses already-open Mzulft/Dwarven Storeroom support and the Ruins of Bthalft overlap.

The Aetherium Forge branch moves earlier from the old Dawnguard bucket. All four shards are already collected, and the Ruins of Bthalft convector is on the Arniel route. There is no Dawnguard main-quest dependency for the Forge. The guide therefore makes `HS-AETHERIUM-FORGE`, records the Staff and Shield branch outcomes via reload, and keeps the Aetherial Crown on the canonical save.

Taron Dreth remains a natural-window random encounter rather than a forced roam. The guide tells the player to carry the Aetherial Crown and take Taron Dreth's Robes if he confronts the player after `Lost to the Ages`.

`Discerning the Transmundane` is started at Septimus Signus's Outpost because the route is in the Winterhold/College loop and the next main-quest block will soon need the Elder Scroll/Blackreach path. The guide takes only the Attunement Sphere and Blank Lexicon setup now. Blackreach, the Runed Lexicon, the blood samples, the Oghma Infinium, and Oghma read/use remain later because they belong with the Elder Scroll route and late progression math.

The Atronach Forge practical-system use is routed now because College/Midden access is stable, the recipe is deterministic, and the route can state exact ingredients. The player guide specifies a minimal Fire Salts recipe and the exact offering-box contents instead of asking for recipe-note collection or a broad forge experiment.

`Onmund's Request` remains held for after `Under New Management` and the Amulet of Articulation. UESP records a random Grand Staff of Charming target pool and a persuasion route; the upcoming Thieves restoration block can provide a clean persuasion aid and avoid adding a second College staff-retrieval target pool here.

Urag's `Fetch me that Book!` and `Shalidor's Insights` remain held. Both use very large dungeon target pools, and Shalidor's Insights can pick gated or awkward targets such as Rebel's Cairn, Snow Veil Sanctum, The Katariah, Ysgramor's Tomb, or Nchardak depending on route state. The route will handle them in a later controlled radiant block when more target gates are closed and when the player can treat the assignment as an isolated dungeon job.

The five master ritual spell quests remain fixed-late. The player-facing route has not yet established Alteration 90, Conjuration 90, Destruction 100, Illusion 100, or Restoration 90, and the final all-perks/progression math is not complete. Their quest books, reward spells, purchase-unlocked master tomes, and Sigil Stone remain assigned to the later skill/perk/spell route.

`Soran's Journal` and `Eranya's Research Notes` remain held in the Arcanaeum because their next meaningful work is the Rift Unicorn route and the Goldbrand/Sivdur route, not the current College service loop. The guide does not repeat local warnings about them in this section.

## Coverage Summary

This pass places one representative `Enchanting Pick-Up`, one `Restocking Soul Gems`, one `Aftershock`, the remaining routed stages of `Arniel's Endeavor`, `Arniel's Convection`, Keening, `Summon Arniel's Shade`, the Aetherium Forge branch save, Aetherial Staff branch, Aetherial Shield branch, Aetherial Crown main outcome, `Lost to the Ages` completion, `Discerning the Transmundane` setup through Attunement Sphere and Blank Lexicon, and the Atronach Forge Fire Salts practical output.

Rows intentionally staged with concrete reasons: `Onmund's Request`, `Fetch me that Book!`, `Shalidor's Insights`, all five master ritual spell quests and their linked books/spells, Blackreach/Elder Scroll/Runed Lexicon/blood/Oghma completion, Taron Dreth's Robes as a natural random-encounter window, `Creature of Legend`, `A Matter of Pride`, and broad College vendor tome buying.

No TB-035-MR-037A unresolved route rows are introduced by this pass, but Taron Dreth's Robes remains conditional on the recorded natural encounter window until a deterministic route is validated or the project approves a random-encounter policy.
