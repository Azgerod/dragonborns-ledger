## Placement Rule

The late `Location Counter Sweep` is not a valid player-facing dumping ground. Safe unfinished objectives must be moved backward into the earliest natural regional route point that can handle them without breaking quest chains, level gates, branch saves, Survival Mode constraints, random-assignment policy, or the Scholar's Insight skill-book policy.

The audit therefore moved or confirmed earlier placement for:

- Whiterun central and western road clearables around the Silent Moons, Goldenhills, and Whiterun support loops.
- Falkreath/Helgen/Southfringe objectives during the Southern Warm Corridor.
- Kyne's Sacred Trials, The Cursed Tribe, Forelhost, and southeast Rift/Dayspring clearables during the Rift road block.
- Valthume, Ragnvald, and remaining Reach road objectives after Peryite while Markarth/Old Hroldan support is active.
- Eastmarch road objectives, Hillgrund's Tomb, Lost Knife Hideout, Mistwatch, and A Night to Remember during the Windhelm/Eastmarch support block.
- Lights Out, Ahtar, Noster, and Haafingar cave/coast objectives during the Solitude/Haafingar blocks.
- Mehrunes' Razor, Kharjo's random retrieval, Mzinchaleft/Grimsever, Frostflow Lighthouse, Shearpoint, and Pale/Hjaalmarch cold-pass objectives during the Dawnstar/Pale blocks.
- Winterhold north-coast objectives and Yngol Barrow during the College/Winterhold block.
- Briarheart Necropsy and Old Friends during the Tel Mithryn block.

## Skill-Book Gate Handling

Several places are now cleared before Scholar's Insight is active. In those cases, the guide routes the location and leaves the selected skill-book copy closed in place. The post-Scholar's Insight book pass must return to the selected copy rather than pretending the book was read during the earlier location clear.

This affects at least the selected copies for `Vernaccus and Bourlor`, `The Warrior's Charge`, and `The Black Arts On Trial`, which are tracked in `data/constraints/progression-source-selections.csv`.

## Random Assignments

Random retrievals remain isolated. The route accepts Noster's, Kharjo's, and similar random assignments at natural hub visits, then sends the player directly to the assigned target and back unless the assigned target is already being routed in that same section. The guide does not build regional objective routing around a preferred random outcome.

## Remaining Late Work

The final reconciliation sections may still contain scaffolds, but they are not allowed to absorb ordinary safe regional objectives. Late placement is reserved for genuine gates such as Scholar's Insight reads, all-perks/level gates, final crafting/reconciliation systems, unresolved random encounters, and source-unresolved rows.
