## Evidence Summary

UESP's Pets of Skyrim quest page lists five pet rewards and their acquisition points: Hilda, Sweet Roll, Thistle, Arachnia, and Skritch. Separate UESP pages identify Dwarven Armored Mudcrab, Nix-Hound, and Bone Wolf as Creation Club pets obtained through their associated quests or purchases.

Saints & Seducers adds two caged Elytra Nymph pets, one Demented and one Manic, each with a source-listed acquisition quest. The My Pet Elytra pages also include bug notes, so the objective rows mark bug risk for later validation. Goblins adds Gogh, a recruitable Goblin companion, as a reward from Blue in the Face; Gogh is treated as a creature follower rather than a pet.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high that the finite pet/follower set above needs objective coverage. Exact route timing, pet waiting locations, teleport spell tracking, pet inventory/carrying use, bug mitigation, and whether any pet or follower should be temporarily used for Survival Mode logistics remain deferred.
