## Claim

UESP's Miscellaneous Quests page lists base-game miscellaneous objectives and favors for Falkreath Hold, Haafingar, Hjaalmarch, and the Pale. This pass covers those entries except for Hearthfire construction, Thane/property objectives, and Bards College instrument rows already represented elsewhere.

## Evidence Summary

UESP lists Falkreath Hold entries for Dengeir, Runil, Siddgeir, and Thadgeir, including `Dark Ancestor`, `Some Light Theft`, dungeon-delving and rare-gift favors, `Kill the Bandit Leader`, and a delivery objective. Hearthfire land/construction and Thane rows in this section are deferred to property and relationship passes.

UESP lists Haafingar entries for Dragon Bridge and Solitude, including `Dragon's Breath Mead`, Ahtar's bandit-leader favor, `No News is Good News`, Aldis's rare-gift favor, `Elisif's Tribute`, `The Spiced Wine`, Noster's dungeon-delving favor, Octieve's persuasion favor, Sorex's delivery, `Return to Grace`, Sybille's vampire-killing favor, and `Fit for a Jarl`. Bards College instrument rows in the same section were already entered.

UESP lists Hjaalmarch entries including Pactur's payment delivery, Falion's investigation objective, Gorm's delivery, Idgrod the Younger's delivery, and Lami's rare-gift favor. UESP lists the Pale entries including Aeri's delivery, Captain Wayfinder's `Salty Sea-Dogs`, Frida's dungeon-delving favor, Rustleif's rare-gift favor, and Skald's giant-killing favor. Hearthfire construction, Thane rows, and `Waking Nightmare` are deferred or already represented.
