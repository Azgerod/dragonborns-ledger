# Source Note: Dragonborn Main Quest Start and Inventory

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000031

## Claim

Dragonborn's main quest inventory consists of `Dragonborn`, `The Temple of Miraak`, `The Fate of the Skaal`, `Cleansing the Stones`, `The Path of Knowledge`, `The Gardener of Men`, and `At the Summit of Apocrypha`. Official Bethesda support identifies Windhelm docks and Gjalund Salt-Sage as the Dragonborn DLC access route; UESP documents the initial Dragonborn quest and its Solstheim locations.

## Routing Relevance

The final route needs a clean Solstheim entry rule and must not begin detailed Solstheim routing until the prerequisite and difficulty policy is settled. The main quest rows also carry several Dragonborn trophy and Black Book dependencies.

## Evidence Summary

Bethesda's support page directs players to the Windhelm docks and Gjalund Salt-Sage to start Dragonborn DLC access. UESP's Dragonborn quest inventory lists the seven main quests from `Dragonborn` through `At the Summit of Apocrypha`, with trophy markers on the relevant main-quest milestones. The initial quest page identifies the Northern Maiden, Raven Rock, the Earth Stone, and the Temple of Miraak as core initial locations.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the main quest inventory and official travel access. Exact route timing remains open: the guide still needs later validation for cultist-trigger timing, whether any early Solstheim visit is worthwhile under Survival Mode, and how Dragonborn main progression interacts with Black Book, Bend Will, and Stalhrim objectives.
