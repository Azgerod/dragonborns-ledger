# Nordic Jewelry Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000241

## Claim

The guide should route `Nordic Jewelry` during the first stabilized Raven Rock pass, after Severin Manor is secured and before the Frostmoon Crag werewolf-ring trip.

For this route:

- Buy one each of `Nordic Amulet`, `Nordic Circlet`, and `Nordic Ring` from Fethis Alor while using Raven Rock services. The quest starts when the first piece is obtained.
- Read the automatically added `Certificate of Authenticity`.
- Travel west from Raven Rock to Hugin Ice-Shaper's body in the ash wastes north of the Earth Stone.
- Defeat the two reavers at Hugin's body, loot the melee reaver's three enchanted Nordic jewelry pieces, read and take `Hugin Ice-Shaper's Notebook`, and take the nearby satchel materials.
- Return to the Raven Rock forge and craft one additional Nordic jewelry item to complete the quest.
- Preserve the certificate, notebook, three purchased base pieces, crafted item, and three reaver-looted enchanted pieces in owned storage.

The source-listed leveled enchanted suffix rows are treated as the health, magicka, and stamina Nordic jewelry families represented by the quest's reaver loot, not as a requirement to shop or reload for every level suffix.

## Routing Relevance

This closes `OBJ-000630` `Nordic Jewelry`, `OBJ-000709` `Nordic Jewelry Equipment and Crafting Set`, `OBJ-001378` `Certificate of Authenticity`, `OBJ-001421` `Hugin Ice-Shaper's Notebook`, checklist rows `CHK-QUESTS-0598` and `CHK-BOOKS-2488`, and item rows `ITEM-000708` through `ITEM-000728`.

It also updates the package parent `OBJ-000496` from an early-city hold to a controlled Raven Rock/Solstheim route. The route avoids incidental first-Whiterun purchase timing, uses a deterministic Solstheim support stop, and avoids optional RNG shopping for leveled enchantment suffixes.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's `Nordic Jewelry (quest)` page records the post-Anniversary Edition trigger: the quest begins the first time the player obtains a Nordic Amulet, Nordic Circlet, or Nordic Ring, and the `Certificate of Authenticity` is automatically added. The same page identifies merchants as a direct way to obtain the first piece, directs the player to Hugin Ice-Shaper west of Raven Rock in the ash wastes north of the Earth Stone, places two hostile reavers over the body, and says the melee reaver carries a leveled Nordic Amulet of Health, Nordic Circlet of Magicka, and Nordic Ring of Stamina. Reading Hugin's notebook teaches the crafting recipe, and crafting one Nordic jewelry item completes the quest. The guide buys one each of the three base pieces because the item page lists all three base apparel rows and the route is already using a Raven Rock general-goods merchant.

The individual Hugin and notebook pages confirm that Hugin is dead west of Raven Rock/north of the Earth Stone and that `Hugin Ice-Shaper's Notebook` is next to him. The certificate page confirms the note is a Nordic Jewelry quest document added on the first Nordic jewelry purchase. Fethis Alor's page identifies him as a Raven Rock general-goods merchant, while the Raven Rock page confirms the town has freely available crafting stations including a forge. These support starting and finishing the quest without moving the route away from its existing Raven Rock support loop.

The Nordic Jewelry Items page lists the three unenchanted apparel bases plus leveled enchanted health, magicka, and stamina variants. The quest page supplies one enchanted item from each family as deterministic reaver loot, but the source-listed suffixes are leveled variants of the same three enchanted families. The route therefore preserves one purchased copy of each base piece, one crafted quest-completion piece, and the three reaver-looted enchanted families, without requiring extra merchant RNG or reloading for every suffix tier.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the purchase start, certificate read, Hugin location, notebook read, satchel material pickup, forge craft, and quest completion boundary.

Confidence is high that the route should wait until Raven Rock is stable instead of starting in Whiterun: the project already held the first-city purchase to avoid incidental early AE routing, and the quest's required follow-up is immediately west of Raven Rock.

There is no remaining open route question for the Nordic Jewelry quest in the current guide. The only policy choice is item-family handling for the leveled enchanted variants, which this note records explicitly.
