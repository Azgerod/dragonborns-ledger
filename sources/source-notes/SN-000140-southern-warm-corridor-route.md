# Southern Warm Corridor Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000140

## Claim

The guide should use the southern warm corridor after the Falkreath/Lakeview/Glenmoril block to route the remaining safe Falkreath and Ivarstead-adjacent objectives that are naturally bundled: Dengeir's theft and vampire follow-up, Bloodlet Throne, Moss Mother Cavern and Valdr's Lucky Dagger, Pinewatch with its Stone of Barenziah, Pinewatch documents, and Curious Silver Mold, Lakeview Small House/steward/carriage progress when Lakeview is available, Ivarstead service favors, Narfi's pre-Dark-Brotherhood quest, `Lifting the Shroud`, Shroud Hearth Barrow, Oakflesh, and the first routed Kyne's Peace word. `Fan Favorite` is routed from Ivarstead only if Falkreath land is already settled; otherwise it remains held until the delayed Falkreath land condition is clear.

The nearby-objective audit also identifies two retrospective corrections: `Blacksmithing Tutorial` belongs in the first Riverwood visit with Alvor, and `Alchemy Tutorial` belongs in the first Whiterun visit with Arcadia. They should not be artificially delayed to this later Falkreath corridor.

## Routing Relevance

This section is the first large warm-region cleanup after the route has a horse, Goldenhills support, Companions progress, and at least conditional Lakeview access. The player can now handle several combat, dungeon, and service objectives without the fragile post-Helgen survival constraints that kept the opening road narrow.

Falkreath work is routed as a coherent Dengeir bundle: start `Some Light Theft`, steal the quest-enabled Private Letter, turn it in, then complete `Dark Ancestor` at Bloodlet Throne. Bloodlet is cleared in the same pass because it is the quest destination and carries a clearable-location row. Its colocated `Incident at Necrom` skill-book copy remains closed for the Scholar's Insight window.

Moss Mother Cavern is routed now because Valdr is at the cave entrance, the quest and clearable-location objective are identical in practice, and the unique reward is obtained immediately after the clear. The guide requires a standard Potion of Healing to avoid the healing-step ambiguity noted on the source page.

Pinewatch is routed in the Lakeview/Pinewatch sub-loop because it is close to Lakeview, is a clearable location, contains the Pinewatch Stone of Barenziah, two tracked documents, and the Curious Silver Mold used by the later Markarth Thieves Guild special job. `Silver Lining` can accept the mold if it was acquired before the quest; collecting it now avoids a later repeat clear. The local `The Red Kitchen Reader` copy is left closed.

Lakeview service progress is conditional on the previous Falkreath land route. If Lakeview exists, the Small House can be completed from the site materials, Rayya can be assigned as the permanent Lakeview steward after the Small House is complete, and the carriage driver can be hired for 500 gold once normal section loot creates spare cash. Steward furnishings, bard service, livestock, and a homestead horse remain held for the later Main Hall/service and exterior-building passes.

Ivarstead is routed as a compact village/barrow bundle. Temba and Boti provide colocated activity/favor rows, Narfi's beggar gift and `The Straw that Broke` are safe before the later Dark Brotherhood contract, and Wilhelm's `Lifting the Shroud` directly unlocks the Sapphire Dragon Claw for the same-location Shroud Hearth Barrow clear. Shroud Hearth also provides a deterministic Oakflesh spell tome and the first routed Kyne's Peace word. Its `Before the Ages of Man` copy remains closed.

`Fan Favorite` is safe and efficient from Ivarstead only if Falkreath land is already settled. The quest page warns that Arena Fan's corpse may despawn if delayed, but it also warns that the quest may stop the Jarl of Falkreath from talking after the Jarl letter. The guide therefore routes it now on saves where Lakeview was already purchased, and holds it on saves where Siddgeir's land chain is still parked behind Cracked Tusk Keep or Knifepoint Ridge.

## Evidence Summary

`Blacksmithing Tutorial` has a fixed smithing sequence and UESP identifies the intended Lod/Falkreath giver as bugged in the unpatched game. Because the route is no-mod PS4 and reaches Alvor safely during the first Riverwood stop, the player-facing guide now routes the blacksmithing tutorial with Alvor instead of delaying it or using Lod.

`Alchemy Tutorial` completes when the player asks a valid giver about the lab and crafts a potion. The guide already sends the player to Arcadia for `Salt for Arcadia`, so the alchemy tutorial is routed there instead of later with Zaria.

`Some Light Theft` supports asking Dengeir about a plot, stealing the quest-enabled Private Letter from Lod's House, and returning it to Dengeir. `Dark Ancestor` follows from Dengeir's favor, sends the player to kill Vighar in Bloodlet Throne, and is available only at level 10 or higher; the route reaches this section after enough early content that the level gate should be satisfied. Bloodlet Throne is clearable and contains `Incident at Necrom`, which remains closed for skill-book timing.

The Moss Mother source pages support healing Valdr, clearing the cave, and receiving Valdr's Lucky Dagger. The guide supplies a standard Potion of Healing before the route reaches Valdr because the quest page notes healing-step bugs and Healing Hands/potion differences.

Pinewatch is clearable and source-listed as containing `Note to Rhorlak`, `The Red Kitchen Reader`, `Father's Missive`, the Pinewatch Stone of Barenziah, and the Curious Silver Mold. `Silver Lining` sends the Thieves Guild to retrieve the same mold later, and its source supports taking the mold from Pinewatch before the quest. The guide therefore clears Pinewatch once, takes the mold and Stone, and preserves the mold for the later Markarth special job.

Construction and steward sources support completing the Small House from provided site materials, then assigning a steward once the Small House is built. Rayya is the route-default Lakeview steward when unlocked. The Lakeview carriage driver costs 500 gold through the steward and is useful for Survival travel, so it is routed after ordinary section loot can fund it. Bard service, steward furnishing, livestock, and homestead horse purchase remain staged because they depend on later Main Hall/exterior/service planning and larger economy.

`The Straw that Broke` requires speaking to Narfi, asking Wilhelm, retrieving Reyda's Necklace, and returning it to Narfi. The guide routes it before the later Dark Brotherhood contract `Kill Narfi`. Ivarstead also supports Temba firewood and Boti crop-sale checklist rows, and Narfi supplies an Ivarstead beggar-favor row. `Grin and Bear It` is accepted during the same Temba conversation, but completion waits until routed bear dens and Broken Oar Grotto provide a controlled set of ten pelts.

`Lifting the Shroud` and Shroud Hearth Barrow naturally bundle together. Wilhelm gives the barrow hook, Wyndelius's Journal turns in for the Sapphire Dragon Claw, the claw opens the rest of the barrow, the barrow contains a deterministic Oakflesh tome, and its word wall supplies a Kyne's Peace word. The guide includes a local warning not to use Unrelenting Force or destructive area effects near Wyndelius's journal because the player is sent to that exact table.

`Fan Favorite` starts from Arena Fan's Note south of Ivarstead and sends the player to Ruins of Bthalft for Dwarven Mail. Its corpse-despawn warning argues for routing it when the Ivarstead pass reaches the camp. Its Falkreath Jarl bug argues against routing it while the Falkreath land chain is still blocked on Siddgeir. The guide therefore makes it conditional: route it now if Lakeview was already bought, hold it if Falkreath land is still parked.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the tutorial retrospective move, Dengeir/Bloodlet routing, Moss Mother/Valdr routing, Pinewatch/Stone/mold routing, Ivarstead/Narfi/Shroud/Temba routing, and the Lakeview steward/carriage conditional on saves where Lakeview exists.

Confidence is medium for the `Fan Favorite` conditional because it balances two source-listed bugs: a corpse despawn if delayed and a Falkreath Jarl dialogue failure if the quest collides with the Jarl letter path. The guide's conditional policy minimizes the live risk on both save states, but later Falkreath-land recovery sections must remember to route `Fan Favorite` promptly once the parked land condition is resolved.

No MR-012 route-resolution notes remain open.
