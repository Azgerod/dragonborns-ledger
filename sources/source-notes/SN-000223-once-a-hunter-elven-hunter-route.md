# Once A Hunter And Elven Hunter Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000223

## Claim

`Once A Hunter` should be routed as a late Sunderstone-area AE stop immediately before `Interception` sends the player to the caravan south of Sunderstone Gorge. The route detours to Falkreath Barracks, reads and takes `Guard Dossier: Aesrael`, `Guard's Bounty Letter Draft`, and `Guard's Note`, follows the marker to Aesrael's camp west of Sunderstone Gorge, defeats Aesrael and Dusk, loots and reads `Aesrael's Journal`, takes `Aesrael's Key`, opens Aesrael's chest, and preserves `Elven Hunter Armor`, `Elven Hunter Boots`, `Elven Hunter Gauntlets`, and `The Crimson Dirks, v7`.

## Routing Relevance

The quest page identifies the Falkreath Barracks documents as the start, Aesrael's camp near Sunderstone Gorge as the destination, Aesrael's key as the chest gate, and the three Elven Hunter armor pieces plus `The Crimson Dirks, v7` as chest contents. The Creation page confirms that `Once A Hunter`, Aesrael, Dusk, the three barracks documents, `Aesrael's Journal`, `The Crimson Dirks, v7`, Aesrael's Key, and the three Elven Hunter armor pieces belong to `Alternative Armors - Elven Hunter`.

UESP records that Aesrael's camp overlaps the final `Redguard Elite Armaments` location at Sunderstone Gorge. The current guide already clears the Sunderstone cave much earlier and later returns to the Sunderstone exterior for `Interception`, so this pass routes the exterior Aesrael camp in that late AE window. That avoids starting the Elven Hunter quest during the Hircine jail visit, avoids a surprise Dusk/Aesrael fight during `Interception`, and keeps the camp reward set with a natural AE Sunderstone approach.

## Evidence Summary

`Once A Hunter` starts by reading either `Guard Dossier: Aesrael` or `Guard's Bounty Letter Draft` at Falkreath Barracks. `Guard's Note` is on the same barracks table and belongs to the same Creation, so the guide reads and takes all three documents in one controlled return rather than leaving a related note behind.

Aesrael is a hostile High Elf at a camp west of Sunderstone Gorge. He carries `Aesrael's Journal` and Aesrael's Key. Dusk is his hostile wolf companion. The route makes a rotating save before the camp, defeats both hostile actors, then reads and preserves the journal and key.

The key opens Aesrael's chest near the tent. The chest contains `Elven Hunter Armor`, `Elven Hunter Boots`, `Elven Hunter Gauntlets`, `The Crimson Dirks, v7`, and leveled loot. The guide names the three armor pieces and the book explicitly because they close the Elven Hunter parent set and checklist-mapped book rows. The Elven page records that the set has no helmet or shield and that the armor can be crafted after the quest, but the deterministic completion pickup is the chest reward.

The camp does not require re-entering Sunderstone Gorge. The current route already clears the cave and Stone of Barenziah earlier. This pass only resolves the exterior Aesrael camp and then continues to the nearby `Interception` caravan objective.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the start documents, camp target, hostile encounter, key/chest flow, quest completion boundary, book/document pickups, and three-piece armor preservation.

The only retained caveat is directional wording: source pages and in-game text describe Aesrael's camp around Sunderstone with south/west language. The player-facing guide follows the active marker and uses the camp west of Sunderstone Gorge wording from the NPC and book pages.
