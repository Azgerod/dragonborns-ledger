# SN-000189 - Fahlbtharz, Deathbrand, And Karstaag Route

## Scope

This note supports the TB-035 expansion of `Fahlbtharz, Deathbrand, And Karstaag` in `drafts/final-guide/main-guide-v1.md`. It records the source-backed placement decisions for Fahlbtharz, the fifth Kagrumez Resonance Gem, Visage of Mzund, the full Deathbrand treasure circuit, Horker Island, Gyldenhul Barrow, Frossel, Glacial Cave, Castle Karstaag, `Summoning Karstaag`, `Paid in Full`, Glover Mallory's house/basement rewards, and the current holds for Benkongerike, White Ridge Barrow, Highpoint Tower, and final Miraak progression.

## Placement Decisions

- Fahlbtharz is routed now because the route has Solstheim cold support, Severin Manor storage, and the first four Kagrumez Resonance Gems already collected. The fifth gem, Visage of Mzund, and Fahlbtharz clearable-location row are handled together instead of leaving the ruin for a generic late location pass.
- Fahlbtharz Forge is not entered in this section. It is physically inside Fahlbtharz when Ghosts of the Tribunal is installed, but it belongs to the Tribunal/Trueflame chain and has its own branch/default handling.
- Deathbrand is completed now because the route already started the quest from the Bloodskal Barrow copy of `Deathbrand`, is well past the level 36 gate, has an Ancient Nordic Pickaxe, and is entering a prepared north-coast Solstheim loop. The full armor circuit, Horker Island, Horksbane, Gyldenhul Barrow, Torn Note, Bloodscythe, and Soulrender are kept together.
- The route uses the Gyldenhul Barrow Key from the fourth Deathbrand armor chest and does not bypass the entrance lock. UESP records a lock-bypass path that can break quest stages; this remains a source-backed route-order reason, not player-facing recovery prose.
- Frossel and Glacial Cave are pulled into this pass because they sit in the same north Solstheim cold-travel corridor and have no later quest prerequisites once Karstaag is being routed. Their East Empire Company strongboxes are handled with the location clear.
- The unmarked giant nirnroot island strongbox and the Riekling-island strongbox north of Haknir's Shoal are routed now because the section already commits to the north-coast water/island loop.
- Castle Karstaag Caverns is routed through the full cave rather than through the master-locked front door, because that clears the location and collects the Bonemold Formula for the already-started `Paid in Full` quest.
- `Summoning Karstaag` is completed now as a prepared late-Solstheim challenge. The guide records the limited-use Summon Karstaag power and preserves all three uses for a later route call.
- `Paid in Full` is completed immediately after Castle Karstaag because the formula is now in hand and Raven Rock is the natural recovery/storage point. Glover's basement content, Glover's Letter, Blackguard's Armor set, and the Glover Mallory's House East Empire Pendant are handled at the same time.
- Glover's Letter is acquired now, but Sapphire's Exquisite Sapphire reward is held for the next coherent Riften return. A ferry/mainland round trip solely for that handoff would be a poor Solstheim-section interruption.
- The section keeps `Pain in the Necklace` active because the Benkongerike strongbox remains with `Black Book: Untold Legends` and `Lost Knowledge`.
- The section names a natural Torkild handling window during the north-coast route but does not ask the player to roam or reload to force the random encounter. `Filial Bonds` therefore remains unresolved in coverage unless Torkild appears naturally on the actual save.

## Deferred From This Pass

- Benkongerike and `Black Book: Untold Legends` remain held for the later Lost Knowledge route. Entering and taking the Black Book before Neloth can send the player there would undercut the planned Lost Knowledge handling.
- White Ridge Barrow, `Black Book: The Sallow Regent`, Cyclone: Nos, Dukaan, the Imbuing Chamber, and White Ridge spell/book candidates remain held for the same post-Gardener/Lost Knowledge route.
- Highpoint Tower and `Old Friends` remain held until `Briarheart Necropsy` is active and a mainland Forsworn Briarheart route can satisfy the prerequisite cleanly.
- `The Gardener of Men` remains parked at Storn until `Filial Bonds` is resolved or the route adopts a deterministic Torkild policy. Completing Storn early starts `At the Summit of Apocrypha`, and UESP records that active finale quest as blocking Wulf's letter turn-in.
- `At the Summit of Apocrypha` and the final Miraak fight remain held for the level 60 Miraak equipment gate.

## Open Questions

`Filial Bonds` still depends on an uncontrolled Torkild random encounter. This section gives the player a natural north-coast action window if he appears, but does not force the spawn. The internal coverage tracker keeps the row unresolved until a deterministic or explicitly approved random-encounter policy exists.

`Note from Mogrul` remains the inherited unresolved row from SN-000187 because the source page still has no fixed executable pickup path.
