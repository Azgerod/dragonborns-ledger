## Scope

This note supports the v1 guide section `Guild Restoration And Amulet Of Articulation`. ## Route Decisions

The guide keeps the Delvin/Vex restoration system as a controlled block after `Darkness Returns`. The Thieves Guild source records a bug risk from completing too many miscellaneous jobs before becoming a Nightingale; the route therefore avoided opportunistic city-job scattering until the Nightingale/Skeleton Key chain was complete. The earlier Solitude exception remains justified because `The Dainty Sload` had to precede `Bound Until Death` while Erikur was safe.

The section starts by turning in `Toying With The Dead` because the player is already in the Ragged Flagon and all four Arondil journals were collected during the Yngvild pass. `Possible Rivals` is also routed because it is in the Cistern and belongs to the upcoming Windhelm special job.

For city restoration, the guide starts from the already-complete Solitude 5/5 state and routes Markarth, Whiterun, and Windhelm in a compact counter block. The player uses saves before job requests because source pages record random city assignment, quit/reload behavior, permanent target-item side effects for some jobs, Raven Rock Bedlam being impossible, and invalid ledger targets. The guide does not build the surrounding route around one random outcome; it treats job targets as isolated divergences and returns to the Flagon.

Markarth carries the remaining job-type coverage by requiring one Fishing Job and one Bedlam Job there. The other three Markarth jobs can be any eligible Markarth Delvin/Vex side job. This closes all seven representative job types without spreading additional random-job burden into unrelated route sections.

`Silver Lining` is fast because the Curious Silver Mold was already recovered from Pinewatch in the southern corridor. UESP explicitly supports the mold being taken before the quest, so the Markarth special can complete through Endon and Delvin without a second Pinewatch clear.

`Imitation Amnesty` is routed after five Whiterun jobs. It sends the player into Dragonsreach Jarl's Quarters, so the guide takes the Dragonsreach Stone of Barenziah and `Letter from Solitude` there, then alters the Whiterun Prison Registry and returns to Olfrid/Delvin.

`Thieves Guild Caravan Fence Quest` is routed after the third completed reputation quest because Tonilia can start it at that point. The caravan location is variable, so the guide isolates the delivery to Ri'saad and returns to Tonilia rather than routing nearby objectives around the caravan's current position.

Uttering Hills Cave is cleared once before `Summerset Shadows` starts because the location source records that the clear state depends on the pre-quest bandit chief. The later `Summerset Shadows` pass then returns to the cave for the quest-specific Linwe, banner, locket, and Linwe armor set work. This is an intentional split caused by source-backed clear-state behavior, not theme bucketing.

`Summerset Shadows` uses the evidence route in Niranye's House even though the Amulet of Articulation will soon make persuasion easy. This preserves Niranye as a fence and routes the two Linwe letters directly from their source location. Linwe is killed on the main route because his four-piece armor set is unique and needs to be preserved.

`Under New Management` is routed immediately after all four reputation quests and `Darkness Returns` are complete. The guide requires the preserved ordinary Thieves Guild armor set before Tonilia's Guild Master armor handoff because UESP records armor availability as a reward blocker. The Amulet of Articulation uses the established hard save because UESP records seven random player versions at all levels, with the strongest version carrying 8 armor, 35% better Speechcraft, and 25-point Articulation.

The 125-job counter is completed after `Under New Management` rather than before it because the Guild Master rewards, restored fences, and restored Flagon services make the long counter block safer and more useful. The guide counts only Delvin/Vex side jobs and records the display milestones and the final safe at the source-listed totals.

Shadowfoot Sanctum is bought after the 125-job block because the counter work provides enough ordinary gold to cover the 7,500-gold purchase without assuming early-property money or exploits. The guide enters the home to complete its quest and ownership rows, but leaves display placement and family moves for the later homes/display section.

`Onmund's Request` remains outside this Ragged Flagon restoration block because it is not colocated with the Thieves Guild work. The follow-up Whiterun/College audit insertion now uses the newly acquired Amulet of Articulation to avoid the random staff-retrieval path.

## Coverage Summary

This pass places `Toying With The Dead`, `Possible Rivals`, Markarth/Whiterun/Windhelm city restoration counts, Fishing Job, Bedlam Job, `Silver Lining`, Endon fence unlock, `Imitation Amnesty`, the Dragonsreach Stone of Barenziah, `Letter from Solitude`, `Thieves Guild Caravan Fence Quest`, Atahbah/Ma'jhad/Zaynabi fence unlock, Uttering Hills Cave clear, `Summerset Shadows`, `First Letter from Linwe`, `Second Letter from Linwe`, Linwe's Armor set, Niranye fence unlock, restored Flagon merchant/recruit availability, `Under New Management`, Tribute Chest Key, Amulet of Articulation, Guild Master's Armor set, the 125-job display/safe boundary, and Shadowfoot Sanctum purchase/ownership.

Rows intentionally staged with concrete reasons: `Mace Etiquette` remains with the selected post-Scholar's Insight skill-book source; Shadowfoot display placement and family moves remain with the later homes/display route. `Onmund's Request` is no longer a long-term hold after this section; it is routed in the immediate Whiterun/College return created by the TB-035 routing audit.

No TB-035-MR-040 unresolved route rows are introduced by this pass.
