# Source Note: Bloated Man's Grotto Bolar's Oathblade Cell State

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000095

## Claim

Bloated Man's Grotto changes state during Ill Met By Moonlight. In the quest version, Bolar's Oathblade and Bolar's Writ are removed from their normal location, and the non-quest clear condition is not available unless the route handles the grotto before the quest or follows the post-quest reset path.

## Routing Relevance

Bolar's Oathblade is a unique item and Bloated Man's Grotto is a source-listed clearable location. The final route needs a precise warning before starting Ill Met By Moonlight or entering the quest version of the grotto, especially if the route wants both the unique weapon and a Sinding-preserving main outcome.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's Bloated Man's Grotto page states that the Great Hunt version of the cell replaces the regular inhabitants and removes the normal shrine-related contents, including Bolar's Oathblade and Bolar's Writ. It also says the clear marker requires the non-quest animal and spriggan clear route, so players who want the blade and/or the cleared marker while letting Sinding live should visit before Ill Met By Moonlight.

The Bolar's Oathblade and Ill Met By Moonlight pages describe the recovery path if the sword was not taken before the quest: kill Sinding, then avoid the grotto for an in-game month so it can reset to the non-quest version. The location and quest pages also note that entering the quest version can force a late-night time change, which matters later for Survival Mode routing.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high that the route needs a pre-Ill-Met warning for Bloated Man's Grotto if Bolar's Oathblade, the non-quest clear marker, or a Sinding-preserving outcome matters. TB-028/TB-029 now own the branch treatment; TB-031G must validate the location-clear/access implications, and TB-032 must place the final cell-state and Survival fatigue warnings if the quest route enters the grotto at night.
