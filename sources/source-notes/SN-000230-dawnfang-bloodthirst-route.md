# A Soul Divided and Bloodthirst Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000230

## Claim

`A Soul Divided` should be routed during the first Ratway/Skritch pass, before the guide later clears Faldar's Tooth. The guide should make `HARD SAVE: HS-DAWNFANG-GUARDIAN-VAULT`, follow the Ghostly Apparition into the Guardian Vault, read/take `Sellonus's Confession` plus `Sellonus's Journal Vol. 1`, `Sellonus's Journal Vol. 2`, and `Sellonus's Journal Vol. 3`, read/take `Verrick's Note` in the north chamber to start `Bloodthirst`, finish the Guardian Vault sequence, take and preserve Dawnfang/Duskfang, then keep `Bloodthirst` active for the later Faldar's Tooth and Broken Fang Cave steps.

`Bloodthirst` should progress at Faldar's Tooth only after `Verrick's Note` has started the quest, because the Faldar's Tooth note is conditional on that active state. The route should then close `Bloodthirst` during the existing late Broken Fang Cave clear by taking Bloodthirst from the boss chest and preserving it with the Dawnfang/Duskfang weapon set.

## Routing Relevance

The current guide already passes the Ratway trigger room, later clears Faldar's Tooth, and much later clears Broken Fang Cave for selected skill books. If the guide clears Faldar's Tooth before reading `Verrick's Note`, the conditional `Cage Bandit's Note` pickup can be missed on the intended pass. Starting and completing `A Soul Divided` at the Ratway pass fixes the chain without adding a detached late route.

UESP records several Guardian Vault bugs, including a confirmed crash when the second Guardian dies, a possible north-chamber Guardian hostility failure, skeleton behavior around the east-room coffin, and a console-specific east-room Guardian spawn issue. UESP also records that reading `Verrick's Note` may fail to start `Bloodthirst`. The route therefore uses a named hard save before the vault and requires a quest-objective confirmation after reading Verrick's note.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's `A Soul Divided` page says the quest is automatically added after encountering the Ghostly Apparition in the Ratway, lists the Ratway and Guardian Vault as the locations, and lists Dawnfang/Duskfang as the reward. The quick route is to follow the apparition, investigate the vault, defeat the Guardian, enter the north chamber, defeat the Guardian again, enter the east chamber, defeat the Guardian again, return to the sword chamber, and retrieve the sword. The page also says `Verrick's Note` in the north chamber starts `Bloodthirst`.

UESP's Guardian Vault page places the alternate ghost start in the same Ratway location as Skritch's cage, with Shadowfoot Sanctum nearby. It describes the Guardian Vault as a Ratway dungeon with no map marker and no clearable marker. It tells the player to find `Sellonus's Confession` from Vellonus before following the ghost, then later identifies `Verrick's Note` on a table in the north chamber and says reading it adds `Bloodthirst`. It also confirms the final return to the sword chamber is when the sword can be taken.

The Sellonus document pages identify `Sellonus's Confession` as a Ratway document and all three `Sellonus's Journal` volumes as Guardian Vault documents. UESP does not provide a precise sublocation for every volume. Community wiki pages give secondary support for `Sellonus's Journal Vol. 1` on the Tomb of the Guardian's Wife and `Sellonus's Journal Vol. 2` in the north chamber's upper locked cell. Because the source set still only places Vol. 3 at the vault level, the player-facing route uses an inventory confirmation before leaving the vault rather than pretending a more precise source-backed placement exists.

UESP's `Bloodthirst` quest page starts the quest by reading `Verrick's Note`, sends the player to Faldar's Tooth, has the player read `Cage Bandit's Note`, then sends the player to Broken Fang Cave to find Bloodthirst among the loot. Its detailed route places `Cage Bandit's Note` in the gambler cage area at Faldar's Tooth and says the Broken Fang Cave boss chest beside the double bed contains Bloodthirst and leveled loot. The Faldar's Tooth page confirms that `Cage Bandit's Note` appears under the caged counter if `Bloodthirst` has started. This makes the first Ratway pass the safe start point before the guide's existing Faldar's Tooth clear.

UESP's Broken Fang Cave page confirms it is clearable, occupied by vampires and skeletons, and contains the selected skill-book copies `The Wolf Queen, v1` and `Mystery of Talara, Part 4` near the vampire lair and arcane enchanter. The current guide already routes this cave before The Cause shrine, so the Bloodthirst pickup belongs in that step rather than in a separate earlier Whiterun detour.

The Dawnfang and Bloodthirst item pages confirm both weapons are artifacts from the Dawnfang & Duskfang Creation. Dawnfang/Duskfang is acquired through `A Soul Divided` from the Guardian Vault in Riften's Ratways; Bloodthirst is acquired from the miscellaneous quest obtained by reading `Verrick's Note` during `A Soul Divided`. The route preserves both unique weapons and does not use Hendraheim basement racks for Bloodthirst because UESP records a rack-removal bug there.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the route order, quest starts, Guardian Vault completion boundary, Dawnfang/Duskfang reward, Verrick-to-Faldar-to-Broken Fang Bloodthirst chain, Faldar's Tooth conditional note, Broken Fang Cave reward chest, and preservation policy.

The remaining precision gap is the exact in-vault sublocation of `Sellonus's Journal Vol. 3`. The source-backed location is the Guardian Vault, and the guide mitigates the gap by naming the volume and requiring all four Sellonus documents in inventory before leaving the vault. If a later source or live PS4 check identifies the exact sublocation, update the guide step and this note.
