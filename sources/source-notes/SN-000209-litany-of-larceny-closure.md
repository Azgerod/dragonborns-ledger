# SN-000209 - Litany of Larceny Closure

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000209

## Claim

`The Litany of Larceny` is complete when the route has delivered all seven Thieves Guild larceny items to Delvin Mallory: Queen Bee Statue, Honningbrew Decanter, East Empire Shipping Map, Model Ship, Dwemer Puzzle Cube, Bust of the Gray Fox, and Left Eye of the Falmer. The existing guide already routes all seven; TB-044 only needs to mark the final Left Eye turn-in as the quest closeout.

## Routing Relevance

The earlier guide sections route the first four larceny items during the early Thieves Guild chain and the final three during the level-46 Thieves/Nightingale chain. The source page records a completion-state caveat if the player holds unturned larceny items until the final item is picked up, so the guide's prompt Delvin turn-ins are the preferred route policy.

## Evidence Summary

UESP lists the route-relevant larceny items as the Queen Bee Statue at Goldenglow Estate, Honningbrew Decanter at Honningbrew Meadery, East Empire Shipping Map in the East Empire Company Warehouse, Model Ship in Snow Veil Catacombs/Sanctum, Dwemer Puzzle Cube in Calcelmo's Laboratory, Bust of the Gray Fox in Riftweald Manor, and Left Eye of the Falmer on Mercer Frey in Irkngthand.

The guide already does the matching route work:

The source page records a bug where picking up the final larceny item can mark the quest complete and prevent remaining held items from being turned in. It also records a possible Left Eye sale issue. The guide's prompt-turn-in policy and final Left Eye closeout minimize these risks without adding a new route branch.
