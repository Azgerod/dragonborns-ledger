# Viriya Fishing Chain Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000232

## Claim

The route should complete Viriya's Fishing quest chain before the Imperial Civil War changes Dawnstar's government. The chain should run from the already-started `In A Pinch` through `The Ultimate Feast`, then continue into `Beneath Bronze Waters`, `Wave Breaker`, and `End of the Line` through the paired Bronze Water source note.

For the pre-Bronze Water chain, the guide should finish `In A Pinch`, then ask Viriya for each next job in order: `Clear-Headed`, `Heart of Gold`, `No Contest`, `Crustacean Extermination`, `Tusked Terror`, `Private Lessons`, `Spirits Away`, and `The Ultimate Feast`. It should route `Two of a Kind` after Heart of Gold's courier letter because that quest is unlocked by Mila Valentia rather than by Viriya's normal work dialogue.

## Routing Relevance

`Beneath Bronze Waters` is not a standalone safe insertion. It requires `The Ultimate Feast`, and `Wave Breaker` follows after `Beneath Bronze Waters`. Routing Viriya's chain as one controlled block avoids a dangling Bronze Water Cave return, closes the related Fishing quest checklist rows, and keeps the Dawnstar shack route before the Dawnstar Imperial-control bug matters.

The route should use deterministic or constrained sources where possible:

- Finish `In A Pinch` by taking the juvenile mudcrab from the giant mudcrab pool southeast of Gjukar's Monument, then return it to Viriya.
- For `Clear-Headed`, use a Fishing Rod at Lake Honrich or another temperate-lake Fishing Supplies spot in clear weather until one Glassfish is caught, then deliver it to Zaria in Falkreath and use the disguised potion handoff with the Falkreath guard.
- For `Heart of Gold` and `Two of a Kind`, collect at least two Goldfish from the Fellglow Keep exterior pond spawn, give one to Mila for `Heart of Gold`, then read the courier letter and give the second fish for `Two of a Kind`.
- For `No Contest`, dismiss followers before the Brutius ambush, take/read only one Brutius's Journal, and fish up Warlock's Ring after the ambush.
- For `Crustacean Extermination`, read `Letter on Mudcrab Attacks`, clear the Dawnstar fishing shack mudcrabs, read/take `Tolif's Journal` in the shack, then read/take `Seviur's Journal` from Seviur.
- For `Tusked Terror`, obtain one Arctic Grayling from freezing-water fishing such as Dawnstar's fishing spot near the boat, use it at Fangtusk's feeding rock northeast of Yngol Barrow, and preserve the Vampiric Ring.
- For `Private Lessons`, make a save before Lynea's dock handoff, teach the lesson at Markarth's Warrens dock, and preserve Lynea's Amulet of Dibella.
- For `Spirits Away`, fish three Aged Flin at the overturned cart west of Old Hroldan and turn them in to Ri'saad for Ring of Khajiit.
- For `The Ultimate Feast`, prepare Steamed Mudcrab Legs, Cooked Arctic Char, and Crab Stew, set Gisli's Winking Skeever table, wait for the note and coin purse, and read/take `Gisli's Note`.

The sex-specific courier document after `The Ultimate Feast` should be represented conditionally: a male character can receive `The Fisher King`; a female character can receive `The Fisher Queen`. The guide should tell the player to read and store whichever courier book arrives, without requiring the other sex-exclusive title in the same continuity.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's quest pages present Viriya's work as a chain: `In A Pinch` leads to `Clear-Headed`, `Clear-Headed` leads to `Heart of Gold`, `Heart of Gold` opens both `No Contest` and `Two of a Kind`, and the Viriya line continues through `Crustacean Extermination`, `Tusked Terror`, `Private Lessons`, `Spirits Away`, and `The Ultimate Feast`. `The Ultimate Feast` lists `Beneath Bronze Waters` as its next quest, making the Bronze Water row dependent on the full Viriya chain.

The Fishing activity page says fishing requires a Fishing Rod at Fishing Supplies and that catch results depend on biome, weather, rod, population, time, and active quests. It lists Glassfish among temperate-lake catches in clear weather and places Fishing Supplies on Lake Honrich, including the Riften Fishery pier area. UESP's Glassfish page separately describes Glassfish as a fair-weather temperate-lake catch. The route therefore keeps the Glassfish catch explicit: use a Fishing Rod at a Lake Honrich temperate-lake Fishing Supplies spot in clear weather.

UESP's Heart of Gold and Goldfish pages both support the Fellglow Keep pond source for Goldfish near Whiterun. Because `Two of a Kind` needs a second Goldfish after a courier letter from Mila, the guide should collect two Goldfish while the Fellglow exterior source is open, then wait or use local Whiterun/Riften services for the courier before closing the second quest.

`No Contest` has a specific bug surface: raised ambushers can block Brutius's journal, and taking two copies of the journal can also block reading. The guide therefore dismisses followers and summons before the Morthal ambush and instructs the player to take/read exactly one journal.

`Crustacean Extermination` requires the Dawnstar shack swarm and Seviur's Journal. Tolif's Journal is a separate Fishing document in the same shack, so the guide should read/take it during the same visit. `Tusked Terror` requires reading the bounty, placing an Arctic Grayling at Fangtusk's feeding grounds, looting the Vampiric Ring, and reporting to the Windhelm steward. UESP's Fishing Items table gives Dawnstar's boat fishing spot as an Arctic Grayling source. `Private Lessons` has a possible Lynea dock-appearance bug, so the guide should save before the meet-at-docks stage. `Spirits Away` requires three Aged Flin from the Old Hroldan cart and a Ri'saad turn-in.

`The Ultimate Feast` requires three dishes: Steamed Mudcrab Legs, Cooked Arctic Char, and Crab Stew. UESP's Food page gives the recipes for those dishes and marks them as needed for the quest. Because the route has already visited Hela's Folly, the guide can use that freezing-water Arctic Char source and cook the char with a Salt Pile. Crab Stew uses Garlic, Potato, Leek, and Crab Meat. Steamed Mudcrab Legs use Mudcrab Legs and Butter. After the feast table is set and one hour passes, Gisli leaves a note and a coin purse. The male/female courier book after this quest is character-sex-exclusive; the route should preserve whichever one arrives.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for Viriya's quest order, route-critical documents, rewards, deterministic Goldfish source, Glassfish biome/weather, Arctic Grayling source, Ultimate Feast ingredients, and the No Contest, Heart of Gold, and Private Lessons bug cautions.

The remaining uncertainty is operational rather than factual: Fishing population can be depleted or slowed by ordinary RNG. The guide uses weather/biome/source controls, not RNG manipulation. If a required fish is not caught immediately, the player continues fishing at the named source under the required conditions or returns after the spot repopulates.
