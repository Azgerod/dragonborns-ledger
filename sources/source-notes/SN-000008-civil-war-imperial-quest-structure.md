## Claim

The Imperial Legion Civil War route has an Imperial-specific opening sequence, an overarching Reunification of Skyrim phase, and hold-recapture quests whose exact subset can change based on Season Unending.

The user-resolved main Civil War route is Imperial. The objective database needs the Imperial quest rows and must mark hold-recapture objectives as order-sensitive but not yet fully routed because Season Unending can change which holds remain to be taken.

UESP lists Imperial Introductions, Joining the Legion, Get Outfitted, The Jagged Crown, Message to Whiterun, Battle for Whiterun, Report to the Jarl of Whiterun, Reunification of Skyrim, and Imperial hold-recapture quests. The Imperial page states that Reunification of Skyrim governs the individual battles and that only a subset may be required depending on Season Unending.
