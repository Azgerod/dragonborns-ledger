# Main Quest Trophy Mapping

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000002

## Claim

Eight base-game main quest trophies/achievements are tied to completing specific main quest entries: `Unbound`, `Bleak Falls Barrow`, `The Way of the Voice`, `Diplomatic Immunity`, `Alduin's Wall`, `Elder Knowledge`, `The Fallen`, and `Dragonslayer`.

## Routing Relevance

The objective database must preserve trophy relevance for quest objectives so later route and QA passes can verify trophy safety and checklist synchronization.
