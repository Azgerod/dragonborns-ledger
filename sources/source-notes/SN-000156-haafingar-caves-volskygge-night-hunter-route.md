# SN-000156 - Haafingar Caves, Volskygge, And Night Hunter Route

Status: route-writing source note for TB-035-MR-028.

## Scope

This note supports the v1 guide section `Haafingar Caves, Volskygge, And Night Hunter`. The section routes Sybille Stentor's `Kill the Vampire`, Pinemoon Cave, Clearpine Pond, Volskygge, Whirlwind Sprint: Kest, Volsung, conditional Kahvozein's Fang acquisition, Widow's Watch Ruins, Ironback Hideout, `Night Hunter`, Ironback Hideout Cellar, Elite Crossbows base rewards, and Ravenscar Hollow.

The section deliberately does not route Shadowgreen Cavern, Noster's Helmet, Broken Oar Grotto, `Lights Out!`, Ahtar, `The Dainty Sload`, Proudspire/Haafingar thaneship, Statue to Meridia/Kilkreath, Lost Echo Cave, Meeko's Shack, Dead Man's Dread, Lord's Mail, Solitude Sewers, or Solitude Civil War spaces. Those rows remain mapped in the internal coverage tracker with their route reasons.

## Route Decisions

Sybille Stentor's `Kill the Vampire` is routed now because UESP identifies Pinemoon Cave as the fixed target, not a randomized cave. Earlier Solitude notes that treated Sybille as target-aware are superseded by this source check. The guide therefore starts the favor at the Blue Palace, clears Pinemoon Cave while already heading into western Haafingar, and turns the quest in at the end of the loop.

Pinemoon Cave is routed as a complete cave/favor bundle. `Incident at Necrom` is not the selected skill-book copy for the route, so the guide leaves it closed when reaching the sleeping quarters rather than carrying an unnecessary duplicate before Scholar's Insight.

Clearpine Pond is routed immediately after Pinemoon because it is adjacent, clearable, and has no quest-state or reward gate. The local `De Rerum Dirennis` copy remains untouched because the selected route copy is elsewhere and the title remains staged for the skill-book reading window.

Volskygge is routed in this pass because the current route state is well beyond its minimum practical combat band, it is in the same western Haafingar sweep, and it combines a clearable location, the Volskygge non-journal quest, Whirlwind Sprint: Kest, and Volsung. The guide gives the four-animal puzzle solution as route-critical instruction. `Surfeit of Thieves` remains closed because the selected route copy is Mistveil Keep and the player has not entered the Scholar's Insight reading window.

Kahvozein's Fang is a conditional pickup only. UESP records that it can be found before `Alteration Ritual Spell`, and Volskygge is one of the dragon-priest dungeon contexts relevant to the dagger. The guide therefore tells the player to check for it and preserve it if present, while leaving the actual ritual quest for the fixed late Alteration block.

Widow's Watch Ruins is routed as a visit, not a clear-state claim. UESP records that it lacks the vanilla keywords required to be marked cleared. The coverage tracker therefore marks the location row as handled by the explicit visit/defeat action with a clear-tag caveat. The local `2920, Rain's Hand, v4` remains closed because the selected copy is Blind Cliff Bastion.

Ironback Hideout and `Night Hunter` are routed together because reading Kragrash's Letter, finding the corpse/key near the Thalmor Embassy, entering the cellar, and claiming the base crossbows form one compact Haafingar AE bundle. The guide stages Enhanced Ebony Crossbow and Enhanced Elven Crossbow because UESP states they are craft-only; that belongs in the later smithing/crossbow crafting pass rather than as a claim from the vault.

Ravenscar Hollow is routed after Ironback because it is in the same Thalmor Embassy/Steed Stone coast band and has no quest-state conflict. `The Art of War Magic` remains closed because the selected copy is outside Fallowstone Cave.

Shadowgreen Cavern is not routed despite being nearby. It is part of the Noster Dungeon Delving target pool, and the route already has other active cave-retrieval/radiant conditionals that can affect whether Noster can be started cleanly. The guide keeps Shadowgreen untouched for a target-aware Haafingar retrieval block instead of pre-clearing a possible target.

Broken Oar Grotto, `Lights Out!`, Ahtar, and Noster remain a later target-aware bundle. Broken Oar can overlap Ahtar and Noster concerns, and the previous Solitude coast note records the Captain Hargar/Kharjo/nonrespawn risk. The current pass does not enter Broken Oar or ask for those jobs.

`The Dainty Sload` remains held out of this Haafingar cave sweep because it belongs with Erikur's Thieves Guild special job and must be completed before Dark Brotherhood `Bound Until Death`. TB-035-MR-030B later owns that controlled Solitude job exception and routes the Dainty Sload ship, Balmora Blue Note, Stone of Barenziah, and skill-book copy timing.

Proudspire Manor, Thane of Haafingar, and Jordis are still property/economy work, not Haafingar cave work. The guide has not assumed a spare 25,000 gold purchase budget.

Statue to Meridia and Kilkreath are held because visiting the statue starts `The Break of Dawn`, while the route has not yet placed the Beacon/Kilkreath/Dawnbreaker/Elemental Fury bundle. Lost Echo Cave is held with `Double-Edged`, Cliffside Retreat, and the Alternative Armors Leather route because UESP links the cave to that Creation quest and its hidden-door state. Meeko's Shack is held for a later marsh/Abandoned Shack/pet route rather than an isolated discovery.

## Coverage Notes

This pass directly places `Kill the Vampire`, Pinemoon Cave, Clearpine Pond, Volskygge, Whirlwind Sprint: Kest, Volsung, the Volskygge non-journal quest, Widow's Watch Ruins visit handling, Ironback Hideout, `Night Hunter`, Ironback Hideout Cellar, Elite Crossbows unlock, Ebony Crossbow, Elven Crossbow, and Ravenscar Hollow.

This pass conditionally places Kahvozein's Fang if the routed Volskygge treasure state contains it. If it is not present, the row remains live for the later `Alteration Ritual Spell` search.
