# Source Note: DLC Radiant Boundaries

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000114

## Claim

DLC radiant rows have three different treatments. Dawnguard main-route radiants include finite reward chains (`Ancient Technology` six times and `Lost Relic` three times), plus representative Dawnguard side radiants and additional cycle filler as needed to unlock all Lost Relic artifacts. Volkihar radiants are branch-only, with finite reward sets for Amulets, Rings, Ancient Power upgrades, New Allegiances targets, and Destroying the Dawnguard, while ordinary repeatable Volkihar hunts receive one representative completion each. Hearthfire `Bandit Attack` is a random/reactive family-property event to resolve if triggered, not a forced route objective.

## Routing Relevance

The canonical route joins the Dawnguard, so Dawnguard finite chains belong on the main route and Volkihar content belongs in a hard-save branch. This note gives the route a stop rule for DLC radiants and avoids mixing branch-exclusive vampire content into the canonical save.

## Evidence Summary

The Dawnguard faction page states that `Ancient Technology` can only be done six times and that `Lost Relic` can only be done three times. The exact `Ancient Technology` page ties those six completions to crossbow and bolt crafting/vendor unlocks through Enhanced Dwarven Crossbow. The `Lost Relic` page ties the three completions to the Dawnguard Rune Hammer, Rune Shield, and Rune Axe, and says the number of other Dawnguard radiants needed between versions is semi-random, ranging from as few as five to as many as eighteen between the first and last versions.

Normal Dawnguard radiants should therefore be completed once each for representative coverage, then repeated only as needed to progress the Lost Relic cycle. `Cleansing Light` needs target caution because Movarth's Lair can be assigned even though the quest cannot complete there; `A Jarl's Justice` has a twelve-day minimum recurrence note.

The Volkihar faction page lists its radiants as branch-only under the vampire faction path. Exact pages make several of those finite enough to preserve: Amulets retrieves two unique amulets, Rings retrieves two unique rings, Ancient Power improves Blood of the Ancients up to a four-body-part maximum, New Allegiances can convert one of three named targets and adds a coffin in the target's home, and Destroying the Dawnguard is available after Volkihar-side `Kindred Judgment`. Ordinary Volkihar hunt/framing radiants are representative once each unless the branch checklist later treats a specific target/world-state as required.

`Bandit Attack` requires a spouse living in one of the three constructable Hearthfire homes and has console-player bug caveats involving spouse behavior. Because it is a reactive random property event rather than a finite unlock or representative system the route can deliberately request, it should be resolved safely if triggered but not forced as required content. The Dragonborn quest list and `Kill the Bandit Leader` page identify Fanari's Skaal Village row as a named radiant favor; complete it once on the Dragonborn route.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for Dawnguard finite counts, Volkihar branch separation, and Hearthfire exclusion. Confidence is moderate for fully repeating `New Allegiances`: the source gives three possible named targets and a duplicate-target bug, but TB-021/TB-028 should decide whether the Volkihar branch should pursue all three conversions or only one representative conversion because this alters named NPC world states.
