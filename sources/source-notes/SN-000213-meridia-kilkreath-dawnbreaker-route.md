# SN-000213 - Meridia Kilkreath Dawnbreaker Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000213

## Claim

`The Break of Dawn` is already placed in the Haafingar route and can be closed by tightening the Kilkreath instructions: start from Meridia's Beacon or the Statue to Meridia, retrieve the Beacon by the active marker if needed, place it at the statue, guide Meridia's light through Kilkreath Temple/Ruins/Catacombs, defeat Malkoran and his shade, loot before taking Dawnbreaker, take and preserve Dawnbreaker, and record the artifact for Oblivion Walker.

## Routing Relevance

TB-038R carried `The Break of Dawn`, Dawnbreaker, and the Dawnbreaker checklist row as unresolved even though the guide already routed Meridia during the Haafingar road. The source check confirms the existing placement is natural because Statue to Meridia and Kilkreath are in western Haafingar near the current route. The guide needed explicit route safety around Beacon access, one-way Kilkreath rooms, Malkoran's shade, the Dawnbreaker pickup transport, and Meridia sky-drop bugs.

## Evidence Summary

UESP lists `The Break of Dawn` as a level-12 Daedric quest started either by finding Meridia's Beacon or by visiting the Statue to Meridia. The statue can mark the Beacon location if the player has not already found it. The route therefore uses the existing Haafingar statue visit: if the Beacon is already in inventory, place it; if not, use the active marker as an isolated out-and-back and return.

UESP records that the Beacon marker can be unavailable when the Beacon is in an inaccessible area and that rare Beacon placement bugs exist. The guide mitigates this with a rotating save before committing to the statue path and a no-console, no-reroll policy: if the active marker points into a route-locked space, the player parks Meridia until that space is opened by the normal route and does not advance Dawnbreaker or the artifact counter early.

UESP's Kilkreath route requires activating light pedestals through Kilkreath Temple, Balcony, Ruins, and Catacombs. The final catacomb doors close behind the player, Malkoran is followed by Malkoran's Shade, and taking Dawnbreaker transports the player back to Meridia. The guide therefore adds rotating saves, a loot-before-pickup warning, and a warning not to create the duplicate Dawnbreaker exploit with area-of-effect displacement.

UESP records Meridia sky-dialogue/drop and dragon-interruption bugs, with overencumbrance noted as one cause of the falling issue. The guide tells the player to shed excess carry weight, save before taking Dawnbreaker, and reload if Meridia's sky sequence breaks or a dragon interrupts it.

## Route Decision

Keep the route in `Haafingar Caves, Volskygge, and Night Hunter`, where the guide already discovers the Statue to Meridia. Expand the Meridia step into explicit Beacon, Kilkreath, Malkoran, Dawnbreaker, and bug-handling steps. Close `OBJ-000173`, `OBJ-001572`, `CHK-QUESTS-0162`, and `CHK-UNIQUE-GEAR-1582` in coverage and TB-038R repair data.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for normal Beacon, Kilkreath, and Dawnbreaker completion. Residual risk remains for rare inaccessible Beacon placement or sky-dialogue bugs on PS4; the route mitigates them with saves and delayed completion rather than console fixes or random rerolling.
