## Placement Decisions

- `Unearthed` is routed now because Raven Rock, Severin Manor storage, an Ancient Nordic Pickaxe, cold-weather support, and enough late-route money are already available.
- The guide deliberately takes each Ralis journal during its phase. UESP records that the journals disappear if not taken during each visit and can become permanent quest items if mishandled, so the route pairs them with phase hard saves and avoids taking them after completion.
- `Ahzidal's Descent` is not duplicated in Kolbjorn or Damphall Mine because the Ashfallow Citadel copy was already read in the Raven Rock core route.
- `First Edition` is pulled into this pass because `Cleansing the Stones` is complete, Cindiri Arano can now offer the favor, and the Wreck of the Strident Squall is on the same Raven Rock south/southeast loop as Hrodulf's House and Kolbjorn.
- Hrodulf's House is routed as a full investigation instead of a partial location stop. Although `Hrodulf's Journal` is an excluded broad-library checklist row, reading it here is part of the non-journal quest's clue chain, not a general book-collection requirement.
- Brodir Grove, Damphall Mine, and Coldcinder Cave are routed during excavation waits because they are safe Raven Rock-west clearable objectives with no later quest, level, branch, or random-assignment dependency. Their East Empire Company Pendants and unique Stormfang acquisition are handled at the same time.
- The Wreck of the Strident Squall is not deferred to a generic Solstheim sweep because `First Edition`, the Lusty Argonian Maid Folio, the East Empire Company Pendant, and the clearable shipwreck are all handled cleanly together now.
- The Ring of Arcana instruction includes the water pit because UESP notes that draugr shouts can knock the ring off its plinth before the player reaches it.
- `Black Book: Filament and Filigree` is completed before leaving final Kolbjorn. The Apocrypha copy of `Three Thieves` is now the selected skill-book copy because Scholar's Insight is already active and the book lies directly on the Black Book route.
- The Ralis kill outcome is played as a short branch from `HS-DRAGONBORN-UNEARTHED-RALIS`, then reloaded. Canonical continuity spares Ralis, recruits him once at The Retching Netch, takes Hoarfrost through follower trade, and dismisses him so follower availability remains preserved.
- `A New Debt` remains conditional on the Mogrul thug encounter. If `Mogrul's Orders` is acquired during this pass, the route pays Mogrul before leaving Raven Rock again; `Note from Mogrul` remains unresolved from SN-000187.

## Deferred From This Pass

- Frossel, Glacial Cave, Haknir's Shoal, Horker Island, Deathbrand, Castle Karstaag, Fahlbtharz, White Ridge Barrow, Highpoint Tower, and the remaining northern/coastal Solstheim objectives stay with later Solstheim sweep or quest-specific route blocks. They are not part of the Kolbjorn excavation corridor and several have stronger ties to Deathbrand, Lost Knowledge, Karstaag, or Fahlbtharz routing.
- The Fishing Supplies inside Damphall Mine are not used here because Fishing remains unstarted for a controlled fishing route.

## Open Questions

No new `NEEDS ROUTE RESOLUTION` rows were introduced by this pass. The existing `Note from Mogrul` route-resolution item remains open from SN-000187.
