# Opening Helgen To Riverwood Route Detail

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000130

## Claim

The v1 MR-002 guide can safely choose Hadvar as the route default during `Unbound`, complete the Helgen escape, then follow the opening road through The Guardian Stones to Riverwood without optional combat detours before the first settlement support stop. The Guardian Stones bandit-camp objective bundle is valid and nearby, but it should wait for an early local cleanup loop after food, rest, carry, and save state are stabilized. In Riverwood, the Hadvar path naturally points the player to Alvor for the optional `Before the Storm` Riverwood step, after which the quest remains active until the player reports to Jarl Balgruuf in Whiterun. The first Riverwood stop can also start `The Golden Claw`, resolve `A Lovely Letter` in Faendal's favor with both forged letters handled, stage Faendal as the default Goldenhills steward/follower option, take a legal woodcutter's axe for the Whiterun Hulda loop, and complete the default Embry drunk-favor representative while the player is already using Riverwood services.

## Routing Relevance

MR-002 needs player-facing instructions, not just a main-quest title list. The route therefore needs a cited basis for the Hadvar default, the Helgen escape completion boundary, the Guardian Stones stop, the decision to hold Treasure Map I and its colocated skill-book warning until early local cleanup, the decision not to route Embershard Mine as a shallow discovery-only step, the Alvor/Riverwood step, the decision not to mark `Before the Storm` complete until the Whiterun report, the early `The Golden Claw` start, `A Lovely Letter` timing and Faendal default handling, Alvor's legal support/woodcutter's axe access, and the colocated Embry drink favor.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's `Unbound` page lists the opening path through character creation, Helgen Keep, the Hadvar/Ralof choice, looting weapons and basic equipment, and escaping Helgen. It also records that choosing Hadvar or Ralof does not lock the later Civil War faction choice.

UESP's `Before the Storm` page lists the optional first step as speaking to Alvor or Gerdur in Riverwood, then going to Whiterun and seeking out Jarl Balgruuf. Its detailed walkthrough describes the road out from Helgen, the companion pointing out The Guardian Stones, the Hadvar path directing the player to Alvor in Riverwood, and Alvor or Gerdur offering starter assistance before sending the player to the Jarl. The quest stage table keeps the Alvor/Gerdur step separate from the final Whiterun report and shows completion only after the Jarl is told about Helgen.

UESP's `Treasure Map I` page places the map at an unmarked bandit camp just south of The Guardian Stones and identifies the associated treasure as a fallen-tree cache near Riverwood. UESP's `Night Falls on Sentinel` page places a skill-book copy at that same bandit camp, so when the camp is eventually routed the player-facing guide should include a local warning to leave that copy closed for the planned skill-book reading window. The bundle is nearby, but it requires optional bandit combat before the first Riverwood support stop; the route therefore holds it for the next early local cleanup/economy block rather than front-loading it into the post-Helgen survival walk. UESP's `The Refugees` page places its nearby copy at a separate unmarked camp west of Helgen and south of The Guardian Stones, so MR-002 does not detour there.

UESP's `Embershard Mine` page identifies the mine as clearable, with placed `Spell Tome: Clairvoyance` and `Light Armor Forging` support rows, so the initial broad skip was too generic. The route still holds Embershard for later because `Smith 'N Slash` uses the same cave and UESP's quest page notes that entering after the cave is already cleared can still spawn the quest chief but does not guarantee a full unenchanted Orcish Plate set from the cave. The guide therefore does not route a shallow "discover but do not clear" step; Embershard should be introduced when the mine can be handled as a coherent Smith 'N Slash / Orcish Plate / clearable-location bundle.

UESP's `Anise's Cabin` page shows the cabin is nearby and has a `Song of the Alchemists` skill-book copy, cellar hostility, storage details, and ownership caveats. Since the opening route does not need cabin storage yet and the skill-book copy is subject to the Scholar's Insight policy, the guide does not route a discovery-only cabin step in MR-002.

UESP's `The Golden Claw` page supports Lucan or Camilla as the normal Riverwood Trader start path. Starting it during the first Riverwood service stop avoids an artificial return purely to ask about the claw while preserving the later Bleak Falls Barrow completion boundary.

UESP's `A Lovely Letter` page identifies Sven or Faendal as the giver, Camilla as the delivery target, the ability to show one forged letter to the other man and receive a counter-forged letter, and Faendal or Sven as the follower reward depending on the chosen outcome. Since TB-031D selects Faendal as the Goldenhills steward default, the guide starts with Sven at the Sleeping Giant Inn, shows Sven's forged letter to Faendal, delivers Faendal's letter to Camilla, and returns to Faendal. This gives the guide explicit coverage for `Sven's Fake Letter from Faendal`, `Faendal's Fake Letter from Sven`, `A Lovely Letter`, Faendal follower availability, and Faendal steward candidacy without stretching the quest across unrelated route sections.

UESP's Alvor page records that the Hadvar path makes Alvor willing to help and allow most low-value items in his house, and the Alvor and Sigrid's House page places a woodcutter's axe on the woodpile in the cellar. The guide therefore takes that axe during the Alvor support step so the first Whiterun inn loop can complete Hulda's chop-wood/Argonian Ale bundle without relying on random merchant stock.

The existing activity/favor source note selects Embry in Riverwood as the default representative for the drunk-favor activity, making it natural to complete while the player is already using the Sleeping Giant Inn. Embry's drink favor is not counted toward Hero of the People under the counter plan; `A Lovely Letter` starts that finite miscellaneous objective counter at 1 of 50.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for MR-002's quest-order and route-state handling. The note does not resolve final character build, exact loot quantities, merchant stock randomness, PS4-specific trophy-pop reliability beyond the existing trophy fallback policy, or later Riverwood objectives that need separate route decisions. Treasure Map I should be routed in the early local cleanup/economy block once settlement support is stable. Embershard Mine should be revisited in the later pass that can route `Smith 'N Slash`, Orcish Plate acquisition, `Spell Tome: Clairvoyance`, `Light Armor Forging`, and the clearable-location row together.
