# Opening Helgen To Riverwood Route Detail

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000130

## Claim

The v1 MR-002 guide can safely choose Hadvar as the route default during `Unbound`, complete the Helgen escape, then follow the opening road through The Guardian Stones to Riverwood. In Riverwood, the Hadvar path naturally points the player to Alvor for the optional `Before the Storm` Riverwood step, after which the quest remains active until the player reports to Jarl Balgruuf in Whiterun.

## Routing Relevance

MR-002 needs player-facing instructions, not just a main-quest title list. The route therefore needs a cited basis for the Hadvar default, the Helgen escape completion boundary, the Guardian Stones stop, the Alvor/Riverwood step, and the decision not to mark `Before the Storm` complete until the Whiterun report.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's `Unbound` page lists the opening path through character creation, Helgen Keep, the Hadvar/Ralof choice, looting weapons and basic equipment, and escaping Helgen. It also records that choosing Hadvar or Ralof does not lock the later Civil War faction choice.

UESP's `Before the Storm` page lists the optional first step as speaking to Alvor or Gerdur in Riverwood, then going to Whiterun and seeking out Jarl Balgruuf. Its detailed walkthrough describes the road out from Helgen, the companion pointing out The Guardian Stones, the Hadvar path directing the player to Alvor in Riverwood, and Alvor or Gerdur offering starter assistance before sending the player to the Jarl. The quest stage table keeps the Alvor/Gerdur step separate from the final Whiterun report and shows completion only after the Jarl is told about Helgen.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for MR-002's quest-order and route-state handling. The note does not resolve final character build, exact loot quantities, merchant stock randomness, PS4-specific trophy-pop reliability beyond the existing trophy fallback policy, or later Riverwood side-objective timing.
