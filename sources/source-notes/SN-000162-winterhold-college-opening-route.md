## Scope

## Route Decisions

The old `Under Saarthal` hold is removed from the College opening because the current v1 route has already passed level 36 and has already started `Forbidden Legend` from the Blue Palace/Folgunthur route. Saarthal is now the correct next Gauldur-chain step: it is unlocked only through `Under Saarthal`, it contains Jyrik's fragment, and keeping it separate until a later "level 36 loop" would now be stale scaffold logic rather than a live constraint.

`Drowned Sorrows` is moved retrospectively into the Bards instrument-road section. The route already visits Winterhold and Hob's Fall Cave for Pantea's Flute, and UESP records that Isabelle Rolaine's body appears in Hob's Fall only after the Winterhold inn lead is started. The guide therefore completes Haran's `A Few Words with You`, Ranmir's drunk favor, starts the Isabelle lead, takes Isabelle's Letter during the Hob's Fall clear, and returns it to Ranmir before leaving the Winterhold road. Ranmir's drunk favor is placed before `Drowned Sorrows` because Ranmir disappears after the quest.

Winterhold town objectives are pulled into the College entry pass rather than held for a Winterhold theme block. Birna's Coral Dragon Claw, Malur's staff theft, Thorgar's mine-ore favor, and the Shrine of Azura/Nelacar lead are safe, close, and naturally adjacent to the Tolfdir travel gap after `First Lessons`. The Black Star artifact outcome remains later because Ilinalta's Deep and the artifact branch are separate route work, not Winterhold town errands.

Faralda's admission test is written as a controlled random test rather than a seed/reload instruction. The route accepts whichever valid test spell she assigns, buys the discounted 30-gold tome only if needed, and records the learned spell/tome conditionally. This avoids unnecessary RNG manipulation while still covering all possible spell outcomes cleanly.

`Out of Balance` is completed immediately after `First Lessons` because the focal points are all on College grounds and the quest is available while the player is already waiting for Tolfdir and the students to walk to Saarthal.

`Under Saarthal` is routed as one complete dungeon pass. The guide preserves all three Enchanted Rings instead of returning them to Arniel because UESP records no reward for the turn-in and the project unique-item policy preserves unique gear. The guide talks to Tolfdir before breaking the amulet wall to avoid the recorded gate bug, includes both pillar solutions, waits for Tolfdir to make Jyrik vulnerable, avoids fire-heavy tactics near the staff until it is secured, and takes `Writ of Sealing (Jyrik)`, Staff of Jyrik Gauldurson, `Ice Form: Nus`, and the College Stone of Barenziah before moving on.

`Hitting the Books` is advanced only to Urag's Fellglow lead. Fellglow Keep is a separate College continuation dungeon, so the current pass starts the quest and records the next target without turning the College opening into a full College-completion run.

`Swift as an Arrow` is routed now because its required tome and research notes are in the Arcanaeum as soon as College access is available. The guide leaves Soran's Journal and Eranya's Research Notes closed despite being nearby because their next steps send the route to the Rift/Unicorn and Goldbrand/Sivdur chain respectively, and the player will return to the Arcanaeum during later College work if a better pickup window emerges.

Brelyna and J'zargo are routed immediately after `Under Saarthal` because they unlock at that point and are resolved entirely on College/Midden ground. J'zargo is paired with the Midden/Forgotten Names pass because UESP specifically identifies The Midden undead as convenient test targets if the quest is accepted before entering.

`Forgotten Names` is routed now because the route is already entering The Midden for J'zargo and has College/Arcanaeum access for the ring chest. The main route releases Velehk Sain to receive the treasure map, consistent with the project branch-default matrix. `HS-COLLEGE-VELEHK-SAIN` protects the outcome and map-spawn state. The kill-Velehk outcome remains unbranched because prior branch policy did not promote it to a full branch.

Onmund's Request is deliberately held. It opens after `Under Saarthal`, but the default path asks for a Grand Staff of Charming at a random dungeon target, and UESP records both a large target pool and a pre-found-staff bug. The route can later use high Speech or the Amulet of Articulation to avoid unnecessary target churn and protect the amulet/follower/marriage unlock cleanly.

Sergius's `Enchanting Pick-Up`, Urag's `Fetch me that Book!`, Urag's `Shalidor's Insights`, and Drevis's `Restocking Soul Gems` are held for a later College/radiant-control pass. These are repeatable or target/material-dependent tasks; starting them during the first College pass would add random target and inventory pressure without a current routing benefit.

Tolfdir's Alembic is held because UESP records that Tolfdir remains at Saarthal watching the orb until `Hitting the Books` is nearly complete. The route will handle the alembic when Tolfdir is actually back at the College.

The Atronach Forge is audited but not used in this pass. The selected practical use remains a good future Fire Salts recipe, but UESP's recipe requires a Salt Pile, ruby or ruby jewelry, and a soul gem. The first College/Midden pass does not guarantee those exact materials, and the forge can consume extra eligible materials if the offering box is overfilled. Route it later when the material set is deterministic.

`Seeks-Ancient-Artifacts' Journal, v1` is left in The Frozen Hearth, and Soran/Eranya desk notes are left in the Arcanaeum. These are not generic deferrals: each starts an AE quest whose next meaningful work is outside this Winterhold/College loop and should be threaded into the route when the related location and reward chain are ready.
