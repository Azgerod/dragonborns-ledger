# Goldenhills Farm And Rorikstead

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000136

## Claim

After the level-8 Silent Moons gate, the guide should route a coherent Rorikstead and Goldenhills pass: start `Pets of Skyrim` from the Whiterun notice, bring Faendal for the default Goldenhills steward role, discover Rorikstead, complete Erik's favor, claim Goldenhills through `The Unquiet Dead`, complete the required `A Farmer's Life For Me` farm setup loop, buy Hilda, read `Halvar's Journal`, and confirm Erik's hireling availability after farm wait time has passed.

## Routing Relevance

Goldenhills is free to claim once `The Unquiet Dead` is complete, so it gives the route a central bed, food, and income base without assuming the player has the 5,000 gold needed for Breezehome or the 7,500 gold needed for Tundra Homestead. The section deliberately does not turn Goldenhills into the primary collection-storage or display base yet because the property detail rows still require later storage/display policy and some furnishings have source-listed caveats.

`The Unquiet Dead` has several source-listed start and progression bugs around first approach, followers, horses, wolves at the well, and leaving the quest after early ghost stages. The guide therefore makes a hard save before first approach, parks Faendal and the horse away from the plantation, starts the quest by direct arrival, completes the ghost investigation in one sitting, and does not acquire the optional `Bounty - Restless Spirits` innkeeper letter.

## Evidence Summary

UESP records two `The Unquiet Dead` start paths: the optional `Bounty - Restless Spirits` bounty letter or direct travel to Goldenhills Plantation. The guide uses direct travel because the project table has no fixed acquisition location for the bounty letter and asking innkeepers for work would introduce an unnecessary random work surface. The guide still represents the letter as an explicit route exclusion in the internal coverage tracker.

The Unquiet Dead page routes the investigation through Urval's corpse and `Torn Page`, `Urval's Journal`, Jonquil's secret lab and `Jonquil's Journal`, Rin's room and `Rin's Journal`, then the old well and Rin's Toy Sword. The same page warns that quest startup and ghost progression can fail if the area or old well state is disturbed early, if followers or horses interfere with the first ghost fight, or if the player leaves after early ghost stages. The player-facing guide therefore hard-saves before first approach, has the player fight the first ghost alone, waits to visit the old well until the journals point there, and completes the investigation before moving on.

`A Farmer's Life For Me` requires planting ten crops, recruiting a steward, hiring farmhands, building Animal Pens, buying livestock, waiting for income, and collecting pantry output. Goldenhills source pages list the relevant livestock costs as cow 300 gold, goats 200 gold, and chickens 100 gold, while the farmhand service costs 500 gold. The guide requires 1,300 gold for the required farm operations and Hilda purchase, not a house-sized fund.

The property detail table and UESP construction pages list additional Goldenhills upgrades: Kitchen, Dining Area, Alchemy and enchanting lab, Loft, Master Bedroom, Child's Bedroom, Library and display room, Apiary, Blacksmithing Workshop, Exterior Decoration, Farmhand Bunkhouse, Stable, Windmill, and the Goldenhills horse. These are named in the guide as later work rather than ignored. They are not bought in this first farm pass because the route has not yet created a storage/display policy or material haul for them, the child-room/library choice is mutually exclusive, and the Goldenhills page notes a crash risk around the lab upgrade.

The route selects Faendal as Goldenhills steward because the relationship-options table marks him as the route-default Goldenhills steward after `A Lovely Letter`, which was completed in Riverwood. Generic steward-hired farmhands remain the farmhand default; named candidates such as Angrenor, Brenuin, Embry, and Narfi are not relocated during this pass.

Rorikstead routing is included because the player is already using the Rorikstead/Goldenhills corridor. `Erik the Slayer` is safe to start and complete before the farm claim, then the farm wait supplies the elapsed time needed for Erik to reappear as a hireling. The guide avoids sleeping overnight at Frostfruit Inn after the quest because the quest page flags that as a bug risk.

`Pets of Skyrim` starts from the `For Sale` notice in the Bannered Mare. Because Hilda is bought from Halvar in Rorikstead, the notice is read before travel and Hilda is purchased after The Unquiet Dead so a pet cannot interfere with the first Goldenhills ghost state. `Halvar's Journal` is read at the same time. The remaining Pets of Skyrim animals are explicitly left for their later regions.

The Goldenhills copy of `Song of the Alchemists` is deliberately left closed. The progression-source selection table chooses the Anise's Cabin copy for the title, and the global skill-book policy keeps title reads for the Scholar's Insight reading window unless the route names an exception.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the Goldenhills quest order, hard-save placement, required farm operations, Hilda route, Erik route, and initial farm base use. The guide does not yet validate Goldenhills as primary long-term unique storage or display space; that remains with the later storage/material/property pass and final QA.
