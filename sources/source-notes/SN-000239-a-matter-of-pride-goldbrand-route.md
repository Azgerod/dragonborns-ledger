# A Matter of Pride Goldbrand Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000239

## Claim

The guide should route `A Matter of Pride` as a level-30-plus Goldbrand bundle tied to the existing Sacellum of Boethiah visit, not as an earlier Sivdur's Respite discovery or an unscheduled College note pickup.

For this route:

- Start `A Matter of Pride` by reading Eranya's Journal from Eranya's body at the Sacellum of Boethiah during the Boethiah route.
- Do not enter Sivdur's Respite before the Goldbrand quest is active.
- At Sivdur's Respite, kill the Boethiah cultists, read and take `Cultist's Note`, and read and take `Eranya's Notes on Sivdur's Tomb`.
- At the Arcanaeum, read and take `Eranya's Research Notes`, then kill the two invisible Boethiah cultists that appear.
- In Whiterun, use the Lars Battle-Born dialogue to obtain Sivdur's Amulet.
- In already-cleared Shroud Hearth Barrow, defeat Magrathi and take Magrathi's Skull.
- Return to Sivdur's Respite, place Sivdur's Amulet on the left pedestal and Magrathi's Skull on the right pedestal, enter Sivdur's tomb, retrieve Goldbrand, and preserve Goldbrand in owned storage.

Sivdur's Amulet and Magrathi's Skull are handled as quest keys for the Goldbrand route rather than preserved final-inventory unique items.

## Routing Relevance

This closes `OBJ-000622` `A Matter of Pride`, `OBJ-000753` `Goldbrand Unique Weapon`, `OBJ-001381` `Cultist's Note`, `OBJ-001388` `Eranya's Journal`, `OBJ-001389` `Eranya's Notes on Sivdur's Tomb`, `OBJ-001390` `Eranya's Research Notes`, `ITEM-001129`, `ITEM-001130`, `ITEM-001131`, and checklist rows `CHK-QUESTS-0601`, `CHK-BOOKS-2460`, `CHK-BOOKS-2461`, and `CHK-BOOKS-2462`.

It also moves the executable `Sivdur's Respite` discovery into the active quest route. This avoids the source-listed bug risk from entering Sivdur's Respite before starting the quest while still preserving the already counted `OBJ-002352` location discovery.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's `A Matter of Pride` page lists two quest-start sources: Eranya's Journal at the Sacellum of Boethiah and Eranya's Research Notes at the Arcanaeum. The same page gives the route sequence: find Eranya's trail, investigate Sivdur's Respite, read the College research notes, obtain Sivdur's Amulet from Lars Battle-Born in Whiterun, obtain Magrathi's Skull in Shroud Hearth Barrow, place both relics at Sivdur's Respite, and retrieve Goldbrand. It also records that the Lars dialogue checks cannot fail and warns that entering Sivdur's Respite before quest start may prevent normal quest start.

The individual book pages identify `Eranya's Journal` on Eranya's body, `Eranya's Notes on Sivdur's Tomb` at Sivdur's Respite, `Eranya's Research Notes` in the Arcanaeum, and `Cultist's Note` on a Boethiah Cultist in Sivdur's Respite. The Goldbrand item pages identify Goldbrand as the quest reward artifact and list Sivdur's Amulet and Magrathi's Skull as Goldbrand Creation items.

The Sivdur's Respite page confirms the same pedestal ordering as the quest page, identifies the tomb as requiring both relics, places Goldbrand on a table in Sivdur's Tomb, and records the honor-guard fight after taking Goldbrand. The Shroud Hearth Barrow page identifies Magrathi as a Goldbrand Creation occupant and lists `A Matter of Pride` as a related quest, supporting a direct revisit after the main route has already cleared `Lifting the Shroud`.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the quest start sources, all four document pickups, Lars Battle-Born amulet acquisition, Magrathi skull acquisition, Sivdur's Respite pedestal handling, Goldbrand reward boundary, and Goldbrand preservation treatment.

Confidence is high that Sivdur's Respite should not be entered before quest start because the quest page records a pre-start entry bug. The guide now moves the player-facing discovery into the active quest route instead of leaving it in a broad Eastmarch marker sweep.

There is no remaining open question for `A Matter of Pride` route placement in the current guide. The route intentionally does not branch or preserve Sivdur's Amulet and Magrathi's Skull as final inventory items because they are used as the quest's opening relics.
