# Source Note: AE Practical Equipment Member Expansion

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000071

## Claim

The current AE practical-equipment parent rows can be expanded into source-listed member coverage for Adventurer's Backpack, Camping, Arcane Archer Pack, Expanded Crossbow Pack, Elite Crossbows, Nordic Jewelry, and Fearsome Fists items.

## Routing Relevance

The specification requires AE systems, AE items, practical crafting-system unlocks, Survival Mode-relevant equipment, and a controlled Legendary difficulty power curve. Member-level coverage is needed before route planning can decide when to craft, buy, loot, or merely verify each equipment system without front-loading powerful or utility gear too early.
