# Source Note: Riften Thaneship, Frost, And Rift Roads Route

Status: reviewed for TB-035-MR-016 draft placement.

Source note ID: SN-000144

## Claim

The v1 guide can treat the post-Snow-Veil Rift pass as a broad geographic route rather than a narrow Riften property bucket. It should complete safe Riften city handoffs, Ivarstead-linked Riften quests, Rift road dungeons, Shor's Stone/Darkwater/Northwind work, southeast Rift clears, `Supply and Demand`, `Promises to Keep`, Honeyside, and Thane of the Rift while continuing to hold Dawnguard, Aetherium, late Thieves, level-gated, and separate AE bundles.

## Routing Relevance

This pass applies the current guide convention that nearby safe objectives should be routed when the player is already in the area. The section therefore pulls forward many rows that the earlier scaffold only implied: `Caught Red Handed`, `Under the Table`, `The Lover's Requital`, `Bow to the Master`, `Mine or Yours`, `Truth Ore Consequences`, `Special Delivery: Sylgja`, `Flight or Fight`, `Telrav's Request`, `Angarvunde`, `Infiltration`, `Repentance`, and multiple Rift clearable/non-clearable location rows. It keeps Frost late in the section so dangerous dragon/dungeon travel near Black-Briar Lodge is resolved before Frost exists on the main route.

## Evidence Summary

The Riften thane route requires `Supply and Demand`, additional citizen help, and Honeyside purchase. The section already enters with `The Raid` complete and enough Riften-area helper opportunities available, so it completes the city/Rift misc objectives before buying Honeyside. The guide requires 8,000 gold rather than relying on the lower-price bug noted by Honeyside sources, and it routes a normal sell-off from the long Rift dungeon pass before the purchase.

`Caught Red Handed`, `Under the Table`, and `The Lover's Requital` all start in Riften and progress naturally through Riften Stables, Black-Briar Meadery, and Ivarstead. The guide starts them just before the Ivarstead trip, completes Wilhelm's delivery and the Lynly identification there, then returns to Riften to finish Sibbi and Dinya's current stages. `The Book of Love` is advanced only through the Ivarstead Fastred stage because the next stage is in Markarth. `Pilgrimage` is accepted now but held for the next Whiterun-compatible route because its target is Andurs.

`Hunt and Gather` places Wylandriah's Spoon in Fellstar Farm, with ownership timing notes; the guide waits for a legal unlocked entry and takes only the Spoon now. The Windhelm and Winterhold items remain with their later regional stops.

The broad Ivarstead/Rift road pass routes Pinepeak, Honeystrand, Telrav/Nilheim, Rift Watchtower, Clearspring Tarn, Treva's Watch, Angarvunde, and Snapleg Cave because they are safe colocated clearable locations without a hard level or branch gate. Pinepeak and Honeystrand also begin the routed pelt supply for Temba's `Grin and Bear It`; the remaining pelts come from the later Broken Oar route before the Ivarstead turn-in. Treva's Watch supplies deterministic Bound Sword, Courage, and Detect Life spell tomes. Angarvunde supplies an Animal Allegiance word and requires Medresi's normal key handoff to avoid the source-listed key/progression bug.

Shor's Stone is bundled with `Mine or Yours`, `Truth Ore Consequences`, Sylgja's `Special Delivery`, the Grogmar mining activity, Shor's Watchtower, Darkwater Crossing, and Northwind. Darkwater Pass is conditional because the source lists a minimum level of 18 and contains Falmer/chaurus. If the player is below level 18, the guide leaves Derkeethus and Darkwater Pass for a later Eastmarch support route; otherwise it completes `Extracting an Argonian`, records Derkeethus as available, and clears the cave.

The southeast Riften road pass routes Heartwood Mill before Broken Helm Hollow to avoid the Leifnarr-body bug. It also routes Sarethi Farm, Snow-Shod Farm, Lost Tongue Overlook, The Shadow Stone discovery, Autumnshade Clearing, Boulderfall Cave, Darklight Tower/`Repentance`, and conditional Avanchnzel if the Lexicon is active. Skill books at those exact locations remain closed for the Scholar's Insight window.

Frost is intentionally placed near the end. `Promises to Keep` sources warn that travel near Black-Briar Lodge while the quest is active can expose Frost to dragon danger, and the project default is to keep Frost on the main save. The guide therefore discovers Lost Prospect Mine before starting Frost, uses the named hard save `HS-RIFT-FROST-LETRUSH` immediately before the Louis handoff, and keeps Frost through the persuade/threat outcome.

## Nearby Objective Audit And Deferrals

The pass does not start `Hard Answers`, `The Pursuit`, Riftweald Manor, Nightingale Hall, or any Delvin/Vex city jobs. These remain under the level-46 late Thieves chain and city-job restoration blocks.

The pass does not route Geirmund's Hall or `Lost Legends` because the project holds the Forbidden Legend-linked state until level 36. It does not route Forelhost, Largashbur/Fallowstone, Redwater Den, Ruunvald, Dayspring Canyon, Nightingale Hall, Faldar's Tooth/Bloodthirst, Crystaldrift, Champion's Rest, Rielle, Gromm's Pass, or high-risk separate AE spaces because those belong to Dawnguard, dragon priest, Daedric, late Thieves, or AE-specific bundles.

`Interception` is held even though Azadi can appear at Shor's Stone. Its Redguard Elite sequence quickly leaves the Rift for courier and cross-hold targets, and the source-listed start/bug behavior deserves a dedicated AE route. Sondas's note and Annekke's bandit job at Darkwater Crossing are held because their next natural progress is Eastmarch/Windhelm support rather than the current Rift thaneship loop.

`Grimsever's Return` remains held because it sends the player to Mzinchaleft in the northern Dwemer route. `Spread the Love` remains unavailable until `The Book of Love` is complete. `Distant Memories`, Bolli's Markarth delivery, and several Riften ingredient favors remain active for their later destination or ingredient bundles. Shadowfoot Sanctum remains held because this pass spends the normal property budget on Honeyside, while Shadowfoot has a separate 7,500-gold AE-home/display role.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the Riften city quest inclusions, Cragslane/Honeyside sequence, Frost hard-save policy, Shor's Stone/Darkwater/Northwind bundle, Treva's Watch spell-tome routing, Angarvunde handling, Broken Helm/Grosta bug mitigation, and Darklight/Illia route. Confidence is medium for routing Darkwater Pass in this section because the cave is source-listed minimum level 18; the guide therefore uses a level check. No TB-035-MR-016 `NEEDS ROUTE RESOLUTION` notes remain.
