# Source Note: Side, DLC, and AE Choice Branches

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000099

## Claim

Several non-primary-faction quest choices have mutually exclusive rewards, follow-on content, or completionist state changes. They should be recorded as hard-save branches when they have substantial exclusive content or unique rewards, and as option-list decisions when they are isolated preference outcomes.

## Routing Relevance

This supports the TB-014 table's split between full branch routes and option-list decisions. It does not choose every final default; unresolved defaults are handed to the branch matrix and route skeleton work.

## Evidence Summary

UESP records that `Lost to the Ages` allows only one Aetherial item to be forged from the Crown, Shield, or Staff. The Thirsk pages record a mutually exclusive Riekling-side versus Nord-side outcome with follow-on favor availability. `Unearthed` ends with a Ralis kill/spare choice: sparing him makes him a follower, while killing him still allows Hoarfrost to be looted. `Forgotten Names` has a Velehk release/kill choice with different reward sets. `Promises to Keep`, `In My Time Of Need`, and `The Blessings of Nature` have meaningful but isolated quest outcome choices.

For AE content, `A Dying Wish` has three Bittercup paths: Fortune, Power, and Nothing, each leading to different follow-on rewards. The source reward table lists Master Transmute for the Fortune path only, Grand Champion's Sword and Grand Champion's Helm for the Power path only, and Ironwood Soup cooking, Rulnik as a follower, and Rulnik's Dagger for the Nothing path only. This makes Bittercup a branch-worthy three-way choice rather than a pure preference fork. `Battle of the Champions` can be entered through either Civil War side; current source-note coverage treats both faction equipment sets as obtainable after the battle, but TB-030/TB-033 should verify final checklist availability before relying on that as route coverage. The player-selected champion side still affects quest staging and commendation. `Ghosts of the Tribunal` has a heretic-join path that grants armory access, crafting permissions, followers, and the companion tasks; destroying the heretics still permits other quests through looted notes but UESP says there is no armory key and Skullcrusher is blocked. `Her Word Against Theirs` further varies Ashfall's Tear upgrades depending on propaganda distribution.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high that these are real choice points. TB-028 selected conservative canonical defaults where the existing source-backed data supported them: Nord-side Thirsk, Aetherial Crown, Bittercup Fortune path, Velehk release, Imperial-aligned Battle of the Champions, Frost kept by the player, Ralis spared, and Ghosts of the Tribunal join/infiltrate. Isolated moral choices such as In My Time of Need and The Blessings of Nature remain option-list defaults rather than full branches.
