## Route Decisions

The earlier Bards/northern-road section now includes a small Dawnstar insertion before Dark Brotherhood contract acceptance: discover Dawnstar, mine one iron ore at Iron-Breaker Mine, and sell it to Beitild. This is required because UESP records that Beitild pays for iron ore normally, but `Kill Beitild` blocks normal ore-sale dialogue while the contract is active. The route therefore handles the Beitild mine-sale checklist row before Nazir's first set of contracts, while the actual assassination remains with the Brotherhood contract loop.

`Kill Narfi` remains safe in the first contract loop because `The Straw that Broke` was completed in the southern warm corridor. The same Ivarstead return is now used to finish Temba's `Grin and Bear It` with exactly ten pelts collected from routed bear locations before killing Narfi. Narfi's non-default Goldenhills farmhand option is closed by the assassination, but the route already chose generic farmhands as the farm default. The player-facing text states the practical consequence once, at the kill point.

Anga's Mill is expanded beyond the Ennodius target because the route is physically there and the next stop is Dawnstar. Aeri's firewood sale, Aeri's Note, Aeri's delivery to Skald, and Aeri's non-default marriage availability are safe, local, and directly sequenced with Dawnstar. Ennodius is killed while awake or outside the tent because UESP records kill-registration and sleeping-body bugs; the guide uses a rotating save before the kill.

Dawnstar is not otherwise expanded here. Beitild's ore sale was already handled before the contract, and Beitild's assassination is a quick contract action. Dawnstar's Daedric, Pale thane/property, museum, Vaermina, Pale Blade, merchant investment, and broader cold-region objectives remain with the later Pale/Dawnstar section, where their level gates and cold-travel routing can stay coherent.

`Mourning Never Comes` is started only after at least one first contract is completed and the Sanctuary return scene gives Astrid's Markarth assignment. The guide reports all three first contracts to Nazir after Astrid starts the main contract, because UESP records that Nazir denies reward turn-ins before Astrid approaches. The section stops after `Whispers in the Dark` starts; the next continuation owns Erikur protection and must resolve `The Dainty Sload` before `Bound Until Death`.

The route chooses Muiri's optional Nilsine objective. This preserves Muiri's Ring, unlocks Muiri as a non-default marriage option, and matches the maximalist reward policy. `Blood on the Ice` has already been completed in the guide before this point, satisfying the source warning to resolve Windhelm's murder investigation before killing Nilsine. Nilsine's key is used to enter the House of Clan Shatter-Shield and collect the Stone of Barenziah from her bedroom. Tova's later suicide state is accepted after the Windhelm-critical rows that depended on her family state have already been routed or held intentionally.

The Clan Shatter-Shield Office and `Suvaris Atheron's Logbook` are not pulled into this house visit. The logbook belongs to `Rise in the East` and sits in a different Windhelm business location, so routing it here would split the related East Empire Company/Japhet's Folly bundle. Torbjorn's Amulet of Arkay favor also remains held because the route does not have a deterministic spare amulet source at this point and Rare Gifts variants are being controlled separately.

Raldbthar is expanded from the old Aetherium handoff. The guide now bundles Alain Dufont, Aegisbane, Raldbthar discovery/clear, the Raldbthar Aetherium Shard, and the Great Lift at Raldbthar. It does not activate the Blackreach mechanism because the guide has not yet routed Septimus Signus, the Attunement Sphere, or the Blank Lexicon. `2920, Last Seed, v8` remains closed because the route is still before the Scholar's Insight reading window and the current source-selection table does not require this copy.

## Coverage Notes

This pass directly places Beitild's Dawnstar mine-sale variant, Dawnstar discovery, `Grin and Bear It`, `Kill Narfi`, `Kill Ennodius Papius`, `Kill Beitild`, Anga's Mill discovery, Aeri firewood, Aeri's Note, Aeri's delivery, `Mourning Never Comes`, House of Clan Shatter-Shield Stone of Barenziah, Raldbthar, Great Lift at Raldbthar, Aegisbane, Raldbthar Aetherium Shard, Muiri's Ring, and Muiri's non-default marriage option.

Held or staged rows are mapped in coverage rather than exposed as player-facing lists: Dawnstar Daedric/property/merchant/Pale content, `Suvaris Atheron's Logbook` and `Rise in the East`, Torbjorn's Amulet of Arkay favor, Blackreach/Septimus access through Raldbthar, Raldbthar skill-book copies, the remaining Lost to the Ages shard and forge work, and Erikur-sensitive `The Dainty Sload` before `Bound Until Death`.
