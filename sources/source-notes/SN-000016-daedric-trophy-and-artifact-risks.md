# Source Note: Daedric Trophy and Artifact Risks

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000016

## Claim

Daedric Influence requires acquiring a Daedric Artifact, and Oblivion Walker requires collecting 15 Daedric Artifacts. Oblivion Walker can be missed because some Daedric quests can be completed without a qualifying artifact, some can fail, and some relevant NPCs can die before quest start.

## Routing Relevance

The final route must preserve PS4 trophies and choose artifact-awarding outcomes. This note supports trophy relevance and missability flags in Daedric objective rows, but it does not decide final branch routing yet.

## Evidence Summary

UESP identifies Daedric Influence as acquiring one Daedric Artifact and Oblivion Walker as collecting 15 Daedric Artifacts. It notes that several quests can be completed without receiving a qualifying artifact, several can be failed, and two have NPC-before-start risks. It also notes that the Skeleton Key does not count for these achievements and that Creation Club Daedric artifacts do not count toward them.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the trophy requirements and broad missability categories. Exact PS4 trophy timing, all artifact-counter edge cases, and final artifact-maximizing choices require later trophy/conflict validation.
