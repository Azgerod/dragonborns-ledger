# Crypt of the Heart Spell Knight Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000244

## Claim

`Crypt of the Heart` should be routed inside the existing level-46 Karthspire/Sky Haven pass, starting from the Silver-Blood Inn in Markarth and finishing before entering The Karthspire for `Alduin's Wall`.

For this route:

- Read and take `Crypt of the Heart - Draft` at the Silver-Blood Inn before leaving the Markarth support stop.
- Visit the bridge near Reachcliff Secret Entrance, follow the Ghost Knight toward Simon Rodayne's grave west of Dushnikh Yal, then make `HARD SAVE: HS-SPELL-KNIGHT-GRAVE` before the grave fight.
- At the grave, defeat the Forsworn, read/take `Forsworn Shaman's Note`, read/take `Knight Captain's Orders`, and loot one full Iron Spell Knight set and one full Steel Spell Knight set from the dead Knights of the Crypt when they spawn correctly.
- Clear Karthspire Camp while the quest is active, including the Beldama Witches and Beldama Ward Mother encounter area; read/take `Beldama Witch's Note`, take the `Corrupted Human Heart`, and leave the unrelated `A Dance in Fire, v2` skill book closed for the guide's later selected copy.
- Return to Simon Rodayne's grave and make `HARD SAVE: HS-SPELL-KNIGHT-HEART`.
- Branch first through the corrupted-heart outcome: place the corrupted heart without using the Shrine of Mara, defeat Simon's hostile ghost, loot the offensive enchanted Ebony Spell Knight set, then reload `HS-SPELL-KNIGHT-HEART`.
- Continue the main route through the purified-heart outcome: activate the Shrine of Mara to transform the heart into `Simon Rodayne's Heart`, place it in the grave, take the defensive enchanted Ebony Spell Knight set, and verify the quest completes.
- If either Knight of the Crypt does not spawn or an Iron/Steel Spell Knight set is missing, reload the grave hard save once; if the bug persists, use the quest's post-completion crafting unlock to craft the missing Iron/Steel pieces at a forge.
- Craft one unenchanted Ebony Spell Knight set later in the all-perks Smithing loop when Ebony Smithing, materials, and storage are clean.
- Do not consume the one-time Spell Knight hearts for alchemy. The Creation page says the heart variants appear in the Ingredients category but cannot be used to create potions at an alchemy table, and the individual heart pages show the same four effects as normal Human Heart and Mort Flesh. The route handles those effect discoveries through the shared-effect alchemy policy rather than destroying the quest heart state.

## Routing Relevance

This closes `OBJ-000646` `Crypt of the Heart`, `CHK-QUESTS-0629`, `CHK-BOOKS-2453`, `OBJ-000730` `Spell Knight Armor Parent Set`, document objectives `OBJ-001351`, `OBJ-001404`, and `OBJ-001429`, alchemy-effect policy rows `OBJ-002712` and `OBJ-002716`, and item rows `ITEM-000895` through `ITEM-000916`.

The route belongs in `Karthspire, Sky Haven, and Dragonbane` because that section already sends the player to Markarth support, Karthspire Camp, Karthspire, and Sky Haven Temple at level 46+. Starting the quest in Markarth immediately before the Karthspire Camp clear prevents a shallow "discover now, do later" pass, keeps the Spell Knight documents with their quest, and resolves the alternate Ebony reward branch before continuing the main quest.

The branch is required because the corrupted and purified heart choices produce different enchanted Ebony Spell Knight reward variants and different quest outcome scenes. The purified-heart route is the main continuity because it preserves the peaceful outcome and still gives a full enchanted Ebony set; the corrupted-heart reward is branch-experienced and reloaded.

## Evidence Summary

The quest page places the start at `Crypt of the Heart - Draft` in the Silver-Blood Inn and sends the player from the Karth River ghost sighting to Simon Rodayne's grave, then to Karthspire Camp for the corrupted heart, and back to the grave for the heart choice. It records that the corrupt-heart outcome requires defeating Simon's ghost and looting the offensive Ebony Spell Knight set, while the purified-heart outcome uses the Shrine of Mara and gives the defensive Ebony Spell Knight set.

The item page records that the defensive Ebony Spell Knight set appears at Simon Rodayne's grave after cleansing the heart, the offensive Ebony set can be taken from the grave after defeating Simon's ghost, and unenchanted Ebony/Iron/Steel Spell Knight armor can be crafted after completing `Crypt of the Heart`. The Knight of the Crypt page records one dead Knight in full Iron Spell Knight armor and another in full Steel Spell Knight armor at Simon's grave, with `Knight Captain's Orders` on the Knights.

The document pages record `Beldama Witch's Note` on a Beldama Witch east of Karthspire Camp, `Forsworn Shaman's Note` on the Forsworn Shaman near the Shrine of Mara, and `Knight Captain's Orders` on the Knights of the Crypt. These deterministic pickups keep the AE documents inside the quest route rather than treating Karthspire adjacency as enough.

The quest page records two relevant bugs: the Knights may fail to spawn around the grave, and the quest can complete after three reward armor pieces, preventing the fourth from being picked up. The route therefore uses hard saves before the grave and before the heart choice, gives a reload condition for reward pickup failure, and uses the post-completion crafting unlock as a fallback only for missing Iron/Steel armor.

The alchemy policy uses two source facts together. The Creation page says the heart variants appear in the Ingredients inventory category but cannot be used to create potions at an alchemy table. The individual heart pages list the same four effects as normal Human Heart, the other Spell Knight heart variant, and Mort Flesh. Because both Spell Knight hearts are one-time quest-state items and the effects are duplicated elsewhere, the route does not consume them; it treats their alchemy-effect rows as covered by the shared-effect discovery policy while the quest route represents the items themselves.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the quest start, route order, document pickups, heart branch, reward variants, crafting unlock, and hard-save need.

Confidence is medium-high for the alchemy policy because UESP's Creation summary and individual ingredient pages describe the heart variants from different angles: the Creation page says they cannot create potions at an alchemy table, while the individual pages preserve the effect list and combinations. The route resolves that conflict by not consuming the one-time hearts and by routing equivalent effect discovery through Human Heart or Mort Flesh.

There is no remaining open route question for `OBJ-000646`, `OBJ-000730`, `OBJ-001351`, `OBJ-001404`, or `OBJ-001429`. The Spell Knight heart rows remain source-listed alchemy support records, but they no longer require an unresolved exact-consumption route.
