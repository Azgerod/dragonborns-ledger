# Source Note: Stone Power Choice Sets

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000047

## Claim

Standing Stones and All-Maker Stones grant power or ability choices that affect a maximalist route and should be represented in the objective database before route placement and default recommendations are decided.

## Routing Relevance

Standing Stones have trophy and leveling-plan relevance, while All-Maker Stones are tied to Dragonborn progression and Solstheim cleanup. This pass records the power systems at source-list level only; default stone choices, reuse policy, and travel-efficient routing are deferred.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's Standing Stone page identifies the thirteen Standing Stones and notes that only one stone effect is normally active at a time. UESP's All-Maker Stones and Powers pages identify the six Solstheim stone powers and describe them as single-use powers that can be regained from the corresponding cleansed stone.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high that these power systems need coverage. Default Standing Stone recommendations, All-Maker power use/recharge policy, Survival Mode travel implications, and final checklist cue placement remain deferred.
