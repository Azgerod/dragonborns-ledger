## Route Decisions

Sky Haven Temple entry is routed only after level 46 because the project constraints and UESP warning treat first Sky Haven entry as the Dragonbane lock point. The guide therefore starts the section with a level-46 requirement and then treats the Karthspire/Sky Haven route as normal main-quest progress.

The guide routes Karthspire Camp and Karthspire together because the source page lists two map markers and the main quest physically passes both. It tells the player to kill all Forsworn before entering the cave because UESP records stuck-combat behavior if Delphine and Esbern remain engaged with Karthspire Camp enemies. `A Dance in Fire, v2` is not read there because the progression-source table currently selects the Fort Snowhawk copy for that skill-book title and the route has not reached the Scholar's Insight reading window.

Inside Sky Haven Temple, Dragonbane, the Blades armor chest, one Blades Sword, and `Remanada` are routed immediately. These are safe, directly colocated, and potentially sensitive to later Blades/Paarthurnax ownership state. `Mace Etiquette` is left closed because the selected source is the Fort Greenwall Captain's Quarters copy and this route has not reached the skill-book reading window.

The guide does not start `Rebuilding the Blades`, `Dragon Hunting`, or `Dragon Research` in this section. Those objectives are available around this state but require a follower-recruitment plan, random dragon-lair assignment handling, and a route decision on whether Dragon Infusion belongs on main continuity before the Elder Scroll step of `Alduin's Bane` or only on the Blades branch. The next main-quest coordination work must resolve that before the Elder Scroll/Paarthurnax path closes the main-continuity potion window.

`Crypt of the Heart` is not pulled into this section despite Karthspire Camp adjacency. It starts from `Crypt of the Heart - Draft` in Markarth, uses the Karthspire Camp/Beldama stage, then branches on a one-time heart transformation that affects Corrupted Human Heart, Simon Rodayne's Heart, and two enchanted Ebony Spell Knight Armor reward variants. That is a concrete reward/ingredient policy reason to keep it in a dedicated Spell Knight route rather than quietly consuming the heart during the Sky Haven gate.

## Coverage Summary

This pass places `Alduin's Wall`, Karthspire Camp, Karthspire, Sky Haven Temple, maximum Dragonbane, Blades Armor, Blades Boots, Blades Gauntlets, Blades Helmet, Blades Shield, Blades Sword, and `Remanada`.

Rows intentionally staged with concrete reasons: `Mace Etiquette`, `A Dance in Fire, v2`, `Rebuilding the Blades`, `Dragon Hunting`, `Dragon Research`, Dragon Infusion, `Crypt of the Heart`, `Crypt of the Heart - Draft`, Spell Knight Armor, Beldama Witch's Note, Forsworn Shaman's Note, Knight Captain's Orders, Corrupted Human Heart alchemy effects, and Simon Rodayne's Heart alchemy effects.

No TB-035-MR-038 unresolved route rows are introduced by this pass.
