## Route Decisions

Ysolda is married immediately before the Dawnguard fork because `The Gift` needs a valid non-vampire spouse on the Volkihar branch, and Ysolda's Mammoth Tusk favor was already completed earlier. The guide chooses Ysolda's House as the temporary residence so the branch has a simple spouse target and does not move the spouse into a Hearthfire home before the later household/display section.

The route enters Dawnguard directly through Fort Dawnguard rather than relying on a city rumor or random Durak encounter. This preserves the guide's positive-routing style and avoids treating random hooks as route instructions. The Rift bounty caveat is retained because the UESP quest page calls it out as an access blocker.

`Awakening` is main-continuity content. The guide therefore routes Dimhollow Crypt directly, including `Notes on Dimhollow Crypt, Vol. 3`, Elder Scroll (Sun) in Serana's custody, `Spell Tome: Necromantic Healing`, the first `Drain Vitality` word, and the Dimhollow clear. The follower-dismissal instruction is a route-safe prevention for the UESP-listed Serana/follower issue when opening the monolith.

The guide buys `Spell Tome: Heal Undead` before the branch save, during the first Castle Volkihar entry. UESP lists Ronthil as the only source and notes that he is normally unavailable on the Dawnguard path, with a narrow first-entry interaction window before Harkon speaks. Because the final route is Dawnguard-aligned, the guide uses that canonical purchase window instead of counting a Volkihar-branch-only spell learn.

`HS-DG-BLOODLINE` remains the branch boundary after the Ronthil purchase. The Volkihar branch accepts Harkon's gift and treats Vampire Lord, faction services, any lycanthropy cure, Volkihar followers, Redwater Den progress, the vampire-side Prophet route, and spouse conversion as branch-only. The canonical route reloads and refuses Harkon, completing `Bloodline` on Dawnguard continuity and starting `A New Order`.

`Amulets of Night Power` requires the Summon Gargoyle Vampire Lord perk, so the guide adds a limited branch-only Vampire Lord prerequisite grind before Feran's amulet quest. This is not the full `Vampire Mastered` route; the full transformation tree remains staged for the later transformation section.

Volkihar random assignments are handled by assignment type rather than by default target. The guide names the possible `New Allegiances` targets, uses direct target handling for rings, amulets, remains, hunts, and city framing, and tells the player not to route unrelated nearby main-save objectives while on the branch.

`Destroying the Dawnguard` is not routed in this section because the source places it after Volkihar-side `Kindred Judgment`. The guide keeps `HS-DG-BLOODLINE` for a later Volkihar finale branch continuation rather than marking this row unresolved or forcing an impossible early completion.

The nearby-objective audit deliberately does not route Stendarr's Beacon during the Dayspring entry. The location involves hostile Vigilant clear behavior, local skill-book/counter implications, and no immediate Dawnguard-fork dependency. It is better kept for a later explicit location/clear policy than split awkwardly into a simple discovery stop.

Redwater Den, Forebears' Holdout, `Vampire's Note`, and any branch-only rewards or locations are not counted as main-continuity completions after reload. Main-route versions of overlapping Dawnguard Prophet or location/document rows must be routed again on the Dawnguard continuity where applicable.

This pass places the route-default marriage, Dawnguard opening, Fort Dawnguard discovery, `Awakening`, Dimhollow Crypt, `Notes on Dimhollow Crypt, Vol. 3`, Elder Scroll (Sun), `Spell Tome: Necromantic Healing`, `Drain Vitality: Gaan`, Icewater Jetty, Castle Volkihar, canonical `Spell Tome: Heal Undead` purchase from Ronthil before Harkon, `HS-DG-BLOODLINE`, Volkihar branch membership, `The Bloodstone Chalice`, branch `Prophet`, Vampire Lord/Summon Gargoyle branch prerequisite progress, `Rings of Blood Magic`, `Amulets of Night Power`, four `Ancient Power` turn-ins, one representative `Culling the Beast`, `Deceiving the Herd`, `The Hunt`, `Protecting the Bloodline`, one successful `New Allegiances`, `The Gift`, reload, Dawnguard-side `Bloodline`, and `A New Order` start.

Rows intentionally staged out of this section: `Destroying the Dawnguard` for a later Volkihar-side finale branch continuation; Stendarr's Beacon for a later explicit clear/location pass; Redwater Den and Forebears' Holdout main-continuity location rows for future main-route handling where applicable.
