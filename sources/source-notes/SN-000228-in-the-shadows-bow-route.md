# In the Shadows and Bow of Shadows Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000228

## Claim

`In the Shadows` should be routed from the level-8 Whiterun/Silent Moons pass: make `HARD SAVE: HS-BOW-OF-SHADOWS`, start the quest with the current Whiterun steward in Dragonsreach, read/take `Steward's Note`, read/take `Business Ledger Copy` at Arcadia's Cauldron, inspect the Hall of the Dead poison evidence while leaving the poisoned cheese uneaten and unpreserved, read/take `Stable Hand's Note` at Whiterun Stables, read/take `Assassin's Journal` during the already gated Silent Moons Camp clear, then return to Dragonsreach while the Jarl is not in an occupied main-quest or Civil War scene, kill the protected assassin with the player's own final blow, loot and preserve `Bow of Shadows`, and report to the steward.

## Routing Relevance

This closes the Bow of Shadows Creation package route, the `In the Shadows` quest row, the Bow of Shadows unique-item parent, the two checklist-tracked AE book rows, and the low-severity note rows without weakening the existing level-8 Silent Moons gate for Lunar weapon handling.

The route keeps the final assassin step out of active Jarl-occupied scenes because UESP records that the assassin does not appear while the Jarl is occupied and lists specific affected stages for `Bleak Falls Barrow`, `Dragon Rising`, `The Fallen`, and the major Civil War battle quests. UESP also records a confirmed no-assassin bug with PC-only console workarounds; the PS4 guide therefore uses a named hard save before starting the quest and a rotating save before the upper-floor Dragonsreach check.

The route does not change the selected `Detect Life` spell source. UESP notes that the dead stable hand carries a Detect Life tome, but the current guide's progression-source table already handles spell tomes through the later selected-source pass. The player may loot the body for the note, but the stable-hand tome is left unread unless a later progression-source pass deliberately selects it.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's `In the Shadows` quest page starts the quest by asking the current Whiterun steward, usually Proventus Avenicci or Brill, if he has work. The steward gives `Steward's Note`, which sends the player to Arcadia's Cauldron. The Arcadia step places `Business Ledger Copy` on the counter and the quest page says this copy can be picked up, unlike ordinary business ledgers.

The ledger points to a Priest of Arkay and a Nord stable boy. The quest page routes the Hall of the Dead and Whiterun Catacombs evidence first, including a dead skeever, poison, cheese, and a warning note. The Creation page lists `Poisoned Sliced Goat Cheese` as a Bow of Shadows content item, while `Skeever Trouble` supplies the warning-note context. The guide treats the poisoned cheese as quest evidence and a food hazard, not as a preserved unique reward or Survival food.

The stables step sends the player to a dead Nord behind a haystack of the leftmost stall at Whiterun Stables. UESP records that the dead Nord carries a note and a `Detect Life` spell tome, with a lunar steel weapon nearby. The route reads/takes `Stable Hand's Note`, optionally takes a nearby Lunar-named weapon as part of the Lunar weapon pool, and leaves the Detect Life tome unread because it is not the selected spell-source row.

The Silent Moons step places `Assassin's Journal` on a table just outside the room containing the Lunar Forge. This makes the level-8 Silent Moons route the natural insertion point: the current guide already enters the camp at level 8+ to preserve Lunar weapon handling and already records exact Lunar weapon forms without save-rerolling.

After the journal, UESP sends the player to the upper floor and balcony of Dragonsreach. The assassin is invisible until the player gets close, can be lured downstairs for help, but is protected, so the killing blow must be dealt by the player. The assassin carries `Bow of Shadows`; after looting it, the quest completes by speaking to the steward for leveled gold.

UESP records that the assassin will not appear if the Jarl is occupied or if `Bleak Falls Barrow` has not been started. It lists occupied Jarl states in `Bleak Falls Barrow`, `Dragon Rising`, `The Fallen`, and the major Civil War battle quests, then records a confirmed bug where the assassin does not appear and the listed recovery is PC console-only. Because the route is for PS4, the guide adds `HS-BOW-OF-SHADOWS` before starting the quest and a rotating save before the Dragonsreach upper-floor check. If the assassin or quest marker is absent, the guide instructs the player to reload and leave the issue at the named save rather than relying on unavailable console recovery.

The Bow of Shadows item page confirms the reward is acquired through `In the Shadows`, is an artifact bow, and casts invisibility for 30 seconds while also granting faster draw speed. It also records confirmed bow bugs affecting Quick Shot stacking, missing smithing material keywords, and possible invisibility failure. These bugs do not change the acquisition route, but the guide preserves the bow instead of using it as an enchantment-learning source or relying on its invisibility for route-critical stealth.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the steward start, document locations, Hall/stables/Silent Moons investigation path, assassin location, player-killing-blow requirement, Bow of Shadows reward, and preservation policy.

The unresolved operational risk is the PS4-relevant no-assassin spawn bug. The route mitigates it with a named hard save and explicit Jarl-state check, but cannot provide the PC console recovery UESP lists. If live playtesting later proves the current level-8 timing still fails consistently on PS4, the route should be moved to the earliest post-`Bleak Falls Barrow`-started window and the Silent Moons/Lunar weapon level gate should be re-audited.
