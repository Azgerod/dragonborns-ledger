# SN-000151 - High Hrothgar, Ustengrav, Morthal, And Embassy Route

Status: route-writing source note for TB-035-MR-023.

## Scope

This note supports the v1 guide section `High Hrothgar, Ustengrav, Morthal, And The Embassy`. The section routes the main quest from the first Greybeards visit through `A Cornered Rat`, then parks `Alduin's Wall` before Karthspire and first Sky Haven Temple entry. The retrospective audit pulled forward colocated Morthal/Hjaalmarch work because Ustengrav already brings the player to Morthal: `Laid to Rest`, Movarth's Lair, Hjaalmarch thane help, Myrwatch, Stonehills, Windstad Manor land purchase/foundation, and linked Solitude/Whiterun delivery turn-ins. It still preserves the level-46 Sky Haven/Dragonbane gate and leaves broader Bards, Solitude, Blades, and full Hearthfire construction/service content to later sections.

## Route Decisions

The section begins from Windhelm. Adonato's Book is delivered to Giraud before the Morthal segment because the route accepted that book in Windhelm and the generic Delivery framework allows only one active delivery objective at a time. Clearing Adonato first leaves Idgrod's Note available for the Morthal/Hjaalmarch pass.

Klimmek's `Climb the Steps` is routed during the first High Hrothgar climb because the start, supply chest, and return objective are directly attached to that main-quest movement. The Greybeards sequence is routed as a complete training block. The guide tells the player not to leave during training because source notes record main-quest-breaking or training-stall bugs if the High Hrothgar shout lessons are interrupted or if the courtyard state does not reset correctly. The Horn is returned before pushing Kynesgrove so the player gets Dah, formal recognition, and the Words of Power trophy before entering the longer Delphine/Embassy/Ratway sequence.

The retrospective audit moved Morthal/Hjaalmarch work into this section because Ustengrav already sends the player to Morthal. `Laid to Rest` is safe at this point, unlocks Windstad Manor purchase, and naturally routes Alva's Journal, Movarth's Lair, and Movarth's Boots. Movarth's Lair is marked as resolved rather than cleared because UESP records that the cave has no clearable state. The guide avoids killing Alva if she appears non-hostile in the cave and leaves `2920, Sun's Dawn, v2` closed under the skill-book policy.

Hjaalmarch thane work is completed through Benor, Jorgen, and Gestur because those help targets are deterministic and clustered around Morthal/Stonehills. Gorm's Letter and Pactur's Payment Delivery are accepted now and turned in during the next Solitude stop. Idgrod's Note is accepted only after Adonato's delivery is clear, then delivered as an isolated errand to the actual active recipient under the later random-assignment convention. Falion's Secret is routed now because it is local and finite. Rockwallow Mine is entered only for Gestur's ore sale; its `Orsinium and the Orcs` skill-book copy is named as closed and left for the later reading window.

Myrwatch is pulled into this same Morthal loop because it is directly east of Morthal, has no hard level gate, and gives an owned rest/crafting stop. The guide routes Hans's Journal, the fire-seal puzzle, Anora's Journal, property ownership, the staff enchanter, and the All Forge. It names the Myrwatch `Catalogue of Weapon Enchantments` and `Chimarvamidium` copies as closed skill books because the route enters the same home before the Scholar's Insight reading window. It explicitly avoids using Myrwatch displays as final storage because the place page records display/storage issues relevant to unique-item preservation.

Windstad Manor land is purchased and its foundation is built after `Laid to Rest` and Hjaalmarch thane work because the land is unlocked by the same route bundle and lies north of Ustengrav. Full Windstad wings, steward assignment, carriage driver, bard, horse, fish hatchery, furnishings, and household setup stay with the later Hearthfire construction/service pass because those decisions are better handled as one property system route rather than a partial one-room visit.

Kynesgrove routes `A Blade in the Dark` and then Iddra's `A Few Words with You`. Iddra is included because the dragon attack sends the player directly to Kynesgrove, the favor resolves locally through Roggi, and helping Iddra also unlocks free Braidwood Inn beds. Dravynea's Frost Salts was already handled in the Eastmarch follow-up route; Roggi's Ancestral Shield is still held because it is a randomized dungeon retrieval and the route should not ask the player to manipulate RNG or accept an unplanned target.

The Embassy route uses Razelan for the party distraction. This avoids using Erikur/Brelas because later Solitude/Thieves/Dark Brotherhood timing depends on cleaner Solitude NPC state. The guide names the invitation, three Thalmor dossiers, and `Dragon Investigation: Current Status` at their actual acquisition points. It tells the player to save before advancing the dungeon evidence because Malborn must survive for the later Windhelm `Find the Thalmor Assassin` objective.

The Reeking Cave Stone of Barenziah is routed during the Embassy escape because UESP explicitly notes that this is the easiest access window after patch 1.4. `Before the Ages of Man` is left closed in the same cave because it is a skill book. The guide does not try to re-enter the Embassy or cave later for this stone.

`A Cornered Rat` uses the previously opened Thieves Guild/Ragged Flagon route. The guide still tells the player to speak with Vekel even if Esbern's marker is already available, because UESP ties the clean Gissur exposure to getting Esbern's whereabouts from Vekel. Gissur and Shavari notes are conditional because their carriers can depend on the exact quest and survival state, but the guide tells the player what to do if they appear. Esbern's room routes `The Wolf Queen, v6` and `Annals of the Dragonguard` after the Riverwood escort. `Children of the Sky` is not routed here because the guide already acquired a checklist copy during the earlier Brinewater/Gulum-Ei route.

`Alduin's Wall` is intentionally parked after Esbern reaches Riverwood and explains Sky Haven Temple. The guide does not send the player to Karthspire, lower any Sky Haven puzzles, enter Sky Haven Temple, collect Blades gear, take Dragonbane, or start Blades rebuilding. This preserves the level-46 Dragonbane gate and the later Paarthurnax/Blades branch policy.
