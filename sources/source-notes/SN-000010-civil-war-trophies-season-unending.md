# Source Note: Civil War Trophies and Season Unending Coordination

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000010

## Claim

The Civil War trophies are Taking Sides, War Hero, and Hero of Skyrim. War Hero can be missed if Season Unending skips the relevant fort battle, and Season Unending can put Civil War progress on hold until the main quest is complete.

## Routing Relevance

The final route must preserve PS4 trophies and coordinate the Civil War with the main quest. War Hero is specifically called out by the specification as a warning-layer risk, so Civil War objective rows and the Season Unending row need this source note before routing begins.

## Evidence Summary

UESP lists Taking Sides as joining either Civil War faction, War Hero as capturing Fort Sungard or Fort Greenwall, and Hero of Skyrim as capturing Solitude or Windhelm. UESP notes that War Hero's associated quest can be skipped depending on Season Unending. UESP also notes that after Season Unending the Civil War is on hold until the main quest is complete.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high that War Hero requires explicit route protection. The exact safest Imperial-side handling, whether to avoid Season Unending entirely or force a safe negotiation state, and PS4-specific trophy timing still need later trophy and conflict-table validation.
