## Route decisions

The guide now starts Ghosts of the Tribunal before the Fahlbtharz north-coast route by reading `Heretic Dossier: Blacksmith's Confessional` in Raven Rock Temple. That lets the player enter Fahlbtharz Forge during the existing Fahlbtharz visit and acquire Hand Kenro's note, disguise, Unenchanted Weapon, first Fahlbtharz Forge Gem, and Pyroil Tar without creating a later Fahlbtharz revisit.

The branch handling is split into two named saves because UESP ties two different route states to Ghosts content. `HS-AE-GHOSTS-TEMPLE` protects the broad join/infiltrate versus destroy-heretics outcome. `HS-AE-GHOSTS-PROPAGANDA` protects the separate `Her Word Against Theirs` recipient choice: the main route distributes letters to Raven Rock civilians, including Geldis Sadri, for the restored service state, while the branch gives letters to the Reclamation Priest so the later attack and `Reclamation Priest's Journal` can be recorded without poisoning the canonical temple state.

The main continuity uses the join/infiltrate path because it preserves Tribunal Armory Key access, Skull Crusher, Hopesfire, the armory gem, crafting permissions, Ashfall services, and the four follower options. The guide delays the craftable Her Hand/Indoril equipment outputs to the later Smithing block because the current route has not guaranteed the Elven Smithing/material state for every player, but it names the unlocked surface and the ordinary Ghosts equipment set explicitly.

Stony Creek Cave remains cleared from the earlier Bards College route. `Careless Curation` therefore revisits only the waterfall chamber for Curate Melita and Mask of Sotha Sil. Because this creates the next coherent mainland trip after acquiring `Glover's Letter`, the guide inserts the Riften handoff to Sapphire and resolves Exquisite Sapphire before returning to Solstheim.

The guide keeps pets out of Ashfall's Tear because UESP records that Ghosts/Pets of Skyrim pets can make the cultists hostile inside the narrow temple. This appears as a local player-facing warning because the player has acquired a Nix-Hound by this point.
