# SN-000150 - Windhelm Blood On The Ice And White Phial Route

Status: route-writing source note for TB-035-MR-022.

## Scope

This note supports the v1 guide section `Windhelm Murder Investigation And White Phial`. The section replaces the old `Windhelm State And Hjerim` scaffold with a controlled Windhelm block: first-entry White Phial handling, Wylandriah's Windhelm satchel, Forsaken Cave, `Blood on the Ice`, the Palace Stone of Barenziah, Calixto museum collectibles, selected Windhelm favors, and nearby Windhelm farm rows. It deliberately stages Hjerim ownership, Eastmarch thaneship, Calder, ferry/Solstheim, and broad Eastmarch road dungeons for later route sections.

## Route Decisions

The Windhelm block starts with The White Phial because UESP records that failing to enter The White Phial the first time Windhelm is entered can leave Nurelion already dying, preventing `The White Phial` and the shop service from starting. The player enters the shop before other Windhelm business, starts Nurelion's quest, and takes Wylandriah's Soul Gem from the same building. The Orichalcum Ingot remains in Winterhold so `Hunt and Gather` can finish with the northern route and a clean return to Wylandriah.

`The White Phial` is completed now rather than merely started. Forsaken Cave is a coherent start-to-finish bundle for Nurelion's Mixture, Curalmil, Cracked White Phial, the Forsaken Cave location row, and the Forsaken Cave Marked for Death word. `A Game at Dinner` and `The Mirror` are left closed because they are skill-book copy candidates, not unique quest documents, and the route is still preserving skill-book reads for the later Scholar's Insight window. The final White Phial artifact row is not marked in this pass because the usable item belongs to `Repairing the Phial`, which requires later main-quest progress.

`Blood on the Ice` is completed in the same Windhelm block because the source records several no-console risks if Windhelm is captured by the Imperials, Dragonborn is started, Nilsine/Tova state changes, Hjerim is entered early, or the quest is left open. The guide uses the source-backed direct Wuunferth route rather than accusing Wuunferth through Jorleif. It sells the Strange Amulet to Calixto because that sale is the only source-listed way to convert the item into the Necromancer Amulet. The route explicitly avoids lockpicking Hjerim or stealing a key before the normal murder-scene start.

Hjerim ownership is held even though the route enters Hjerim for the investigation. The canonical Civil War route is Imperial, and the constraint table records Eastmarch thaneship/Hjerim ownership as post-Imperial-Civil-War content. Hjerim cleanup and furnishings also have source-backed ordering bugs, especially the murderer's mess versus living-room upgrade. The player-facing guide therefore treats Hjerim as an investigation site only and keeps storage/home validation for the later property section.

Windhelm farm rows are routed immediately because the player arrives by carriage at Windhelm Stables, and Brandy-Mug, Hlaalu, and Hollyfrost are short, low-risk exterior stops with direct checklist location/crop-sale rows. The guide does not send the player into farm interiors for duplicate skill books or general books; those interiors are not needed for the crop or location objectives.

The selected Windhelm city favors are routed after Blood on the Ice stabilizes the city state. Hillevi's delivery is local to Wuunferth, Revyn's ring favor is local to Viola/Sadri, Stands-In-Shallows has a fixed theft target in New Gnisis Cornerclub, and Scouts-Many-Marshes resolves through Torbjorn in the same city. These are safe, source-backed same-city objectives that also advance Hero of the People tracking and useful relationship-option coverage. Angrenor's one-gold gift is represented as a local non-default spouse/farmhand option, while Stenvar, Silda, Rolff, and Torbjorn's drunk favor are audited but not made required actions because their representative favor/brawl/drunk rows were already handled earlier or have no route-default role.

Adonato's Book is accepted now because the route is already in Candlehearth Hall and the next main-quest staging sequence soon sends the player through Solitude/Bards College. By contrast, Adonato Leotelli's Journal and The Crimson Dirks v4 are left closed: they start AE dungeon routes to Cronvangr Cave and Yorgrim Overlook, which are better bundled with their own Eastmarch/Pale road objectives, pets/items, and location clears rather than started and left sitting.

The broad Eastmarch road sweep is not collapsed into this block. `Rise in the East`, Oengul's Queen Freydis target, Shahvee's Amulet target, Kjar's `Harsh Master`, Gallows Hall, Cronvangr, Yorgrim Overlook, Mara's Eye, Mistwatch, Morvunskar, and other Eastmarch road markers are kept for later coherent bundles. Some are randomized or destination-heavy, some overlap later main-quest or Daedric movement, and some need road/cold/ferry planning. They should not be presented as Windhelm city-state tasks merely because the player can see the city or docks.
