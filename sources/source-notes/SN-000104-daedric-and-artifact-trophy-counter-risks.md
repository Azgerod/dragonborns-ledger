# Source Note: Daedric and Artifact Trophy Counter Risks

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000104

## Claim

`Daedric Influence` requires acquiring one qualifying Daedric artifact, and `Oblivion Walker` requires 15 qualifying Daedric artifacts. The trophy counter is not the same as the in-game Daedric Quests Completed statistic, Creation Club Daedric artifacts do not count, and the main route must choose artifact-awarding endings for every base-game Daedric quest unless the route intentionally uses an optional Hircine dual-artifact branch.

## Routing Relevance

Because the normal artifact-safe main route can obtain exactly 15 qualifying base-game artifacts without exploiting the Hircine dual-artifact outcome, every artifact-awarding choice is route-critical. The route should not rely on AE artifacts, the Skeleton Key, Black Books, or the Daedric quest-completion statistic for `Oblivion Walker`.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's achievements and Daedric quest pages identify `Oblivion Walker` as missable and explain that the route needs artifact-awarding endings rather than merely 15 completed Daedric quests. UESP also notes that the Skeleton Key and Black Books do not count, and that Creation Club Daedric artifacts do not count.

The route-relevant hard choices are the same choices captured in TB-014: Masque over Rueful Axe, Mehrunes' Razor over sparing Silus, Skull of Corruption over sparing Erandur, Ring of Namira over saving Verulus, and an artifact-awarding Hircine outcome. `The Black Star` can award either Azura's Star or the Black Star; both are qualifying artifact outcomes. UESP says both Hircine artifacts can be obtained and can count, but the main route does not need that exploit-adjacent outcome if every normal artifact-safe choice succeeds.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for counter requirements and non-counting AE artifacts. TB-028 still needs to decide whether the dual Hircine outcome is documented as an optional branch or appendix-only exploit note.
