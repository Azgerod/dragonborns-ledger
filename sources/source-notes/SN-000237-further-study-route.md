# Further Study Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000237

## Claim

`Further Study` should be accepted once, immediately after `Fishing Legend` inside the controlled Riften Fishing block. At that point the route has completed Swims-In-Deep-Water's fixed fishing challenges, has both specialty rods, can keep the Lucky Fishing Hat rain control active, and already uses the Riften, Dawnstar, and Broken Oar Grotto fishing spots.

The route should:

- Make a rotating manual save at the Riften Fishery before asking for work.
- Ask Swims-In-Deep-Water for work once, then read whichever random bounty note he gives: `Bounty: Angler Larvae Specimen`, `Bounty: Pearlfish Specimen`, `Bounty: Pygmy Sunfish Specimen`, or `Bounty: Spadefish Specimen`.
- Avoid reload manipulation for the random fish or alchemist; follow the active marker.
- Use the existing Angler Larvae storage from `Frozen Fish`, or catch another Angler Larvae at Dawnstar freezing-water supplies with the Alik'ri Fishing Rod if the bounty consumes the only copy.
- Store one spare Pearlfish, Pygmy Sunfish, and Spadefish before leaving the Fishing block so the later alchemy-effect pass has deterministic copies.
- Turn in the requested fish to the assigned alchemist if the marker is in mainland Skyrim.
- Park the requested fish for the later Solstheim visit if the target is Milore Ienth, Elynea Mothren, or Edla, rather than taking an early ferry detour just for this random radiant.
- Reload the pre-acceptance Fishery save if the assigned alchemist dies or the quest fails.

## Routing Relevance

This closes `OBJ-000599` Further Study and checklist row `CHK-QUESTS-0630`.

It also closes the four possible bounty-note document rows:

- `Bounty: Angler Larvae Specimen`.
- `Bounty: Pearlfish Specimen`.
- `Bounty: Pygmy Sunfish Specimen`.
- `Bounty: Spadefish Specimen`.

Because `Further Study` can consume one of the same fish needed for alchemy-effect discovery, this pass also secures the ingredient copies for Pearlfish, Pygmy Sunfish, and Spadefish. Angler Larvae was already staged by `Frozen Fish`, but the guide keeps the same spare-copy policy if this radiant consumes it.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's `Further Study` page identifies Swims-In-Deep-Water as the giver, records it as a Fishing Creation radiant quest, and says the player asks him for work and reads the bounty letter. The page lists the four possible requested fish as Pygmy Sunfish, Angler Larvae, Pearlfish, and Spadefish, then lists possible alchemist targets across mainland Skyrim and Solstheim. Quest stages show the objective to read one of the four bounty notes, then obtain the named fish for the assigned alchemist, with completion after providing the fish and a failure stage if the alchemist dies.

The four bounty-note pages each identify the document as a Fishing Creation note tied to `Further Study`, with value and weight 0, and list its source as Swims-In-Deep-Water when asking for work. That supports reading whichever note the radiant chooses rather than routing all four on one save.

The four ingredient pages support the route's fish handling. Angler Larvae can be caught in freezing water such as Dawnstar and has a guaranteed Pilgrim's Trench fallback. Pearlfish is supported by rainy temperate lake/stream fishing and guaranteed coastal fallbacks. Pygmy Sunfish is supported by rainy temperate-lake fishing, Lucky Fishing Hat rain, Alik'ri rod support, and 6-9am/6-9pm population timing, with guaranteed Ancestor Glade fallback. Spadefish is supported by rainy temperate-stream fishing and guaranteed fallbacks. The ingredient pages also provide the alchemy-effect lists used by the later eating pass.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the quest giver, random fish set, bounty-note source, completion boundary, failure-on-alchemist-death risk, fish catch controls, and alchemy-effect support.

Confidence is medium for exact radiant target logistics because the alchemist is random, can be in Solstheim, and the source records a Zaria faction bug without USSEP. The guide mitigates that by saving before acceptance, accepting one result without reroll instructions, following the active marker, parking Solstheim targets until the matching planned visit, and reloading if the target dies or the quest fails.

`Rubbish Retrieval`, `Stocking Up`, Brook Bass, Juvenile Mudcrab, Emperor Crab Guardian Spirit, Fangtusk, and Snippy remain separate unresolved Fishing work.
