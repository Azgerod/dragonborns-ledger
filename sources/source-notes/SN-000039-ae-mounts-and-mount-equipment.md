# Source Note: AE Mounts and Mount Equipment

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000039

## Claim

The official AE Creation Club bundle adds finite mount objectives for seven Wild Horses, the Unicorn, the Dwarven Horse, and the Saturalia Reindeer, plus Elven and Steel Horse Armor mount-equipment objectives.

## Routing Relevance

The specification requires all pets and mounts obtainable on the main route. Mount rows need source-backed coverage before later passes decide regional timing, stable routing, active-mount switching, Survival Mode travel value, saddle/armor compatibility, storage handling for removed armor, and whether any mount affects route difficulty or traversal assumptions.

## Evidence Summary

UESP identifies seven tamable wild horses and lists their descriptive names and locations: Black Horse, Chestnut Horse, Dapple Brown Horse, Grey Spotted Horse, Pale Mare, Red Horse, and White Spotted Horse. UESP also treats the Unicorn as a separate unique mount tied to Creature of Legend.

Forgotten Seasons adds the Dwarven Horse, obtained by reassembling it through The Dwarven Horse quest. Saturalia Holiday Pack adds the Reindeer mount, purchased by bill of sale from Agrane Peryval. Elven and Steel Horse Armor are obtained through hostlers and have compatibility/storage caveats that matter later when the route chooses active mounts and storage policy.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high that these rows cover the finite AE mount and mount-equipment set at source-list level. Exact acquisition order, active-mount management, stable visits, saddle choices, horse-armor preservation, and Survival Mode travel use remain deferred.
