# Source Note: Reach Setup, Markarth, Nchuand-Zel, And Old Hroldan Route

Status: reviewed for TB-035-MR-018 draft placement.

Source note ID: SN-000146

## Claim

The v1 guide should convert the internal `Reach Setup` bucket into a concrete Markarth/Old Hroldan route. This pass can complete safe Markarth delivery favors, the Markarth stage of `The Book of Love`, `My Pet Mudcrab`, `Nimhe, the Poisoned One`, `The Lost Expedition`, Soljund's Sinkhole, Old Hroldan setup, and nearby location/activity rows while holding prison, Daedric, Temple of Dibella, Forsworn-camp, property, late Thieves, and Sky Haven/Dragonbane content for coherent later sections.

## Routing Relevance

The player enters this pass from Riften/Honeyside with `The Book of Love` active at Markarth, Bolli's Purchase Agreement already carried from Riften, and `Hard Answers` active but untouched. The route uses the carriage to reach Markarth, then treats Markarth as a hub rather than a single main-quest waypoint: stable delivery, farm work, mine work, market incident, city delivery favors, Calcelmo, Nchuand-Zel, Soljund's Sinkhole, and Old Hroldan all become one same-region pass. The pass keeps the next section focused by stopping `The Forsworn Conspiracy` at the Shrine of Talos meeting and by returning to Markarth with a clean save before Cidhna/Molag/Namira.

## Evidence Summary

`The Book of Love` naturally belongs in this pass because Dinya has already sent the player to Calcelmo. The Markarth stage is local: Calcelmo sends the player to Yngvar, Yngvar sells the Love Poem, Faleen gives a letter back to Calcelmo, and the next step returns to Dinya. The guide uses a rotating save before the poem/letter handoffs because the source records letter-delivery bugs.

Markarth's first entry forces the Weylin/Margret incident. The guide accepts and reads Eltrys' Note because the incident is unavoidable when entering the market, but it stops before the Shrine of Talos so the next section can treat Cidhna as a deliberate prison/branch-state block.

The safe city errands are routed now because they begin and end in the same Markarth visit: Kerah-to-Calcelmo, Banning-to-Voada, Bothela-to-Raerek, Omluag-to-Mulush-to-Omluag, Ondolemar/Ogmund, and Bolli-to-Kleppr. Rogatus is picked up at Salvius Farm because the route is already leaving Markarth for Old Hroldan, and the delivery endpoint is the same inn.

Calcelmo's Nchuand-Zel content is routed now because `Nimhe, the Poisoned One` grants access to the excavation and leads directly to Alethius's Notes. The guide takes the journals in source-listed quest order, puts Stromm first, and avoids the known bug where taking later journals before Stromm can break normal completion. It also uses the source note that the quest arrow may point back to the Armory after the lever; the guide sends the player out to Calcelmo instead.

After Nimhe is reported, the Dwemer Museum Stone of Barenziah can be taken under Calcelmo's access. The guide takes only that Stone because Stendarr's Hammer is 100-weight carry/storage-sensitive and is now routed in `SN-000246` after Vlindrel Hall is owned, while Calcelmo's Laboratory and Tower are reserved for `Hard Answers`.

Soljund's Sinkhole is pulled into this pass because it is on the Old Hroldan road, is far above its suggested/minimum level by this point, and has a same-site Perth turn-in. The source says Soljund's Sinkhole does not receive a cleared-map state even though the quest clears the draugr; the guide therefore uses the quest turn-in and location discovery cue rather than a cleared-location cue. `Jornibret's Last Dance` and `Boethiah's Proving` get a local warning because this route sends the player into the exact mine room where those books may be visible.

Old Hroldan is used as a support point and quest setup. The source lists the Tiber Septim room sleep trigger and randomized Hjalti's Sword targets, so the guide records the actual target and does not ask the player to reload for a preferred redoubt.

## Nearby Objective Audit And Deferrals

The pass does not buy Vlindrel Hall or pursue Thane of the Reach. The current route has just spent the normal Riften property budget on Honeyside, and Reach property/thane work needs a coherent property, local-help, shield, and Civil War state validation pass.

`The Heart of Dibella`, `Speak to Degaine`, `Lisbet's Missing Shipment`, `The House of Horrors`, and `The Taste of Death` are not blended into this setup pass. The first two belong with the Temple/Broken Tower/Dibella route, Lisbet has a source-listed bug if its Forsworn camp is precleared, and Molag Bal/Namira belong to the next Markarth Daedric/prison section.

Kolskeggr Mine and `Coated in Blood` are held because they interact with Orc-favor and material sequencing. The route still allows a simple Left Hand ore-sale activity because it is local and does not complete Kolskeggr.

`Buy Dwarven artifact` is not manufactured here. It depends on buying a qualifying Dwarven item and later courier behavior; the guide should route it only when the selected source item, preservation policy, and courier timing are intentional.

The Treasury House Stone of Barenziah is held for the next Markarth investigation pass because `The Forsworn Conspiracy` sends the player to Thonar Silver-Blood there. Calcelmo's Laboratory, Calcelmo's Tower, Chimarvamidium, the Dwemer Puzzle Cube, and `Twin Secrets` remain with the level-46 `Hard Answers` route. Sky Haven Temple and Dragonbane remain held for the level-46 main-quest gate.

`Pilgrimage` is not advanced because this pass travels directly from Riften to Markarth and then remains in the Reach; the Alessandra's Dagger handoff waits for the next Whiterun-compatible pass.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the Markarth city bundle, Calcelmo/Nchuand-Zel route, Soljund's Sinkhole inclusion, and Old Hroldan setup. Confidence is medium for the exact `Hero of the People` counter accounting of Soljund's Sinkhole versus some checklist variants, but the objective itself is a source-listed miscellaneous objective and is tracked explicitly. No TB-035-MR-018 `NEEDS ROUTE RESOLUTION` notes remain.
