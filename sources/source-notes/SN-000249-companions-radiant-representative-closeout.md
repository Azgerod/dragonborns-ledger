# Companions Radiant Representative Closeout

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000249

## Claim

The Companions radiant representative parent row should close through a bounded late Jorrvaskr closeout, not an appendix-only deferral.

For this route:

- The four required Companions interstitial jobs still count toward representative coverage.
- The ordinary post-`Take Up Arms` pool should get one clean completion for each route-feasible type: both `Animal Extermination` variants, `Trouble in Skyrim`, `Family Heirloom`, `Escaped Criminal`, and `Rescue Mission`.
- `Hired Muscle` / `Scare My Enemy` remains a seed-sensitive caveat: accept and complete it if the early Farkas-first windows or the ordinary closeout naturally offer a clean target, but do not restart the run or extend the route into arbitrary Farkas-only repetitions solely to force that alias.
- The post-`The Silver Hand` Aela revenge window remains a two-of-three boundary. The unoffered title among `Striking the Heart`, `Stealing Plans`, and `Retrieval` is a one-save-limited outcome, not a remaining route defect.
- `Dragon Seekers` needs one representative completion after `Glory of the Dead` and `Dragon Rising`.
- Hard saves in this loop are bug recovery for dead, inaccessible, already-cleared, missing-item, missing-hostage, or stuck-marker targets; they are not normal target or title rerolls.

## Routing Relevance

This closes `OBJ-000115` by making the aggregate boundary explicit in `main-guide-v1.md`. It also upgrades the player-facing treatment of `OBJ-000102` through `OBJ-000108` from early-gate-only conditional coverage to a bounded late representative closeout, while preserving the existing one-save limits for `OBJ-000109` through `OBJ-000111`.

The closeout belongs after `Glory of the Dead`, the three totems, and Farkas/Vilkas `Purity` because the route has already stabilized Beast Blood, follower state, and late-game travel support. It should not run before the opening Survival base is stable, and it should not delay the windowed Aela revenge jobs until after `Blood's Honor`.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's Companions page lists seven repeatable radiants available after `Take Up Arms`, three repeatable Aela revenge radiants available after `The Silver Hand` that become permanently unavailable after starting `Blood's Honor`, and post-`Glory of the Dead` radiants including `Dragon Seekers`. It also records the primary questline's required radiant gates and two important limitations: the first post-`Take Up Arms` radiant is determined when a new game starts, and only two of the three Silver Hand revenge radiants can normally be completed in one playthrough.

The ordinary-radiant pages define the route actions and bug boundaries. `Hired Muscle` is a Farkas brawl contract whose target can become dead or impossible; `Trouble in Skyrim` is a Farkas leader-kill location contract with already-cleared and inaccessible-target risks; the two `Animal Extermination` pages split Aela's house/shop/farm beast contract from her animal-den boss-beast contract; `Family Heirloom` retrieves a random item from a boss chest; `Escaped Criminal` sends the player after a moving criminal target; and `Rescue Mission` requires freeing and escorting a kidnapped citizen home.

The Aela revenge pages define the windowed title set after `The Silver Hand`: `Striking the Heart` kills a Silver Hand leader, `Stealing Plans` retrieves Silver Hand plans, and `Retrieval` retrieves a Fragment of Wuuthrad. Source and constraint notes already establish the one-save two-of-three boundary, so the TB-044 closeout records the unoffered title as one-save-limited rather than trying to exploit late-Aela behavior.

`Dragon Seekers` is available from Farkas or Vilkas after `Glory of the Dead` and `Dragon Rising`, chooses a random dragon lair, and is representative after one completion. Later level-78 Legendary Dragon work may reuse the same quest as trophy support, but that repeat is not extra Companions representative scope.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the named radiant pools, required gates, Silver Hand two-of-three boundary, `Dragon Seekers` prerequisite, and individual quest completion actions.

Confidence is medium for how often late Farkas/Vilkas/Aela work requests surface every ordinary title on a live PS4 save. The route handles that by following active assignments without normal rerolling, using the hard save only for source-listed broken targets, and carrying the `Hired Muscle` seed caveat forward instead of demanding a new-game restart.
