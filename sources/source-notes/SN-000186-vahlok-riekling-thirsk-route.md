# SN-000186 - Vahlok, Riekling Roads, And Thirsk Route

## Scope

Supports the v1 guide section `Vahlok's Tomb, Riekling Roads, And Thirsk`.

This pass completes the just-started `Lost Legacy`, routes the Thirsk branch save and canonical Nord-side continuity, completes the Nord-side Thirsk favors, and pulls safe central/northwestern Solstheim clearable locations, East Empire Pendant strongboxes, and Riekling Spear sources into the same practical route.

## Routing Decisions

The section starts by converting previously staged materials into executable Thirsk favor readiness. The Old Hroldan pass now collects Juniper Berries for Elmus; the Northshore pass now mines the local ebony ore; Raven Rock Mine supplies the remaining ebony ore after `The Final Descent`; and the stored stalhrim from the previous section supplies Halbarn's stalhrim requirement.

`Lost Legacy` is completed immediately because it was accepted at the end of the Skaal/Stalhrim pass and Vahlok's Tomb is now accessible. The route keeps Vahlok's Tomb intact rather than splitting the claw halves, Battle Fury words, and Vahlok fight across sections.

The nearby Dunmeri Camp east of Vahlok's Tomb is routed with `Lost Legacy` because it contains Vahlok's Tomb East Empire Pendant strongbox and the largest guaranteed loose Riekling Spear cluster for Hilund.

The section routes Ramshackle Trading Post, Altar of Thrond, Snowclad Ruins, Moesring Pass, Mount Moesring, Bristleback Cave, Broken Tusk Mine, the Riekling Chest, and Reaver's End because they are safe central/northwestern Solstheim objectives with no active level, branch, randomized-target, Black Book, or later-quest dependency that justifies holding them. The Riekling sites also materially support Hilund's 50-spear requirement.

`Ahzidal's Descent` is not duplicated in Bristleback Cave because the guide already routed the Ashfallow Citadel copy in the Raven Rock core section.

The Thirsk branch save is made after Elmus's pre-choice Ashfire Mead favor, because UESP records that the favor can fail once Thirsk is reclaimed for the Nords. The Riekling side is played first from `HS-DRAGONBORN-THIRSK-CHOICE`, then reloaded for canonical Nord-side `Retaking Thirsk`.

The canonical Nord-side ending supports the project's Thirsk default: preserving Kuvar's Heavy Armor training and keeping Elmus, Halbarn, and Hilund favor/service/spouse surfaces available. The player-facing route chooses Bujold's secret and then reassures Kuvar to preserve that service state.

Horker Island is held even though it is north of Bujold's Retreat. It contains Horksbane and a pendant, but it also directly overlaps Deathbrand/Gyldenhul Barrow. Holding it keeps the island's unique item, Deathbrand/Gyldenhul access, Deathbrand map/gear circuit, and pendant together in the later Deathbrand route instead of creating a shallow partial island visit.

Kagrumez is held because full completion requires the later Nchardak/Revus/Tel Mithryn resonance-gem route and awards Steadfast creature followers plus the Dwarven Black Bow of Fate. White Ridge Barrow is held because it contains `Black Book: The Sallow Regent`, a Cyclone word, Dukaan, and the imbuing chamber; it fits the Tel Mithryn/Black Book/imbuing pass rather than this Thirsk favor pass.

Deathbrand, Horker Island, Kagrumez, White Ridge Barrow, Netch Leather, Sun Stone, Tree Stone, Morwen's Falkreath handoff, Skaal Amulet, and Torkild remain staged by their existing route reasons.

## Unresolved
