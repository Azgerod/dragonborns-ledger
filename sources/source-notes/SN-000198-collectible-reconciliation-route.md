## Route Decisions

Angi's Camp is now safe to route as a coherent late bundle. The early Falkreath passes deliberately avoided a discover-only detour and held Angi's practice for high Archery value and Scholar's Insight planning. The selected `The Gold Ribbon of Merit` read has already moved to Untold Legends in MR-059, so this section can complete Angi's practice, claim Angi's Bow, take Treasure Map V, and loot Treasure Chest V without damaging the book plan.

Michaela's Flagon and Ancient Traveler's Skull are not late-route destinations. They belong in already-routed Morthal and Wayward Pass visits, so the guide now inserts exact pickup instructions at those natural stops and coverage marks them as retrospective placements.

Konahrik belongs after `Dragonslayer` because Nahkriin is required and Skuldafn cannot be revisited through normal PS4 play. The guide now sends the player back to the Labyrinthian mask building with the Wooden Mask plus Hevnoraak, Krosis, Morokei, Nahkriin, Otar, Rahgot, Vokun, and Volsung. It warns to use a hard save, not mount a horse during the Wooden Mask transition, expect improvements on the eight placed masks to be stripped, retrieve all eight masks, take Konahrik, and unequip the Wooden Mask to return.

No Stone Unturned cannot close in this section without moving the Proudspire purchase forward. The route has intentionally held Proudspire Manor for the property-economy section; therefore the Proudspire Stone remains the concrete gate. The guide names that gate and records that Vex/Tolvald/Prowler's Profit must wait until after the Proudspire Stone is collected. It also preserves the source-backed Tolvald rubble warning: do not enter Tolvald's Cave for the crown before Vex gives the crown objective.

Fishing is not ready for player-facing catch instructions. The source page confirms that catch routing depends on spot, biome, weather, time, rod, spot population, active Fishing quests, and specialist rods from Viriya and Swims-In-Deep-Water. The existing objective rows and SN-000064 list the species set but do not give an executable per-species route that preserves alchemy ingredients and quest flow. MR-064 therefore records specific `NEEDS ROUTE RESOLUTION` rows for OBJ-001893 through OBJ-001918 instead of using a generic fishing line.

Dragon Hunter and Dragonrider remain counters, not collectible item sets. Dragon Hunter is tracked by absorbed souls only and remains safe to check here, but the current guide does not have a deterministic final count. Dragonrider has one required Sahrotaar ride recorded; the source-backed safety constraints require ordinary rideable dragons, hard saves, and post-dismount checks. The remaining four rides need exact safe targets or a validated policy before becoming guide steps.

Balbus's Fork remains unresolved under the existing random-encounter policy. The source confirms the gift condition after `Recipe for Disaster` plus Gourmet's Writ of Passage, but it does not provide a deterministic encounter route. The guide should not ask the player to force random encounters unless the project adopts an explicit policy.
