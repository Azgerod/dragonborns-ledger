## Claim

The base-game College of Winterhold primary questline consists of nine quests, including the College Objective Quest pointer entry, and has trophies tied to joining the College, Revealing the Unseen, and The Eye of Magnus.

## Evidence Summary

UESP lists the College primary questline as College Objective Quest, First Lessons, Under Saarthal, Hitting the Books, Good Intentions, Revealing the Unseen, Containment, The Staff of Magnus, and The Eye of Magnus. The trophy entries are Gatekeeper, Revealing the Unseen, and The Eye of Magnus.
