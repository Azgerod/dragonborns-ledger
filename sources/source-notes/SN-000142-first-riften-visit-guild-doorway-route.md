# Source Note: First Riften Visit and Guild Doorway Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000142

## Claim

The v1 guide can use the first Riften visit as a dense city-and-Ratway routing block: discover Riften support points, complete low-risk local favors, start long collection and delivery favors whose next targets lie elsewhere, complete the first skooma-chain step, deliberately fail Brynjolf's market scheme to preserve Brand-Shei, enter the Thieves Guild, collect Ratway-local items and Skritch, receive Thieves Guild armor, and use Galathil. Larger property, Frost, Guardian Vault, Bloodthirst, temple, jail, fishing, and city-restoration work should remain staged for later coherent route blocks.

## Routing Relevance

Riften concentrates many completion rows in one warm hub. The route needs to route safe colocated work aggressively without turning the first Riften visit into a property purchase, random-job, or branch block. The key ordering constraints are Brand-Shei preservation during `A Chance Arrangement`, nonlethal `Taking Care of Business` debt collection, Wujeeta and the Riften skooma chain, Ratway item/pet opportunities, and not accepting Delvin/Vex jobs before assignment-save tracking is ready.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's Riften page records the city gate shakedown, Brynjolf's approach in the market, the city's merchant and service density, and the presence of both standard and AE property surfaces. `Bloody Nose`, `Stabilized`, `Bring It!`, `Helping Hand`, and `The Raid` are short Riften-local favors or quest steps that can be completed during a first city pass, while `Ringmaker`, `Ice Cold`, `Stoking the Flames`, `Sealing the Deal`, `Truth Ore Consequences`, `Few and Far Between`, `Hunt and Gather`, and Bolli's `Special Delivery` are efficient to start because they give explicit item-retention or later-destination instructions.

The Fishing pages support an early fishery introduction: `Angler Acquaintances` is an introductory handoff at Riften Fishery, `Catch of the Day` provides fair-weather fish context and `List of Fair Weather Fish`, and `In A Pinch` starts from Viriya with `Bounty: Juvenile Mudcrab`. The actual catching is not colocated with first Riften city access and remains better suited to a fishing route with weather and water-type planning.

The skooma-chain pages establish that `Helping Hand` opens the Wujeeta information route, `The Raid` uses the Riften Warehouse and `Shipment's Ready`, and `Supply and Demand` sends the player to Cragslane Cavern afterward. This makes `Helping Hand` and `The Raid` safe first-visit completions, while `Supply and Demand` belongs with a later Cragslane route.

`A Chance Arrangement` explicitly supports the route's intentional failure: the quest can progress if the ring-planting scheme fails. This avoids imprisoning Brand-Shei, which protects `Distant Memories` access and avoids the documented long-term Brand-Shei jail caveat. `Taking Care of Business` supports the Ratway path and nonlethal debt routes: Keerava can be handled through Talen-Jei's family information, Bersi can be pressured by breaking the urn, and Haelga can be collected from directly when she is the last debtor.

The Ratway page places Gloves of the Pugilist on Gian the Fist and records Skritch's cage and nearby Beggar skill-book copies. The Pets of Skyrim and Skritch pages support freeing Skritch and receiving the Teleport Pet spell. The same Ratway area also includes Guardian Vault and Dawnfang/Bloodthirst triggers, but those lead into a larger AE dungeon and vampire-weapon chain and are not required for the first Thieves Guild doorway. The player-facing guide warns only at the immediate Ratway trigger point.

`Meet the Family`, `Thieves Guild Armor`, `Surgery`, `Galathil`, and `Achievements` support finishing the first guild establishment, receiving the full starting armor set from Tonilia, and completing Galathil's face-sculpting objective for `A New You`. The guide stops before Delvin/Vex jobs because later Thieves Guild restoration requires controlled job assignment saves and city-tally tracking.

## Route Placement Decision

TB-035-MR-014 routes all safe first-Riften and first-Ratway objectives that are either completed locally or usefully started while the player is already talking to the relevant NPCs. It intentionally starts long collection favors when the quest start is colocated and the route can give clear item-retention rules immediately.

The route does not buy Honeyside or Shadowfoot Sanctum in this pass. Honeyside has prerequisite and economy checks, and Shadowfoot is a 7,500-gold AE home purchase from Vekel. The user also warned not to assume early house money. Both properties stay with a later property route.

The route does not start `Promises to Keep`, `The Book of Love`, `Pilgrimage`, `Caught Red Handed`, `Under the Table`, or `The Lover's Requital` in this pass. Those starts are close geographically, but their next steps involve a named Frost outcome route, Temple of Mara cross-region stages, Whiterun or Ivarstead delivery synchronization, theft/marks planning, Black-Briar Meadery, or jail/Ivarstead follow-up. Keeping them out avoids long immersion gaps and keeps related objective bundles together.

The route does not enter the Guardian Vault or pursue Dawnfang/Bloodthirst during first Ratway access. Those are same-path triggers, but they are larger AE quest content with their own dungeon and follow-up destinations. They remain staged for a later coherent Ratway/Guardian route.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the city-favor starts and completions, Brand-Shei preservation strategy, first Thieves Guild entry, Ratway item and Skritch acquisition, and property/Frost/Guardian deferrals. No MR-014 `NEEDS ROUTE RESOLUTION` notes remain. Later passes still need to place `Supply and Demand`, `Promises to Keep`, Riften property purchases, Guardian Vault/Dawnfang/Bloodthirst, the Riften temple and jail quests, full fishing turn-ins, Delvin/Vex job tracking, and Riften merchant investments.
