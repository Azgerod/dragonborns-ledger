## Route Decisions

The section starts after all-perks completion because it deliberately spends a large gold reserve and assumes stable late-route combat, storage, sleep, and travel access. The guide therefore sells down to at least 95,000 free gold before city-home, Hearthfire, farm, service, and mount spending.

Proudspire and the final Stone of Barenziah are routed in this pass because the homes surface owns the Proudspire purchase. `No Stone Unturned` immediately follows the Proudspire stone pickup so the old MR-064 gate for No Stone and Prowler's Profit is closed here rather than left to final reconciliation. The guide keeps the UESP rubble warning as route order: do not enter Tolvald's crown chamber before Vex gives the crown objective.

The Reach property route now starts Igmund's `Kill the Forsworn Leader` before the Cidhna Mine state change. The marked Forsworn target is folded into the earlier Reach-redoubt target list, then reported during the homes pass before `Find Hrolfdir's Shield`, Vlindrel purchase, Thane of the Reach, and Argis.

The guide treats AE homes as ownership and safe-storage confirmations, not as artifact-display assignments. Hendraheim, Dead Man's Dread, Bloodchill Manor, and Nchuanthumz are acquired here because their starts, ownership quests, or late travel loops fit the homes pass. Tundra, Myrwatch, Shadowfoot, Gallows Hall, Goldenhills, Severin, Breezehome, Honeyside, Hjerim, and the Hearthfire lands were already acquired or routed earlier, so this section confirms them by name and completes pending furnishing/service work where needed.

Hearthfire construction uses the route-default wings from `data/route-planning/route-default-decisions.md`: Lakeview Armory, Storage Room, and Bedrooms; Windstad Kitchen, Alchemy Laboratory, and Greenhouse; Heljarchen Library, Trophy Room, and Enchanter's Tower. The guide routes the stewards Rayya, Valdimar, and Gregor; each carriage driver; each bard; homestead horses; livestock; exterior projects; Cellar and Aquarium work; and the Master Architect condition. Steward furnishing is treated as paid work that may require waiting and revisiting before the guide marks full furnishing complete.

The household default remains Ysolda with Lucia and Sofie at Tundra Homestead. Child pets and alternate spouses, children, stewards, bards, farmhands, city stable horses, and household homes remain option-list choices rather than route branches.

The mount plan completes all remaining finite wild horses and unique AE mounts. Red, Grey Spotted, Black, Chestnut, Pale Mare, Unicorn, and Dwarven Horse are tamed here; the previously routed Dapple Brown, White Spotted, Whiterun stable horse, Frost, Shadowmere, Arvak, Reindeer, Elven Horse Armor, and Steel Horse Armor are confirmed in the closing audit.

The pet and creature-follower audit confirms all finite route-required pets by name. Gogh is added through `Blue in the Face` because the route had not yet completed the Goblins creature-follower surface. Non-default animal followers remain option-list rows unless already routed for a required pet or faction outcome.
