Status: needs review.

## Claim

The AE Creation Club bundle includes source-listed unique, named, quest-reward, and equipment parent sets beyond Alternative Armors. These need parent objective coverage before individual item preservation, branch, leveled-reward, and checklist rows are expanded.

## Routing Relevance

The specification requires all unique items obtainable on the main route, branch handling for meaningful mutually exclusive outcomes, unique-item preservation, and gradual difficulty progression. Parent rows keep coverage visible without prematurely deciding exact item pickup order, branch treatment, display/storage policy, or member-level route placement.
