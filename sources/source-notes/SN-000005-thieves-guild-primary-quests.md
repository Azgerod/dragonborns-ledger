## Claim

The base-game Thieves Guild primary questline consists of twelve named quests. Its faction trophies are tied to Taking Care of Business, Darkness Returns, and restoring the guild through Under New Management.

## Evidence Summary

UESP lists the Thieves Guild primary questline as A Chance Arrangement, Taking Care of Business, Loud and Clear, Dampened Spirits, Scoundrel's Folly, Speaking With Silence, Hard Answers, The Pursuit, Trinity Restored, Blindsighted, Darkness Returns, and Under New Management. The trophy entries are Taking Care of Business, Darkness Returns, and One with the Shadows.

## Confidence and Open Questions
