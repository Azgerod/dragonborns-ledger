# Source Note: Leveled Reward Cell-Entry Locks

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000094

## Claim

Several leveled unique rewards are not controlled only by pickup or final reward timing. Chillrend, Dragonbane, and the Gauldur Blackblade/Blackbow require location-entry or location-spawn warnings, while Silent Moons Camp is a respawn-sensitive Lunar weapon source. The current sources do not confirm a Frostmere Crypt first-entry level lock for The Pale Blade.

## Routing Relevance

The guide must preserve maximum-tier leveled unique rewards where possible. These rows define the minimum "do not enter or spawn before this level" warnings that later route drafts need before selecting regional pathing.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's leveled-reward material says Chillrend's level is set by first entering Riftweald Manor. The Alduin's Wall page warns against entering Sky Haven Temple before level 46 if the guide wants the best Dragonbane. The Forbidden Legend page ties the Gauldur weapons to boss level, then ties boss level to the first spawn of the linked dungeon set; it also says reading Lost Legends or approaching any related dungeon can spawn all four related dungeons.

Silent Moons Camp is softer but still route-relevant. The location page says the best Lunar weapon results require level 8+ and warns that early low-level contact or repeated nearby travel can require a 30-day reset.

The Pale Lady and The Pale Blade pages support the level 27+ reward target and the Ra'jirr/Pale Lady acquisition context. They do not, in this pass, establish that entering Frostmere Crypt before level 27 fixes The Pale Blade's tier. Frostmere Crypt still has separate routing risks through Kharjo's Amulet of the Moon target bug.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for Riftweald Manor, Sky Haven Temple, Forbidden Legend dungeon spawning, and Silent Moons Camp respawn sensitivity. Confidence is moderate for the negative Frostmere conclusion: the route should delay claiming or resolving The Pale Blade until level 27+, but TB-013 should not label Frostmere Crypt itself as a confirmed Pale Blade first-entry lock unless later sources prove that behavior.
