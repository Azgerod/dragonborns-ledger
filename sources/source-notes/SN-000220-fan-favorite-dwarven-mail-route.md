# Fan Favorite And Dwarven Mail Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000220

## Claim

`Fan Favorite` should remain an Ivarstead-to-Ruins-of-Bthalft AE route only after Falkreath land/Lakeview exposure is safe. Saves that already bought Lakeview Manor complete the quest in the Southern Warm Corridor; saves that parked Siddgeir's bandit job at Cracked Tusk Keep complete the Cracked Tusk/Mehrunes closeout first, buy and visit Lakeview Manor, then immediately route the Arena Fan camp, `The Crimson Dirks, v8`, Urgnok, Ruins of Bthalft, and the four-piece Dwarven Mail set before returning north.

## Routing Relevance

The `Fan Favorite` page identifies the quest as part of `Alternative Armors - Dwarven Mail`, started by reading Arena Fan's Note, with the camp south of Ivarstead and the Ruins of Bthalft as the relevant locations. The same page warns that Arena Fan's corpse can despawn if the player triggers the corpse and leaves it for too long, and also warns that the quest can stop the Jarl of Falkreath from speaking after the Jarl letter. The guide therefore preserves the earlier conditional hold: do not approach the camp while Falkreath land is still parked, but once Lakeview is safe, complete the camp and ruin route in one sequence.

The camp source cluster supports taking both Arena Fan's Note and `The Crimson Dirks, v8` from Arena Fan. The Urgnok source confirms that Urgnok is enabled by the quest and wears the full Dwarven Mail set. The Dwarven armor page confirms the set members as `Dwarven Mail`, `Dwarven Mail Boots`, `Dwarven Mail Gauntlets`, and `Dwarven Mail Helmet`. The Ruins of Bthalft page confirms the clearable exterior bandit location and also records Aetherium pedestal risks, so the Fan Favorite pass clears the exterior and Urgnok but leaves Aetherium Shards and the pedestal untouched until the Lost to the Ages route.

## Evidence Summary

`Fan Favorite` has a short route boundary: find Arena Fan south of Ivarstead, read the note, then kill Urgnok at the Ruins of Bthalft and claim the armor. Because the route uses both the quest start document and the checklist-tracked Crimson Dirks book from the same corpse, the guide now instructs the player to read and take Arena Fan's Note and take `The Crimson Dirks, v8` in the same visit.

Urgnok is not a prequest encounter; he is enabled after the corresponding quest starts. The route therefore does not send the player to Ruins of Bthalft first. After the note start, the player clears the exterior bandits, kills Urgnok, and preserves all four named Dwarven Mail pieces.

Ruins of Bthalft overlaps the later Aetherium Forge route. The Fan Favorite pass may clear the exterior and use the ruin as Urgnok's quest destination, but it must not activate the Aetherium pedestal or insert Aetherium Shards. The later Lost to the Ages/Aetherium Forge route can then use the same location without depending on a shallow location-only visit.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the Fan Favorite start, reward, book, NPC, and location route. The only retained caution is procedural: do not approach and abandon Arena Fan's camp while Falkreath land is still parked. The guide now has an explicit delayed closeout for the Cracked Tusk branch, so no Fan Favorite route-resolution note remains open.

No new branch route is needed. The early and delayed versions are the same main-route content under two possible Siddgeir target timelines.
