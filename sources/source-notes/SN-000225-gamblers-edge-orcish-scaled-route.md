# Gambler's Edge And Orcish Scaled Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000225

## Claim

`Gambler's Edge` should be routed inside the existing Riften and Cragslane Cavern work instead of left as a delayed task. The route reads and takes `Guard's Dossier: Antonius` from the second floor of Mistveil Keep Barracks, clears Cragslane Cavern for `Supply and Demand`, loots and reads `Antonius' Note` and `The Crimson Dirks, v6` from Antonius, then returns to the Bee and Barb, pickpockets `Orders` from the Armor Collector, buys the full Orcish Scaled set from her, completes `Gambler's Edge`, and preserves all four armor pieces.

## Routing Relevance

The current guide already sends the player from Riften to Cragslane Cavern for `Supply and Demand`, then returns to Riften for loot selling, the Bee and Barb, Frost, Honeyside, and Thane of the Rift. Starting `Gambler's Edge` immediately before the Cragslane clear and closing it at the next Bee and Barb stop avoids a separate cave trip, resolves the Orcish Scaled parent-set gap, and turns the former Mistveil dossier delay into explicit route prose.

The route uses the legal purchase path for the armor set and a controlled pickpocket for `Orders`. It does not ask the player to persuade the Armor Collector out of Riften and kill her for the set.

## Evidence Summary

`Gambler's Edge` starts by reading `Guard's Dossier: Antonius` on a second-floor dresser in Mistveil Keep Barracks. The quest then points to Cragslane Cavern, where Antonius is already dead near the pit-fight route. His note explains that he sold the armor to a collector in Riften.

The existing guide already clears Cragslane Cavern for `Supply and Demand`, and the Cragslane page lists both `Supply and Demand` and `Gambler's Edge` as related quests. The same page identifies the local `The Wolf Queen, v1` skill-book copy, so the guide keeps that copy closed for the already-selected Scholar's Insight reading window.

UESP's `Antonius' Note` page places that note on Antonius in Cragslane Cavern. The UESP `The Crimson Dirks, v6` page confirms the title, Creation association, and quest relation but has a blank location field. The route uses secondary community-wiki support for the exact v6 body source and records the source-priority caveat here.

The Armor Collector is at the Bee and Barb and carries `Orders`, a random item selection, and the Orcish Scaled armor set. The quest page allows the armor to be purchased, obtained through persuasion/intimidation follow-up, killed-for, or pickpocketed. The guide buys the set to preserve clean main continuity and uses a rotating manual save for the `Orders` pickpocket, consistent with existing guide handling for unique document pickpockets.

The Orcish Scaled reward set has four pieces: `Orcish Scaled Armor`, `Orcish Scaled Boots`, `Orcish Scaled Gauntlets`, and `Orcish Scaled Helmet`. It is light armor and has no shield. The blacksmith forge lists crafted Orcish Scaled items under Misc rather than the Orcish category, but this route does not need to craft the set because the Armor Collector carries the deterministic reward set.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the quest start, Cragslane progression, Antonius' Note pickup, Armor Collector location, `Orders` source, purchase path, and four-piece Orcish Scaled preservation.

Confidence is medium for `The Crimson Dirks, v6` acquisition from Antonius because the route uses lower-priority secondary support to fill a location detail missing from UESP's individual book page. The Creation page and book page still confirm that the title belongs to the same Creation and quest.
