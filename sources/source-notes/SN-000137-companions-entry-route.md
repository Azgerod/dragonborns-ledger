# Companions Entry Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000137

## Claim

After Goldenhills and Rorikstead stabilize food, rest, income, and follower state, the guide should route the Companions entry block through `Take Up Arms`, one clean post-entry radiant job, and `Proving Honor`. The first Companions job must be written as a conditional route because the available contract target is randomized and can collide with already-cleared or otherwise broken targets. `Hired Muscle` should be accepted if Farkas offers a clean target, but the route should not require restarting the run to force it.

## Routing Relevance

This route keeps the Companions join and first Dustman's Cairn trip together instead of making a shallow Jorrvaskr visit earlier. Starting the faction earlier would reduce some radiant-target collision risk, but it would also send a Legendary Survival character into Companions radiants and Dustman's Cairn before the route has built a stable food/rest/economy base. The guide therefore uses a first-job hard save and explicit bug-recovery rules rather than moving the entire faction entry before the opening Whiterun and central-support sections.

The player-facing section names the exact first-job recording method: quest title, giver, target NPC or target location, target hold, and result. This is necessary because Companions radiants are not all fixed-location quests. The section completes the actual clean job assigned by the save, marks the required gate, and records `Hired Muscle` / `Scare My Enemy` only if that specific contract is offered cleanly.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's Companions page lists the primary sequence as `Take Up Arms`, one radiant quest, `Proving Honor`, one radiant quest, `The Silver Hand`, two radiants, then the late Companions chain. It also says the first post-`Take Up Arms` radiant is determined when a new game starts and recommends `Hired Muscle` because it becomes unavailable later. The guide therefore asks Farkas first and routes `Hired Muscle` if it is clean, but it does not ask for a new-game restart.

The first-job instructions cover all post-`Take Up Arms` contract types because the route cannot assume a fixed target on PS4. `Hired Muscle` requires a brawl without weapons, magic, or shouts and can become impossible if the target is dead, inaccessible, or lacks the correct dialogue. `Trouble in Skyrim` can become impossible when the target location has already been cleared. `Animal Extermination (B)` has a similar already-cleared-den bug. `Rescue Mission` starts immediately when offered and has several hostage-following or missing-hostage bugs. The guide uses `HS-COMPANIONS-FIRST-JOB` as bug recovery: keep a clean contract, but reload and choose a different available leader if the target is already broken.

`Take Up Arms` requires speaking to Kodlak, sparring with Vilkas, delivering Vilkas's Sword to Eorlund, delivering Aela's Shield, and following Farkas. UESP warns against placing Vilkas's Sword on a weapon rack or plaque, so the guide sends it directly to Eorlund. UESP also states magic, bound weapons, and enchanted weapons are inappropriate for the Vilkas sparring test, so the guide requires an unenchanted weapon or fists.

Jorrvaskr and Skyforge contain several colocated books/items, but most are not routed in this entry pass. The Jorrvaskr Stone of Barenziah is in the Harbinger's room and the objective table already gates it behind `Glory of the Dead`. Jorrvaskr skill-book copies are non-selected duplicate copies or private-room copies, while the progression source-selection table chooses other copies for those titles. The Skyforge copy of `The Armorer's Challenge` is source-listed and free, but the progression table chooses Bilegulch Mine as the default copy, so the entry pass leaves the Skyforge copy closed.

`Proving Honor` requires one completed radiant first, uses Farkas as the temporary follower, and sends the player through Dustman's Cairn. UESP's bug notes support keeping Farkas with the player rather than telling him to meet at Dustman's Cairn, avoiding other followers during the ruin, saving before the lever-trap scene, and speaking to Vilkas before the initiation ceremony. The Dustman's Cairn page confirms the ruin is quest-locked before `Proving Honor`, does not respawn, contains `Battle of Sancre Tor` in the first room, uses Dustman's Cairn Key in the lower room, and has the Fire Breath word wall and Fragment of Wuuthrad in the final chamber.

The Fire Breath page confirms Yol is the Dustman's Cairn word. The guide records only Yol in this section; Toor and Shul remain with Sunderstone Gorge and `The Throat of the World`.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the fixed quest order, sparring restrictions, Jorrvaskr/Skyforge nearby-objective holds, Dustman's Cairn route, Fire Breath word, and Proving Honor bug mitigations.

The unavoidable uncertainty is the exact first Companions radiant target on a live save. The guide resolves this by requiring target recording and bug-recovery saves rather than pretending a deterministic target exists. Later guide sections must respect any random location already cleared by this first job when they reach that region.
