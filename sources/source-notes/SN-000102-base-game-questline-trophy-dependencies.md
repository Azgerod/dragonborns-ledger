# Source Note: Base-Game Questline Trophy Dependencies

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000102

## Claim

Base-game questline trophies are mostly unlocked by completing named main, faction, and Civil War milestones, but three questline areas need route protection: Thieves Guild restoration can be blocked if Erikur dies before `The Dainty Sload`, Dark Brotherhood trophies are unavailable on the destroy branch, and `War Hero` can be skipped by some `Season Unending` outcomes.

## Routing Relevance

The canonical route should complete the main quest, faction questlines, and Imperial Civil War path normally, while preserving the already planned hard saves around Civil War and Dark Brotherhood branch points. Erikur must remain alive until Thieves Guild restoration is secured.

## Evidence Summary

UESP's achievements page lists eight main-quest trophies, three Companions trophies, three College trophies, three Thieves Guild trophies, three Dark Brotherhood trophies, and three Civil War trophies. It identifies `One with the Shadows`, the three Dark Brotherhood trophies, and `War Hero` as missable or branch-sensitive.

For `One with the Shadows`, UESP's missable-achievement summary warns that Erikur dying before `The Dainty Sload` can block the trophy. For the Dark Brotherhood, UESP states that choosing `Destroy the Dark Brotherhood!` makes `With Friends Like These...`, `Bound Until Death`, and `Hail Sithis!` unavailable. For Civil War, `War Hero` depends on capturing Fort Sungard or Fort Greenwall and can be skipped depending on `Season Unending`.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for trophy requirements and branch risks. TB-016 should place exact Erikur protection, and TB-017 should add any no-console bug mitigations around Thieves Guild restoration and Civil War progression.
