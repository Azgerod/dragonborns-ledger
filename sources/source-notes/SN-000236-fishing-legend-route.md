# Fishing Legend Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000236

## Claim

`Fishing Legend` should be routed immediately after `Darkest Depths` inside the controlled Riften Fishing block. At that point the route has already completed Swims-In-Deep-Water's prior challenges, owns both specialty rods, and has already validated the same Riften, Dawnstar, and Broken Oar Grotto fishing spots.

The route should:

- Ask Swims-In-Deep-Water for the final challenge after `Darkest Depths`.
- Receive and read `List of Rare Fish`.
- Take and read `Fishing Mastery, v5` from the Riften Fishery back-room shelf after `Fishing Legend` starts.
- Catch Angelfish and Lyretail Anthias with the Alik'ri Fishing Rod at temperate lake and stream spots.
- Catch Angler and Scorpion Fish with the Argonian Fishing Rod at freezing-water and underground spots.
- Return the four fish to Swims-In-Deep-Water, complete `Fishing Legend`, and preserve Fang of Haynekhtnamet.

## Routing Relevance

This closes `OBJ-000597` Fishing Legend and the matching checklist row. It also closes direct support rows for:

## Evidence Summary

UESP's `Fishing Legend` page gives Swims-In-Deep-Water at Riften Fishery as the giver, lists `Darkest Depths` as the previous quest, and asks for Angelfish, Angler, Lyretail Anthias, and Scorpion Fish. The page and quest stages specify that Angelfish and Lyretail Anthias require the Alik'ri Fishing Rod in temperate lakes and streams respectively, while Angler and Scorpion Fish require the Argonian Fishing Rod in freezing water and underground fishing spots respectively. It also records Fang of Haynekhtnamet as the reward after turning in the four rare fish.

`List of Rare Fish` is given by Swims-In-Deep-Water when `Fishing Legend` starts and names the four rare fish plus both required specialty rods. `Fishing Mastery, v5` is on a Riften Fishery back-room shelf after `Fishing Legend` begins.

The Fishing activity page supports the route's selected spots: the Riften Fishery/Lake Honrich fishing supplies are a lake spot, the Riften canal supplies are a stream spot, Dawnstar has freezing-water spots, and Broken Oar Grotto has an underground spot. The same page documents the Alik'ri and Argonian rod restrictions for rare fish and the ordinary catch/population variance that the guide handles through rest/resupply rather than reroll instructions.

The direct Angelfish and Lyretail Anthias pages confirm they are ingredients and repeat their rare-fish catch restrictions. The Fishing Items page confirms Angler and Scorpion Fish as rare large fish with the corresponding Argonian rod sources, and Fang of Haynekhtnamet has its own UESP item page identifying it as a dagger artifact related to `Fishing Legend`.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the quest order, required catches, document handling, selected spots, rod requirements, reward, and route insertion point.

The remaining uncertainty is only ordinary catch variance. The guide handles it by using source-backed rods and biomes, storing spare ingredient copies where needed, and returning to the same fishing spots after rest/resupply if a spot depletes.

`Further Study`, `Rubbish Retrieval`, `Stocking Up`, Brook Bass, Juvenile Mudcrab, Emperor Crab Guardian Spirit, Fangtusk, and Snippy remain separate unresolved Fishing work.
