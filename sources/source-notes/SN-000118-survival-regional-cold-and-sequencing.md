## Evidence Summary

Cold is affected by location, time of day, weather, and water. The Cold page classifies environments as Warm, Cool, or Freezing; night increases ColdLevel, rain/snow/blizzards raise it further, and freezing water has an extreme ColdLevel and applies direct health damage until the player exits. It also notes that a player can freeze to death in blizzards or in freezing areas at night while raining or snowing.

The same page lists specific cold interiors in Skyrim and Solstheim, including Alftand Glacial Ruins, Bleakcoast Cave, Bonechill Passage, Cold Rock Pass, Duskglow Crevice, Forsaken Cave, Frostflow Lighthouse, Glacial Crevice, Greywater Grotto, Haemar's Shame, Hob's Fall Cave, Septimus Signus's Outpost, Sightless Pit, Southfringe Sanctum, Steepfall Burrow, Stillborn Cave, Yngvild, Altar of Thrond, Benkongerike, Bristleback Cave, Frossel, and Glacial Cave. Interiors can protect from weather, but source-listed cold interiors do not solve the cold problem by themselves.

The Survival Mode page specifically warns that night can make daytime-passable terrain hazardous, that Winterhold and Solstheim are challenging cold areas with limited transport, that caves/interiors may shield from weather without warming the player, that hot soups/torches/warm gear/heat sources matter, and that Clear Skies can slow freezing by clearing rain or snow. Transport confirms no carriages on Solstheim, while Inns identifies The Frozen Hearth in Winterhold, Pale inns at Nightgate and Dawnstar, and the Retching Netch as the only Solstheim inn.
