# SN-000161 - Dawnstar, Vaermina, And Cold-Weather Setup

Status: route-writing source note for TB-035-MR-031.

## Scope

This note supports the v1 guide section `Dawnstar, Vaermina, And Cold-Weather Setup`. The pass converts the old northern-preflight scaffold into an executable Dawnstar pass: Survival hot food, Camping Supplies, Rustleif, Silus's museum start, `Waking Nightmare`, Nightcaller Temple, the Skull of Corruption branch policy, and Skald's giant-bounty target. It also records the concrete reasons for the remaining Dawnstar/Pale holds.

## Route Decisions

The guide completes the Survival hot-food row here rather than continuing to treat hot food as a setup concept. The player is in Dawnstar, the route is about to open multiple cold northern legs, and Dawnstar Sanctuary has a source-listed cooking pot. The guide uses one saved Fire Salts for hot food and keeps `Stoking the Flames` unfinished until the route explicitly has ten spare Fire Salts for Balimund.

Camping Supplies are crafted here because the player is next to a Dawnstar inn, a wood chopping block, a woodcutter's axe, a tanning rack, and a forge, with a source-listed loose deer hide in the restored Dawnstar Sanctuary as a fallback leather source. The guide presents camping as emergency cold-travel support only, consistent with the Survival and Camping sources: camps help with warmth and limited cooking, but outdoor sleep does not replace an inn or owned bed for planned rest and level-up timing.

The retrospective nearby-objective audit rejects "Dawnstar/Pale bundle later" as a sufficient hold reason. Safe Dawnstar objectives are pulled into this pass even though the original scaffold only intended cold-weather setup. This keeps the route geographic without splitting tightly linked quest or dungeon chains.

Rustleif's `Rare Gifts` is routed now because the main route already collected a Volunruud `Night Falls on Sentinel` copy unread. The guide gives that copy to Rustleif and explicitly does not count the skill-book title as read; the skill-book checklist row remains open for a separate selected read under the skill-book policy.

`Salty Sea-Dogs` and Frida's Ring of Pure Mixtures are now left unstarted in this pass. The user-level random-assignment convention was tightened after the original Dawnstar draft: the guide should not start a random cave retrieval merely because one possible target overlaps a nearby route. These jobs remain staged until the guide can route the actual assigned cave as an isolated assignment and return to the main route without building the itinerary around one preferred result.

`Pieces of the Past` is started now because the player is level 20+ and already in Dawnstar at Silus's museum. The guide handles the museum objective and `The Keepers of the Razor` now, but the Razor pieces remain with their actual locations and the final Mehrunes' Razor hard-save policy remains later. This is quest-chain integrity, not a thematic Dawnstar hold.

`Waking Nightmare` is fully routed now because it starts at Windpeak Inn, moves directly to Nightcaller Temple near Dawnstar, has no higher project level gate, and needs an explicit Daedric artifact branch. The guide runs the spare-Erandur follower outcome on `HS-DAEDRIC-VAERMINA-SKULL`, reloads, then takes the artifact-safe main outcome by killing Erandur and preserving the Skull of Corruption. The Dreamstride save warning is route-critical because the UESP quest page records console/PC save instability around Dreamstride; the guide therefore saves outside Nightcaller Temple before and after the sequence when possible and avoids saving during it.

Skald's giant bounty is accepted after `Waking Nightmare` because the guide has now stabilized one Pale land prerequisite and can record the actual giant-camp target. The assigned camp is not forced immediately; completion waits until the target camp is routed so the guide can combine the kill, location handling, and any same-camp objectives without asking the player to chase a random camp in isolation.

Pale land permission, Heljarchen Hall, Thane of the Pale, and Gregor remain staged because their dependencies are not all complete in this section: Skald's giant bounty must be completed, the property purchase/foundation needs a funded Hearthfire material plan, and thaneship/housecarl state must be validated after the help/property sequence. Dawnstar merchant investments remain staged because the Investor perk is not yet routed.

The section does not rely on Dawnstar Sanctuary as long-term artifact storage. Prior guide text already warns against using the Sanctuary as a long-term storage base, and the source page distinguishes safe cupboards/end tables/wardrobes from respawning chests. The preflight uses the Sanctuary cooking pot but leaves storage policy unchanged.

## Coverage Notes

This pass directly places `Survival Mode Food and Warmth Consumable Set`, `Camping Supplies Crafting System`, Rustleif's `Rare Gifts`, `Visit the museum in Dawnstar`, `The Keepers of the Razor`, `Waking Nightmare`, Nightcaller Temple discovery, `The Dreamstride`, Vaermina Robes, Skull of Corruption, Erandur's branch follower outcome, and Skald's giant-bounty target recording.

Rows that remain staged have concrete non-theme reasons: Balimund's `Stoking the Flames` needs ten spare Fire Salts; Wayfinder and Frida require later isolated random-assignment handling; Skald completion is target-dependent; Pale property and thaneship require the giant bounty, funding, and property-state sequence; Merchant investments require the Investor perk; `Mannimarco, King of Worms` remains closed for the skill-book reading window; `Night Falls on Sentinel` remains unread even though one copy is consumed by Rustleif.

No `NEEDS ROUTE RESOLUTION` rows are introduced by this pass.
