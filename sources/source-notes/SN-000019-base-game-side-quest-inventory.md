# Source Note: Base-Game Side Quest Inventory

Status: needs review.

Source note ID: SN-000019

## Claim

UESP lists Skyrim side quests separately from questline, Daedric, and miscellaneous quests, and sorts them by usual starting location. Excluding official DLC entries and the already-entered Bards College quest, the base-game named side quest source list contributes 32 entries for this pass; `Delayed Burial` is retained as a duplicate cross-reference because the canonical objective row lives in the Dark Brotherhood slice.

## Routing Relevance

The guide specification requires all named non-radiant side quests on the main route. This note supports objective rows for the remaining base-game side quest inventory only; detailed rewards, NPC dependencies, quest conflicts, bugs, and exact route placement remain deferred.

## Evidence Summary

UESP describes side quests as standard-length quests that are not part of a questline and are not Daedric quests. Its side quest list includes base-game city, town, settlement, dungeon, and other side quests. This pass excludes Dragonborn and Dawnguard entries, which belong to later DLC objective passes, and does not duplicate `Tending the Flames`, which was entered with the Bards College slice.

The base-game rows entered from the UESP list are Markarth, Morthal, Riften, Solitude, Whiterun, Windhelm, Riverwood, other settlement, dungeon, and other side quests. `The Lost Expedition` appears in both the Markarth and dungeon sections on UESP and is entered once. `Delayed Burial` appears in the side quest list but is otherwise categorized as a Dark Brotherhood quest, so OBJ-000053 is the canonical objective row and OBJ-000207 is an excluded duplicate cross-reference.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the inventory and usual-starting-location grouping. Individual quest pages still need later review for prerequisites, missability, bugs, unique rewards, leveled rewards, cell-entry risks, and route-critical NPC dependencies.
