## Evidence Summary

UESP's Black Book compilation lists seven books in the series, and the individual book pages provide source-listed locations. The Forgotten Vale Books Quest page lists four Ancient Falmer Tomes and the translated copies Urag gro-Shub gives in exchange. The Oghma Infinium page identifies the book as a Daedric artifact reward from Discerning the Transmundane and notes that it is removed after use. The Elder Scroll page identifies the three in-game scrolls and their acquisition contexts. The Wind and Sand quest page identifies the book as a radiant retrieval target after the listed prerequisites.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the title inventory and acquisition candidates in this child slice. Open questions include final route timing, whether any Black Book or Elder Scroll sale/removal is desirable after all quest uses, how to present the Forgotten Vale original-versus-translation exchange, whether Oghma Infinium should be read immediately or delayed for leveling efficiency, and the exact radiant target selected for Wind and Sand.
