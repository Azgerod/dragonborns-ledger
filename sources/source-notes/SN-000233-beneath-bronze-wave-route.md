# Beneath Bronze Waters, Wave Breaker, and End of the Line Route

Status: researched.

Source note ID: SN-000233

## Claim

`Beneath Bronze Waters` should be routed only after `The Ultimate Feast` is complete and only after the route has deliberately returned to Bronze Water Cave for the Fishing quest. The guide should continue directly through `Wave Breaker` and `End of the Line` before the Imperial Civil War changes Dawnstar's Jarl state.

The guide should make a hard save before the Bronze Water exterior work, read/take `Bounty: Dwarven Investigation`, read/take `Fisherman's Journal (Fishing)` outside Bronze Water Cave, retrieve the Dwarven Fishing Rod Schematic from Alftand Ruined Tower, give it to Calcelmo, wait 24 hours for the Dwarven Fishing Rod, fish the two Aetherium Dynamo Cores outside Bronze Water Cave, place them in the actuators, defeat the Dwarven spider workers, loot the reward chest, and preserve the Dwarven Fishing Rod, Irkngthand War Axe of Fire, and Irkngthand War Axe of Freezing.

After `Beneath Bronze Waters`, the guide should handle `Wave Breaker` at the Dawnstar fishing shack while Dawnstar is still Stormcloak-held in the current route state. It should then read `Letter from Viriya`, bring Viriya 18 Firewood, 20 Nails, 4 Iron Fittings, and 1 Lock, wait 24 hours, and visit her Grand Plaza stall for Viriya's Charm.

## Routing Relevance

Earlier guide sections already use Bronze Water Cave as the `Blindsighted` exit and intentionally stage the Fishing exterior. UESP records a premature Dwemer spider ambush bug outside Bronze Water Cave that can make `Beneath Bronze Waters` impossible to start. The route should therefore correct the earlier Pale sweep so it discovers Bronze Water Cave but does not clear or disturb the exterior until this source-checked Fishing block.

`Wave Breaker` has a Dawnstar government bug if Dawnstar is under Imperial control. The current guide later commits to the Imperial Civil War. Routing `Wave Breaker` before that commitment avoids the exiled-Skald/report-target conflict without needing a recovery branch.

## Evidence Summary

UESP lists `The Ultimate Feast` as the previous quest for `Beneath Bronze Waters` and `Wave Breaker` as the next quest. The `Beneath Bronze Waters` walkthrough starts from Viriya's work dialogue, gives the player `Bounty: Dwarven Investigation`, sends the player to read `Fisherman's Journal (Fishing)` at Bronze Water Cave, then sends the player to Alftand Ruined Tower for the Dwarven Fishing Rod Schematic. Calcelmo builds the Dwarven Fishing Rod after 24 hours. The Dwarven rod is then used at two Bronze Water Cave fishing spots to catch Aetherium Dynamo Cores; placing the cores activates the mechanism and reward chest.

UESP's Bronze Water Cave and Beneath Bronze Waters pages both record the exterior spider ambush bug. Because `Blindsighted` exits through Bronze Water Cave before the Fishing route is ready, the guide must explicitly leave the exterior journal, supplies, mechanism, and spider trigger untouched at the Nightingale exit and in any earlier Pale sweep.

UESP's Fishing Items page confirms the Aetherium Dynamo Cores are caught from the two Bronze Water Cave fishing spots during `Beneath Bronze Waters`, must be placed into the nearby actuators, and can be recovered afterward. The same page confirms the Dwarven Fishing Rod Schematic is in Alftand Ruined Tower and is needed for the quest. It also lists the Dwarven Fishing Rod and Irkngthand weapon members relevant to preservation.

UESP's Wave Breaker page says the quest follows `Beneath Bronze Waters`, uses Viriya's courier letter, stages the Dawnstar shack attack, and requires using flaming pots in catapults against the Emperor Crab Guardian Spirit until the objective completes. The page also records a bug if Dawnstar is under Imperial control. Since this guide's canonical Civil War route later installs Imperial control in Dawnstar, Wave Breaker should be resolved before that route section.

UESP's End of the Line page says the follow-up courier letter arrives after `Wave Breaker`, and Viriya requires 18 Firewood, 20 Nails, 4 Iron Fittings, and 1 Lock. After the supplies are delivered, the player waits 24 hours and speaks to Viriya at her new stall for Viriya's Charm.

## Confidence and Open Questions

Confidence is high for the prerequisite chain, Bronze Water exterior timing, Alftand schematic route, Calcelmo wait, Dwarven rod/core mechanism, reward preservation, Wave Breaker pre-Civil-War timing, and End of the Line supply quantities.

The remaining operational risk is the Bronze Water exterior bug itself. The guide mitigates it with two layers: earlier sections do not disturb the exterior, and the actual `Beneath Bronze Waters` block creates `HS-FISHING-BRONZE-WATER` before returning to the exterior mechanism.
