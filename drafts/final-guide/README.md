# drafts/final-guide

Final black-box guide drafts belong here.

Do not start this layer until the objective database, constraint tables, route skeleton, checklist mapping, and warning layer are mature enough to support polished itinerary prose.

`main-guide-v0.md` is a scaffold-level draft. It is not detailed enough to satisfy the self-contained final guide standard.

The active expansion target is `main-guide-v1.md`, planned in `docs/main-guide-v1-expansion-plan.md`. The v1 guide must explicitly represent every objective row and every mapped checklist row inside the guide document itself, with objective-level route instructions, branch handling, option/default handling, exclusions, and any unavoidable `NEEDS ROUTE RESOLUTION` notes.

Keep `main-guide-v1.md` player-facing. Put objective IDs, coverage ledgers, row counts, staged/completed status, and cue-to-objective mappings in `data/guide-coverage/main-guide-v1-coverage.csv`, not in the normal guide body.

`appendices-v0.md` is the TB-036 reference/verification appendix draft generated by `tools/build_appendices_v0.py`. It supports review of `main-guide-v1.md`; it must not become the only place where execution-critical route instructions appear.

`qa-checklist.md` is the Phase 15 QA checklist. Its TB-037 coverage section is generated by `tools/build_coverage_qa_checklist.py`; its TB-038 order/delayed-task section is supported by `tools/audit_main_guide_order_delayed_tasks.py` and the TB-038R repair register from `tools/repair_main_guide_order_delayed_tasks.py`; its TB-039 trophy, leveled-item, and cell-entry section is supported by `tools/audit_main_guide_trophy_leveled_cell.py`; its TB-040 Survival Mode and Legendary difficulty section is supported by `tools/audit_main_guide_survival_legendary.py`; its TB-041 branch and spoiler section is supported by `tools/audit_main_guide_branch_spoilers.py`; its TB-042 simulated playtest section is supported by `tools/audit_main_guide_playtest.py` and `playtest-notes.md`; its TB-043 unresolved-risk section is supported by `tools/build_unresolved_risk_report.py` and `unresolved-risk-report.md`.

`playtest-notes.md` is the TB-042 simulated playtest notes artifact generated by `tools/audit_main_guide_playtest.py`. It records the document-level playtest scope, result counts, known unresolved route-resolution carryforward, and live-playtest boundaries.

`unresolved-risk-report.md` is the TB-043 final-risk summary generated by `tools/build_unresolved_risk_report.py`. It summarizes the explicit unresolved objective rows by severity, likely owner, affected route surface, and follow-up priority; the row-level register stays in `data/guide-coverage/main-guide-v1-unresolved-risk-register.csv`.
